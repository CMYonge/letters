My dear [[person:2108]Miss Abraham] Another despairing search for a [discard?], not your papers, but the letter that went with one, and I think the more that the post must have lost the M.S. I see it was at a time when I was turned out of my usual sitting room by a new carpet, and I think the pamphlet may have gone among the accumulation that grows upon one

It is very unlucky

Yours truly C M Yonge
