Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] I have to thank you for sending me the parcel of German books, which I hope will much add to the correctness of my [[cmybook:62]story]. I am not sure however that one has not come for me to which I have no right -[[otherbook:383]Alt-Nordisches Leben] Von Dr Karl Weinhold -

[[person:793]Miss Keary] told me that you had promised to get for her some Old Northern books, so perhaps it was meant for her. In that case shall I send it? Did you also put in Duncan’s [[otherbook:384]Shipwrecks] which do indeed look like a store of Golden Deeds -?

Any way I am very glad of the freight, and much obliged to you for putting me in the way of getting these books.

Will you be kind enough to have a copy of [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds] sent to

Mlle [[person:1077]Marianne von Nathusius], Meyendorf Leckhausen Magdeburg.

She wants to get the start in translating it, before [[person:1396]Tauchnitz]’s English edition comes out. [[person:423]Mme de Witt ]is translating it into French

Yours sincerely C M Yonge

Please to let sheets of the [[cmybook:58]Clever Woman] go to M. Tauchnitz, unless this has been already done. I have heard from him today about it
