Dear [[person:1642]Miss Cox] I cannot promise to vote for [[person:1679]Anne Fay] but she interests me much, as I have seen a good deal of the same kind of illness and I will see whether it is possible.

I well remember the days of the Cardioscope, which I think was one of the best ideas [[other:52]we] ever had.[[footnote:1]] I have now a [[person:329]fellow Editor] who has taken the MSS especially of poetry. I think she must have your verses

yours truly C M Yonge
