My dear [[person:329]Christabel] Photography must certainly turn you into a most pensive mood. You are a great contrast to the solid jolly damsel in the old [[other:104]Gosling] book.[[footnote:1]] Your Ridge is very good. I will send it round with the answers, but as yet I have had no answers but [[person:1603]Cricket] and [[person:17]Lady bird], and the [[person:1520]Turks cap] has been so ill used that she has had neither questions answers nor [[other:89]Barnacle], this time – so I shall give a horrible hiss when I send my Cackle. [[person:1521]Genl Wilford] gives up his house at Woolwich this next month, so it will be best to direct everything to her to the care of [[person:384]Mrs Cristall], St Cross, Winchester. I think after Christmas we shall fill up [[person:675]Cobweb]’s place with a [[person:1008]Lilian Mitchell] at Kings Teignton who has rather a romantic history.[[footnote:2]] I saw [[person:973]Pena] twice, but she was away most of the time I was in her parts. I have not heard from [[person:327]Alice] since the great affair at Hurstpierpoint-[[footnote:3]] I have written letters and tidied all day and my wits are exhausted

your affectionate C M Yonge
