My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] is in want of a volume of Milmans[[otherbook:411] Latin Christianity] which the [[other:29]London Library] does not send. She will write to you which it is and perhaps you could kindly cause it to be sent to her. I cant think what [[other:173]Clay] meant by our delaying the [[cmybook:71]proofs] for we had never done so.

I have never had any of [[cmybook:19]Heartsease] to put the headings to the pages.[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
