Dear [[person:2094]Mr Wooldridge]

[[person:1604]Mrs Yonge] comes to Otterbourne Vicarage this afternoon, and will be in the village till some day next week. I think you had better send the papers to me, as I am not certain whether she will be at the Vicarage or the Grange, and I will get her signature[[footnote:1]]

Please pay the 4/ and add to your account

Yours truly C M Yonge
