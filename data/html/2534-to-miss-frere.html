My dear [[person:525]Miss Frere]

You have chosen a very cold summer for your visit to England and I am afraid you must think you have come there in the winter by mistake. We have had some revolutions in the state of the [[other:52]Monthly Packet], a new [[person:373]partner] at [[other:108]Mozley]’s[[footnote:1]] and a new printer, and my effort for some time past has been to get old material finished up. I have still so many verses on hand that I think I had better return yours.

For the story I think there will probably be room for it about July, if you feel able to go on with it. Eleven months hence sounds a long allowance, but one has to live beforehand with magazines[[footnote:1]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
