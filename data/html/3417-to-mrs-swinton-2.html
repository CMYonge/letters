My dear [[person:1388]Mrs Swinton] Thank you once more for the Waterloo letters which I have read with the greatest pleasure.[[footnote:2]] They bring it close to us, just as did [[person:313]Lord Seaton] and [[person:1631]my father] talking it over. I rather wonder to see the Prince of Orange such a favourite but probably he was very different to young girls from what he was to Lord Seaton who was his ‘bear leader’ for some time

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
