My dear [[person:443]Mrs Drew]

No book ever was quite equal to the [[otherbook:60]Conversations with Cousin Rachel], which I believe Masters still publishes. It may here and there seem rather antiquated, but the solid part is of all times. Then there is a little book called [[otherbook:1049]‘Girls’] - published by Skeffington, and one by Lady Barker, published by Hatchard in connection with [[other:23]G F S]fr. I cannot recollect its name, but Hatchard’s list gives it.[[footnote:1]]

As to fiction, girls in their teens seem ready for anything, and the less it is about servants the better they like it - I hardly know what specially to recommend. Esmé Stewart’s [[otherbook:1051]For Half a Crown]- National Society is a great favourite and [[otherbook:1052]‘A Promise Kept’] of the same set is very good. The [[other:32]NS] has published my [[cmybook:196]‘What to lend - &c’ ]- and you will find a list there

Have you seen the [[other:93]Mother’s Union Journal]? It is to be quarterly, and costs a penny.[[footnote:2]] I wish the size could be larger for the money, but it must make its way first. Its first start is very good, I think.

It is to be had of [[person:2377]Mr Warren] - Printer High Street Winchester

Thank you for your kind promise of the address

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
