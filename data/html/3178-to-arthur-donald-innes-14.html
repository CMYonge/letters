Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes] The [[cmybook:18]Castle Builders] and [[cmybook:67]Six Cushions] are out of date, and need not be reprinted- unless as is just possible, [[other:111]Macmillan] might like to purchase them for the sake of making up the set of my books. [[footnote:1]]

[[cmybook:104]Questions on the Epistles] has never been of much use, so few Sunday Schools go on far enough to want them Nor has [[cmybook:166]Sewing and Sowing] been popular. I am sorry for [[cmybook:152]Langley Little Ones] for I meant it to fill a want, but there is rather a turn against stories of village children just now, so we will leave it for the present and perhaps bind it up by and by with others

Yours truly C M Yonge
