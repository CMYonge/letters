Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

Thanks for the last set of my books, and for the Kingsley’s whereof we have been going through a course.[[footnote:1]]

If I might have the payment for the first edition of [[cmybook:201]Beechcroft at Rockstone] I should be glad.

I suppose the [[cmybook:213]last volume of Cameos] is waiting for its index, but I hope it will be out before the season is over.[[footnote:2]]

[[cmybook:205]The Reputed Changeling ]is a much better thing I hope than the Rockstone story, which is really only a continuation for the sake of old friends

I suppose it will soon be ready. Please let these copies be sent, and the sheets to [[person:2124]Tauchnitz].

I hear that [[cmybook:62]the Dove in the Eagle’s Nest] is much used as a class book in Switzerland

Yours truly C M Yonge
