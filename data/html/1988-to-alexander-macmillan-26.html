Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I am afraid the [[cmybook:58]Clever Woman] cannot come out till March, as [[other:159]Hogg] gives me no hope of finishing her before that time—so it would be hardly worth while to begin printing her till the beginning of the year.

I have sent all the rest of the [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds] direct to the printers, they will end with [[person:1915]Dr. Kane.]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
