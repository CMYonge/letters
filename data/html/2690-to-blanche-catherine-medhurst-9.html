Dear [[person:988]Miss Medhurst],

Thank you for your kindness to [[person:1358]Emmeline].[[footnote:1]] I believe she is coming down here for a month to recruit if [[person:2287]her brother] can spare her. I hope it is only want of love, but [[person:1359]her aunt] is anxious because loss of voice was the beginning of illness with [[person:1361]another aunt] - a school mistress - who went into a decline and the thought of whom prevented us from pressing Emmeline to be a school mistress.

I am sorry to hear of the break up of your home in Dorset square. I am afraid you will miss all your old habits very much[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
