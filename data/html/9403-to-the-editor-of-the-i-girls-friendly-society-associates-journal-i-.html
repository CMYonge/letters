'OUR MAGAZINES Dear Madam,- To please every one is avowedly impossible, V.C.’s complaint of the stories of foreign life in [[other:71]Friendly Leaves] only convinces me that it would be well to amalgamate our two magazines, so as to afford scope for pleasing both those who can only take an interest in homely English life and those who have imagination enough to look beyond.[[footnote:1]] I find my own village girls weary of a tale of cottage girls, and prefer something beyond their own experience. [[otherbook:431]The Children of the New Forest], [[otherbook:432]The Little Stepdaughter] (set in France), [[otherbook:433]The Silver Skates] (a Dutch tale), have been favourites of late when read aloud to them. It will not do to confine ourselves to one class of tales for a wide a field of readers. I remain, &c., C. M. Yonge.
