Dear [[person:743]Sir][[footnote:1]]

I am very sorry if I did not answer your letter, and acknowledge the little book, I fully thought that I had done so.

I have gone quite half way in the [[cmybook:199]Life of Hannah More]. If the requisite number of pages be not attained, I can easily shorten or lengthen the account of her works, but I think the length will be right.

I have the [[otherbook:1019]Mendip annals] - i.e. Patty More’s Journal, which I find very useful

Yours truly C M Yonge

I think I shall be able to send you the whole MS in less than a month
