'My dear [[person:1520]Florence] Publishing on commission does mean taking the risk and paying a percentage, above the expenses to the publisher.  It is what I often do, always with [[other:108]Mozley]  The round sum for each edition is pleasant, and I don’t think you would get so much in the end by this as by selling copy right.  But if Mr Cowie[[footnote:1]] has answered you and is considering is there much use in my writing to him.  I would do it if it were to ensure his attention, but I think he will give that, and if one writes without necessity, it is rather wasting one’s recommending power and making it less effective when it is really needed  I wish [[other:235]Ward] would take the story, but he declined Chardham Fair,[[footnote:2]] which I thought would have made beautiful illustrations and he has taken such stupid stories instead that I cannot judge of him your affectionate C M Yonge
