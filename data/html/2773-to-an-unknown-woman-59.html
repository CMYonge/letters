Dear Madam

A friend of my family, who- being a neighbour, I know better personally than by her handwriting is named [[person:2352]Anna Georgina Macaulay], and always goes by the appellation Nina. She is apt to stay a good deal away from home and I concluded that the letter I received was from her. For I suppose I am right in believing that you wrote to ask me to review a book on Scripture evidences and that my answer will apply that I would try, if I could and if I had the book to mention it in a Conversation on Books.[[footnote:1]]

With many apologies for the error caused by the resemblance of name

Yours truly C M Yonge
