'Dear [[person:1143]Sir] I am much obliged by your sending me the account of ‘[[cmybook:20]The Little Duke]’ and the accompanying [[other:160]£100]. I should be glad if, in sufficient time beforehand, you would be kind enough to tell me your opinion with regard to a second edition, and whether it would be advisable to renew it in the present form, or to have one much smaller and cheaper. I have also to thank you for ‘[[otherbook:179]The Plurality of Worlds]’, I had seen the first edition of that remarkable book but I am very glad to possess this with the additional matter at the commencement. I suppose 400 pages will not make the volumes too large. I do not know whether the printer has taken into account that most of the latter part of the M S is written less closely than the earlier portions Yours faithfully C M Yonge
