My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith] I remembered with consternation this morning that I had never answered about your poor girl. I hope if we had had a vote to give that I should have behaved better, but the fact is that there is a child from the parish where my [[person:106]grandfather] was Rector whom we must vote for till he is disposed of. I always feel very sore about that [[other:38]Asylum], having subscribed for an idiot of our own parish and then found him ineligible because unluckily he had had parish relief--[[footnote:1]]

We have just set out on a two months expedition from home -- and are enjoying this most charming place with all our hearts--

A direction to Otterbourn will always find me--

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
