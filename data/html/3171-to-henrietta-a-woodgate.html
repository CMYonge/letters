Dear [[person:1554]Madam] I am afraid I have not knowledge enough to help you with questions, as books of the kind do not come much in my way. From what I have heard, [[person:2103]Professor Cheyne]’s books would be to be avoided.[[footnote:2]] I believe there is one which reduces Elijah’s miracles to myths, but I only heard it discussed, and there are also his interpretations of the Psalms.

I saw the first number of the [[other:83]Newbery House Magazine]. There the paper on the Psalms, which called the Second not Messianic, was enough for me, so I do not know how they went on, but I rather think the papers were discontinued It is a very difficult thing to choose safe readings for the Higher Education subjects

yours truly C M Yonge
