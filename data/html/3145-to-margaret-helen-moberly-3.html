My dear [[person:83]Maggie] I hope you will have a happy Christmas I daresay you have been singing already at [[person:1019]Mamma’]s doors with all the rest and I think you can begin to keep it as a very happy day.

I send you a green book with some very pretty pictures of [[cmybook:21]Tom Thumb]. I think you will like to see him driving his six little white mice and by and by you will be able to read his story.

I wonder when you will write a letter to me.

your affectionate godmother Charlotte M. Yonge
