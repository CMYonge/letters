My dear Miss Yonge on money [?] [[person:939]I] enclose a [[other:160]cheque] as usual. I am off on a brief holiday to Scotland, my American trip being abandoned for this year. I hope to be home for good & may it be so - by the end of September and quite looking forward to seeing you when you come to London in October. In case you have any point of importance to bring before the press, a note to[[person:379] G.L. Craik] Eqr. my partner addressed here will get attention at once

I am inclined to think we should make the [[cmybook:62]Dove] fly well to Aug. It really is very charming.

very truly yours A. Macmillan
