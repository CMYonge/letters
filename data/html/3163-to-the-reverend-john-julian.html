Dear [[person:788]Sir] I suppose you must have a privately marked copy of the [[otherbook:186]Child’s Christian Year], since it never was published with authors’ names. My mother’s maiden name was [[person:1606]Frances Mary Bargus], born Jan 13th 1795 - married to [[person:1631]William Crawley Yonge] of the 52d regiment died Sept 28th 1868[[footnote:1]]

I think all I care to have published about myself is already in biographical notices

I remain &c C M Yonge
