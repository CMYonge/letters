Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] If you think this [[cmybook:51]preface] will do, please send it to the printer.

I thought some acknowledgment of the sources was due -- and some hint too of what is less authenticated.

I have wound up with a suggestion about penny readings,[[footnote:1]] as I think the book might be useful for it, but if this is not advisable it may come out.

From Monday to Saturday of next week we shall be staying with the [[person:1304]Warden] of New College at Oxford, then at home again

Yours very truly C. M. Yonge
