My dear [[person:741]Miss Ingelow] This is [[person:1371]Mr Stone]’s answer about the [[otherbook:453]knight of Intercession]. It seems to be one of the legends that evades all search. How would it be to send a question to Notes and Queries?

I have been wishing to tell you that the last sermon that I heard [[person:798]Mr Keble] preach, a year and half ago, - one on the anniversary of his Church Consecration, he brought in a sentence about the Church bells sounding on the ear of travellers in the desert, and he told me afterwards that it came out of ‘[[otherbook:278]the one story by two Authors’].[[footnote:1]]

yours sincerely C M Yonge
