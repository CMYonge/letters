My dear [[person:772]Mrs Johns],

We are much obliged for your kindness, and if things - weather included turn out well - I hope to bring the [[person:1603]Cricket] and her sister in time for a four o’clock tea with you on Monday.[[footnote:1]] I am afraid we cannot do more, as after that it gets so late and cold for an open carriage. Our basket only holds three so that my [[person:1606]mother ]- though thanking you for your kind message will remain at home

Yours sincerely C M Yonge

I have sent this to shew my good intentions but just as I had written it, it turned out that my cousins must go on Monday to the Isle of Wight, and they do not come back till Wednesday. Then Thursday is [[person:1744]Miss Chamberlayne]’s wedding, and they may have to leave us on Friday, but if possible, I will try to bring them that day.
