'[[[person:1019]To Mary Ann Moberly]]

I tremble to say that I am going to write [[person:1155]his] life, and I am probably going to Lichfield to talk it over with the Selwyns and Abrahams.[[footnote:1]] It is very awful, for it is embalming the Saint for the Church. I hope the [[person:1295]Bishop] will let me have copies of the letters to him.
