My dear [[person:489]Miss Erskine], Your letter only came to me this morning as you see I am on my travels - so though it is before Tuesday, I think I had better answer to May. I shall be at 45 Westbourne Terrace for a few days after Wednesday, and shall I hope have a good sight of [[person:351]Mary Coleridge] whom I have quite missed in Devonshire[[footnote:1]]

I have told Ivanova ([[person:126]Miss Batty]) of the Institution, and if she will undertake to write an account of it, I will ask you to be kind enough to send the report either to her or to me - but I will let you know what she says -[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
