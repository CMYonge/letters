My dear [[person:524]Miss Freeman]

The first week in May will suit me very well and I hope we shall be at our best between banksia roses and nightingales. I am afraid however that I really have only room for one and that I must not have the pleasure of having your sister though I am very sorry to be so inhospitable, but when you see our household you will understand. I have a friend coming to me on Wednesday or Saturday, I am not sure which, but she means to stay a week, so on Saturday week I shall be quite ready, or any time after that suits you. [[person:1462]Miss Walter] (it has no S) is mending with the Spring, but has not been out of her room since November, I hope she will soon.

yours very sincerely C M Yonge
