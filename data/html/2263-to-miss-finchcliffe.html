Dear [[person:511]Miss Finchcliffe], I am very glad to have seen [[person:382]Edith Crawley’]s letter which I return with many thanks.[[footnote:1]] I had proposed the Irishwoman to [[person:579]Mrs Gray] for the Cape ladies, and she had rejected her, but perhaps they know their own plans best. I am glad that another and elder lady has been added to them for someone to take the command was much wanted.

I am glad that dear [[person:930]Miss Mackenzie] is so much better, but it has been a long illness. These last two or three days have been very sad with us, [[person:1606]my mother] has become so much more feeble and seems to be losing her appetite, but it is most thank worthy that she is quite free from all suffering.

Yours sincerely
