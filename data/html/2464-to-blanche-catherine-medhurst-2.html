Dear [[person:988]Miss Medhurst] I like your New Year’s Eve poem, but I ought to have had it long ago.

If you have not disposed of it otherwise, will you send it to me next October for December of 1873.

I believe [[person:990]Mrs Mercier] has put a little pupil of mine under your care as member of the Guild [[person:1358]Emmeline Spratt]. She is a really good girl I believe, and very affectionate but I should not wonder if you thought her a little sentimental

Yours truly C M Yonge
