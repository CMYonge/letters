My dear [[person:469]Miss Gatty],

The Signora [[person:1444]Linda Villari] wife to [[person:2345]Professor Villari] at Florence sent me this pretty little sketch of a real favourite bird. It is too youthful for the [[other:52]Packet], but I think you will like to open a connection with her, as she writes prettily and freshly. I enclose a card she sent with her address. She wrote a very pretty story once for the Packet called [[otherbook:917]the Angel of Viareggio].

I hope you can give a tolerable account of [[person:494]Mrs Ewing]. We were greatly charmed with Laetus Sorte Meâ[[footnote:3]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge

On second thought I will keep the card as if rejected you had better return Dinderino to me
