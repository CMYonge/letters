Dear Madam

I am very sorry not to have answered your letter sooner, I put it aside to enquire about [[person:2392]Mrs White], and in the hurry of Christmas preparations it was forgotten. She is not strictly an inhabitant of this parish, but of Compton- where I believe [[person:2393]her husband] is man to [[person:98]Mr Baldwin ]the maltster.[[footnote:1]] I had heard that she took in washing, and I believe that she is respectable but I know nothing of her capabilities. [[person:99]Mrs Baldwin] might be able to tell you

I remain &c C M Yonge
