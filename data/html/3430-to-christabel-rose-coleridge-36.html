My dear [[person:329]C C] Thank you for Crispin I was glad of him for we are feeding [[person:182]Alethea ]with light literature, she having broken down, with nothing the matter but a course of overwork after the influenza- first the children’s measles, then going to Holmwood to lodgings with the children, no nursery maid, and the lady nurse not looking after her, or doing nursery maid’s work and then a good deal to do at homecoming, mission and guests. So she is to take a great rest, and to go to Dorking alone when [[person:1604]Frances] goes home. She left Torquay yesterday for the [[person:616]Hallidays] at Taunton. I do not think you should get pledged to [[other:64]Chwoman], but oh! if you could get hold of G L, or something of the sort. It was such a useless story in G L and very sad besides.

Nest] so [[person:111]Macmillan] concluded it was not worth his while 30 000 must be ordered to make a /6 ed answer! I am afraid the local people only say ‘Out of print[‘] and don’t send the orders.
