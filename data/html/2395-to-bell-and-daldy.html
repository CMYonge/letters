[[other:135]Gentlemen] Are you disposed to undertake the publication of four historical tales by [[person:423]Madame Guizot de Witt], translated from MSS which have never yet appeared in France or England?[[footnote:1]]

The subjects are the Countess of Montfort’s defence of Brittany, the childhood of Blaise Pascal and his sister, and some scenes from Madame de Sevigné’s life, and to these I could add Francis Xavier in Japan, translated by myself, but which has appeared in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] My name may be used as editor and I am very anxious for the publication as Mme de Witt wants means of assisting the sufferers in France

yours faithfully C M Yonge
