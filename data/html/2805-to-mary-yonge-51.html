My dear [[person:1626]Mary]

[[person:1462]Gertrude] has brightened up this week but she had an embarras de richesses on Wednesday for[[person:2360] Jane Harper], two children, [[person:1690]Bella Heathcote] [[person:236]Mrs Buston] and [[person:404]Emily Dampier] all came. She could not see the last two but they are coming on Wednesday afternoon to see her.

We had 104 communicants today - 50 early and 54 late. [[person:1604]Frances] was neuralgic and could not come out so [[person:1630]Maurice] was alone. [[person:1610]George] has grown very tall. Poor [[person:967]Caroline Martin] died the night before good Friday.

I never had such a scarcity of primroses at Easter!

Your affectionate C.M. Yonge
