Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

Many, many thanks for prompt [[other:160]payment], an agreeable sight on a winter’s morning I think [[cmybook:131]the story] in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet ]will finish there about next spring It is not longer than the [[cmybook:120]Three Brides], but has been in short chapters. I have not finished writing it yet, but the beginning may be printed when it is convenient. I am correcting the [[cmybook:126]Moors and Christians] gradually, and will send the result soon I see one or two little corrections are wanting in the [[cmybook:353]Primer]. When shall I send them

Yours truly C M Yonge
