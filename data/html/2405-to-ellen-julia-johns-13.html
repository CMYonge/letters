My dear [[person:772]Mrs Johns] Thank you for your kind note. The second part of the little book is already half way printed - so I hope it will be ready the time the first is finished.[[footnote:1]]

I believe I am going to London on Monday week, and shall be away for about a month, unless indeed the scarlatina in the parish spreads so as to prevent my going; and then I am afraid it would be equally in the way of meeting you.

So I am afraid I cannot leave the coming to afternoon tea - which I should much like to do - till after I come home. I hope that by the time you will be stronger

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
