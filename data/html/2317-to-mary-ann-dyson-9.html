'My dear [[person:464]Marianne]- We have had a very successful time, so successful that I have had no time for letter-writing or anything else, but I have been most enjoying myself. I did just shake hands with [[person:1215]Dr. Pusey], in his red doctor’s gown, and moreover heard him speak about the Palestine---no, the Sinai exploration.[[footnote:1]] 9th.---There, I wrote on Monday, and not a bit of time have I had to write since, but I am enjoying all things. I think the eager life here just suits me, from the wonderful unflagging feeling about it. It is so much the sparkling, hurrying stream.[[footnote:2]] I know it is a delusion to hope to make this letter longer, so I shall not try--- Your most affectionate C. M. YONGE
