Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes], I have disposed of [[person:740]Mr Hutchinson’]s Stars except for this one [[otherbook:637]chapter]. I found when I wrote to him that he had not meant to contribute a regular course but miscellaneous papers now and then. I want however to get the [[otherbook:632]Engineering recollections in America] into this volume. They are 7 but short, very entertaining and one may be doubled. The story is short too. I have not got [[person:1606]Miss Sewell] yet, but I hope to get all in, including [[person:608]Mrs Hallett].[[footnote:1]] The chapter of [[otherbook:595]Christian’s Name] is very short. I will see about [[person:2096]Miss Watson] and her [[otherbook:635]German] in time. I was going to write to you about Mr Fowler’s [[otherbook:636]magpie] but it appeared in your parcel yesterday also this note for you from [[person:2097]Mr Church]- and a letter which I am sending him to ask if there is any biography of [[person:2098]General Church] - it is odd that he and the [[person:297]Dean of St Pauls] were discouraged from publishing one, because it was not thought likely to take.[[footnote:1]] For General Church was a remarkable man who took a great part in the War of Greek Independence.

I am guarding against being overwhelmed this quarter, but here are 3 or 4 papers about a trip to Italy by Mme [[person:1444]Linda Villari] - just come, with a postcard to answer her upon. I had [[otherbook:638]some from her before], which were liked and she is a good name but continuous papers on Travels are to be avoided I think. I shall defer the answer as to acceptance till [[person:329]Miss Coleridge ]comes

Yours truly C M Yonge
