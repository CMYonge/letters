Dear [[person:470]Mrs Egerton] Unluckily the matter of the Grants could not come on till the last and I never found you again. I am sorry to say that the Council refused to forward your application as it would be a dangerous precedent to other Branches who might ask for the same grant. I have written to [[person:1682]Mrs Chester] and she may send it to you. I am sorry I had to run away from you, but there was need of my seeing [[person:2133]Miss Chambers] and I hoped you would have spoken when the subject came on.

Yours truly C M Yonge
