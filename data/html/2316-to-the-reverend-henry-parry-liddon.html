Dear [[person:877]Mr Liddon] Thank you for your very kind answer. A letter will be an excellent way of conveying your recollections.[[footnote:2]] I think considering what Hursley Vicarage was, it would have been perfect treason to have made notes of the daily life and conversation - What seems to me most wanted is something to give an idea of [[person:798]Mr. Keble]’s greatness and his championship - and this [[person:344]Sir J Coleridge] writing from an equality and without perfect sympathy, has in some degree missed.[[footnote:3]]

A fortnight or a month’s time will be quite soon enough for me. I shall be at home I hope on the 12th

With many thanks Yours very truly C M Yonge
