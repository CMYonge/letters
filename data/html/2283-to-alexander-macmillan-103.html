Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] The enclosed rather puzzles me, for I thought it was settled that sheets of the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet of Pearls] were to be forwarded to [[person:522]Mr Franke] as they were ready for the Magazine?

Will you kindly see if this has been done?

Please send me back [[other:202]Wooed and Won]. I must try what I can do with it.

Are you reprinting Kingsley’s [[otherbook:358]Heroes]. We tried for both them and Miss Keary’s [[otherbook:534]heroes of Asgard] for my brother’s children, and found both are out of print.

The [[other:76]Literary Churchman] is very favorable [sic] to the [[cmybook:71]Selections], as I hear they have begun on them at a great girls’ school

Yours very truly C.M. Yonge
