'Dear [[person:1864]Mr Chandler] I think your friends MS is Vallombrosa but there is no address on it, and another about a Family Adventure in Normandy equally unaddressed has come to me, so that I am not sure which is which[[footnote:1]] Both are good but I like Vallombrosa best and gladly accept it. I am just starting for a few days at Salisbury yours truly C M Yonge
