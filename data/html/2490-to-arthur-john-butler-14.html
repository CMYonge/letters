My dear [[person:238]Arthur] I should have written yesterday only that the [[person:1015]Bp of Salisbury] came to see a sick old farming man, and was a sight for sair een, who took up all my morning. I shall look out for the [[otherbook:843]Maypole] - my Sub is going to bring up the weeded [[other:116]Spiders] this afternoon- 17 Chrysostoms! I think your Spider will have five competitors with the Aryans. I fully expect someone will take them for the heretics - as by the bye someone did by your friends the Dolomites.[[footnote:1]]

Well, I congratulate you with all my heart and hope some day I may be worthy to hear the rest of her name[[footnote:2]]

Wat Tyler had not always been a tyler but had been abroad in the service of a merchant- (Query was he the original mad hatter?) [[footnote:3]] So he must have learnt to ride on that expedition any way, and it was hard if he could not pick up a horse on his expeditions to London. I think I shall set that rebellion as a question to the Spiders only I can’t do it yet as one spider is gone to Bermuda, and another to Cannes, and they had their questions before hand, I must stick to what I have given them. There are about 80 in all, but happily they never answer all at once.

yours very sincerely

[the letter is unsigned, though the paper is whole]
