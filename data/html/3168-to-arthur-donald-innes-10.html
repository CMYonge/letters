Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes], I do not suppose the understanding was ever absolutely clear, I have certainly always paid over £20 to [[person:512]Miss Finlaison] half in summer and half in winter, and I have, as you say had [[other:160]£40] in summer, and £50 in winter, but I an impression that I had £10 more for the Christmas number. Indeed I am quite sure that [[person:1344]Mr Smith] gave me £10 more for the subeditor, and that I made it £20, £10 being his, and the other £10 from myself[[footnote:1]]

I began editing the Christmas number for nothing, then he - of his own accord gave me £10 (I believe it was this, but it was often mixed up with payment for writing so that I am not sure[)].

This must be as you please

Yours truly C M Yonge
