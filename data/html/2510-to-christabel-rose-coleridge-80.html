My dear [[person:329]Christabel] Some of the answers are at home, and some have followed me about and I must wait to act [[other:104]Mother Goose] till I have got them all together as I hope to do when I get to Tyntesfield the end of next week. I have made acquaintance with [[person:25]Gridiron][[footnote:2]], whom I found staying at Wantage. She is not so lame as [[person:1462]Gertrude] but I should think in a far more precarious state though she came all the way from Wales to Wantage by herself. I also saw [[person:50]Polypodium] and Hartstongue[[footnote:3]] who are very nice and sensible, and I gave [[person:242]Mary Avice Butler] leave to become a double to [[person:822]Frog] I am not sure whether she is to be Tadpole or Polyanthus I wanted her to be Pollywag but her mother did not like it. The two new Scotch ones meant to be Thistle & Heather old names but it does not signify, they are said to be very clever, and as they belong to the same Forbeses as the [[person:516]Bishop of Brechin] I should think they must.[[footnote:4]]

Alas it is too late for your story, the Xmas no is in the printer’s hands. I think next year of having in combination ‘a little knowledge is a dangerous thing’ and ‘dont teach your grandmother to suck eggs,’ with perhaps the reverse on old dwarfs & giants. Only the dwarfs must teach the Giants. I am staying all around the world, and hope to get home by the 5th of Septr

yours affectionately C M Yonge
