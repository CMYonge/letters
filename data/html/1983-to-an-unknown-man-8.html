Sir, I am inclined to think that your purpose would best by answered by [[person:1475]SW]’s Manuals on the Prayerbook, which have been written to enable scholars to prepare for competition for the Bishop of Exeter’s Prize.[[footnote:1]] They, however, are only on parts of the Prayer book. There are also easy questions, going through the Prayerbook in recent volumes of the [[other:81]Monthly Paper], I think the 3d and 4th but am not sure.

Yours faithfully C M Yonge
