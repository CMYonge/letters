My dear [[person:1533]Francis],

Here is a new stamp just issued in Philadelphia[[footnote:2]] for carrying letters from one part of the city to the other, I suppose a successor to our old friend Blood. I conclude you have not got it, but if you have one, please return this, as my stamp office has four clients, of whom you always have the first turn, as the original one - I wish you had all been at the picnic on the heath, it was great fun.

When any of you do come this way, we shall shew ourselves off to much more advantage than when your [[person:1539]mother] saw us last, though as some articles are yet to come, we dont consider ourselves as come to perfection - but still I think if she came this way, she would bear the sight of us better than last time --[[footnote:3]]

Yours very sincerely

C M Yonge
