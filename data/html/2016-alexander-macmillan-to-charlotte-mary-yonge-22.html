Dear Miss Yonge

[[person:1933]Mr Sutcliffe] is a very old friend of ours. [[person:939]I] must write him on the subject of plain hand. He[,] the head of an Educational department[,] ought to know better.[[footnote:1]]

I have sent you four copies of [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds] through [[other:178]Nutt & Wells]. I suppose you can get them thence.

[[person:941]My sister] threatens you with a letter.[[footnote:1]] But we wanted to see the first [[other:177]chapter] in type first. The printer is doing this.

Yours ever truly A. Macmillan
