Dear Miss Yonge, We sent the 11 copies to make your dozen by rail the same day that the single copy was sent you by post Feb:27.[[person:939] I] have seen in the parcels book the signature of the clerk at the Golden Cross booking office. I have sent down to enquire about it. Perhaps you could enquire at your end. The address was quite clear Elderfield Otterbourne Winchester I am sending you a copy of the gilt-edged cover by the post.[[footnote:1]]

Yours very truly A. Macmillan
