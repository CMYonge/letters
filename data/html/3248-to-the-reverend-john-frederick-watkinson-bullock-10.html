Dear [[person:221]Mr Bullock], Here I send Archbishop Sancroft, hoping he is the right length. Miss Strickland’s [[otherbook:689]Seven Bishops] made it rapid work.

Have you thought of Frampton - a non juror, though not in time to be added to the 7. I have a [[otherbook:690]life] of him and could write it. I have just had your letter. I do not much incline to Sir R Fanshaw. I had his wife’s [[otherbook:520]book] long ago, and lost sight of it. I think it was from the [[other:29]London Library] but went on into vanities of political mission rather wearily. However, Lord Carnarvon who was killed at Newbury or Edmund Verney are more model Cavaliers.[[footnote:1]]

Thank you for your card about Bp Andrews. I handed it to the Chairman of our society - Andrews’ [[person:40]descendant] is Master of [[other:130]St Cross][[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
