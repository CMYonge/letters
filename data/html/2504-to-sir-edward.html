My dear Sir Edward

I forgot about [[cmybook:91]Lady Beatrix Graham] to which I certainly contributed nothing but the preface.[[footnote:1]] I did not even profess to edit it so it was quite gratuitously attributed to me. I ought to have remembered that is had been so.

I am not quite sure where your knowledge of the ornaments of the County hall stopped short. I went over it about Christmas and thought the effect most beautiful

There is a collection being made of the worthies connected with the Castle whose shields are to appear in the windows. No doubt [[person:2296]Mr Wyatt] has told you of the curious projection of masonry [words missing] of the upper end, intended perhaps to support the throne, and with an opening in the wall, apparently on a level with the head of a person standing on it, as if to give orders to someone outside[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
