My dear [[person:1626]Mary] I am out in the drawing room again but no farther till the wind changes, and the cough departs, but [[person:1613]Helen] is coming to look after me on Monday, and [[person:512]Miss Finlaison] has done so most kindly. By the by I never have had a headache all through so I don’t know how she came to [illegible] it- I hope [[person:2178]Sydney] is better - [[person:1180]Augusta] has begun to write letters again - All the spring seems cut short by the weather except the catkins.

I am sorry to miss the Friday evening sermons which are on popular sayings

All is not gold that glitters A friend in need is a friend indeed Time and Tide wait for nobody – I only heard the first and second and liked them much

your most affectionate C M Yonge
