Dear Miss Yonge [[person:939]I] will see [[otherbook:419]Theuerdank] somehow though I fear I shall not be at Oxford before I go to America on the 28th. The Bodleian dont let books out - in which they are quite right. Cambridge U.L. does - in which they are quite wrong, though I often benefit by it. I have written to Cambridge. When I see what the [[other:167]pictures] look like I will be able to judge how the [[cmybook:62]book] can be treated so as to have some novel effect.[[footnote:1]]

ever faithfully yours Alex Macmillan
