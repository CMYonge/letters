Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan]

Many thanks for your prompt response which relieves me of a good deal of worry.[[footnote:1]]

Yes there are two short stories to be recovered from America, about the length of the Ghost. One I make sure of in a month or six weeks, the other must depend on the civility of [[other:249]Perry Mason].[[footnote:2]]

They would both come at the end being modern, but if I need write any more to make up the set, I should know in time, as my present ideas are of the historical order

With many thanks

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
