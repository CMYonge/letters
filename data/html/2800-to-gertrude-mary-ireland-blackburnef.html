My dear [[person:160]Miss Blackburne]

Thank you for the sight of this. I have sent the review on to [[person:1654]Mr Shorthouse].[[footnote:2]] That [[other:252]Glasgow paper] must have clever people belonging to it, for they always make notices to the purpose[[footnote:3]]

I have sent the proof to the printers, and hope it will get in this next April number.

It is very interesting to see what Mr Shorthouse likes as his interpretation Artistically I think it was a great mistake of [[person:2160]Lewes] to keep adverse reviews back- for if they are ever so foolish and provoking and reviews are much less useful than they were when I began, they shew one’s mannerisms and blunders.[[footnote:4]] And as to their stinging, one gets hardened and cares very little. Is [[otherbook:784]your novel] out? I have not heard of it, and I do not think you told me its name.[[footnote:5]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
