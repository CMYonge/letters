Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] Would you be so kind as to direct this letter on to Miss [[person:971]Frances Martin]. The address on her letter was printed in some ‘old English type’ and after much consideration I decided that the word looked more like Carrara than anything else but the Post acknowledges no such place.

Thank you for your letter. I think this will suit us, but I will write on to [[person:329]Miss Coleridge]. If you do not hear in three or four days, consider that we consent[[footnote:1]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
