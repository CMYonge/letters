My dear [[person:749]Edith] Having always so heard and thought of you it seems the more natural. I was sending a parcel to [[person:1015]Dr Moberly]’s, and so put yours with it. Alas - what shall we do when it is Dr Moberly’s no longer-![[footnote:2]] You will find, shut into the book a paraphrase of some lines in German that [[person:1988]Alexandrine] wrote when she sold her pearl necklace - they were written out from the [[other:91]Month].[[footnote:3]] I mention it lest you should find the paper astray and be at a loss where it came from.

The [[otherbook:483]gates of Paradise] are gone to the printer and I shall probably send you a proof in about a fortnight.

Have you seen the [[otherbook:487]Copsley Annals], it is a very pretty book that does not seem to be enough known, and it is just the thing for superior village libraries.

yours very sincerely C M Yonge
