My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith],

I am half afraid of plunging into a tour because I have got so many on hand, that [[otherbook:344]Eastern journal] is indeed printed to the end, but I cannot get it in, and the [[otherbook:345]Journal in Holland] lingers on, and I have some [[otherbook:346]Italian letters] that I grieve not to have got in, so on the whole I think I had better decline yours, though if I had not been so ‘furred up’ as Hants people say I should have liked it very much.

I must confess to having betrayed you to [[person:1520]Florence Wilford ] She so very nearly knew that I was obliged to tell, but I think she is very discreet. She has been staying a long time with [[person:384]her aunt] nursing a sprained foot, but is now gone home.

I heard of nothing amiss about the postage of your last [[otherbook:275]proofs].

yours sincerely

C M Yonge
