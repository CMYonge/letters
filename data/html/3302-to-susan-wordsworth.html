My dear [[person:1570]Miss Wordsworth] I had just got a fresh railway book and studied possibilities, and I find that I had better come by the train that gets in at 4.25 so that I shall be with you by tea time. I propose to go home on Wednesday at 3.15 so as to be able to go to the Cathedral and see my pupil teacher[[footnote:1]]

Yours very sincerely C M Yonge
