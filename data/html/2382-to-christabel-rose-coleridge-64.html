My dear [[person:329]Christabel] The [[other:104]Goslings] will not die. I find we had better kill the useless members, and get some quite young ones in keeping the questions easier- We have now left only [[person:1603]Cricket], [[person:822]Frog], [[person:1161]Double Daisy] and[[person:539] Pixie] to whom is to be added

The Honble [[person:317]Alethea Colborne], Beechwood Plympton

I am not sure what she is to be called [[footnote:2]]- Can you still be Secretary even if you have no time for the Questions? It would be a great help to me. Frog [[footnote:3]] proposes a new one but I have not the particulars of her yet. I am going home on Thursday I have had a very pleasant expedition all round Devon ending with Torquay and Ottery. Frog suggests the Goslings writing their criticisms below Cackle, which would be amusing, I think
