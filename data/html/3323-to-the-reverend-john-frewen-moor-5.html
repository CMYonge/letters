My dear [[person:1046]Mr Moor] I have a very kind woman, [[person:2154]Mrs James Hoskins] who has brought up 6 daughters and two waifs and strays very well - is a Communicant &c She has a vacancy now having lost an old aunt who lived with her and having now only a brother and sister 8 and 7, would be glad of another child.

Only the [[other:2]Waifs and Strays] specify that other or lodgers must not be taken, meaning I think to exclude [[other:115]Bernardos][sic] - Would you send me in full the antecedents of this child, so that I could ask permission of [[person:1119]Mr Rodolf] of the Waifs.[[footnote:1]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
