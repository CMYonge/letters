[[person:263]Madam][[footnote:1]] I shall be very happy to avail myself of your pretty and pathetic tale of [[otherbook:31]Lucy and Christian Wainwright] for the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] as soon as I can find space for its appearance, but I fear this may not be immediately as I should be sorry to break up the story into several numbers as the effect would be injured.

My [[other:102]rate of payment ]is 1/6 per printed page, and on putting this into type I will communicate with you.

Allow me to make one remark. Should not Christian have spoken of a pang long ago on hearing of Col Prescott’s marriage rather than his death?

yours truly C M Yonge
