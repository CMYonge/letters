Dear Mr Craik

I do not think I ever got the Dedication back from Balmoral I know I have seen it in type, and that it needed no correction and so - on receiving your telegram, I wrote to [[other:251]Clarke].[[footnote:1]]

I did not think there was any occasion for a preface and I do not think the Queen’s name should be introduced without special permission which it would take long to get, and most likely might not be granted.

I will however send a few words to Clarke saying that I can claim accuracy for the Outline as it has been carefully revised by the best authority.

As tomorrow does not affect Edinburgh post, he will get [it] in time, and I will write it so clearly that he need not send a proof. I will tell him so.

Yours truly C M Yonge
