'Dear [[person:1643]Mr Holgate] I think I saw in some county book about Wiltshire that one of the Tukes of Tuke house was said to have been the original of Sir Roger de Coverley. [[person:1644]One of my cousins] the Crawley Boeveys who believe themselves to belong to [[person:1646]the Widow]’s heir-at-law, learnt (in an unlucky hour for me) that I had heard this report, and insists on my finding it out for him. I am sure I came upon it when staying in Salisbury, and I think in a book. Can you help me to clear it up for him I believe he is making a [[otherbook:597]family history][[footnote:1]] Yours sincerely C M Yonge
