My dear [[person:1046]Mr Moor] Time vanished away suddenly from me yesterday, or I should not have missed thanking you for the division of the Income, which will be an excellent standard for the unpractical young [[other:104]ladies], some of whom I fear have been answering the question but I shall get theirs on the 25th[[footnote:1]]

[[person:1606]Mamma] has been comparing your scale with our own, and finding we exactly agree in coals & wages and not at all in bread and meat -

Thank you much for the lock of the door, we have made some progress in finishing but are not quite in order yet

Yours very truly C M Yonge
