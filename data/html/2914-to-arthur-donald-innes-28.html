Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes]

Many thanks for the account and the [[other:160]cheques] for which I enclose receipts.

I will keep [[otherbook:1087]the Molyneuxes] till you return, as they give much pleasure here.[[footnote:1]] I think it should be observed to the author that there is an inconsistency in making the estate come through the mother and then placing a Molyneux ancestry there.

Also I think the Baptism of Eggs is an expression not desirable in a child’s book.

When you return there will be plenty of time to see about the Advertisements for next year

Yours truly C M Yonge
