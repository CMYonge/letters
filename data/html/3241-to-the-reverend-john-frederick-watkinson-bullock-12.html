Dear [[person:221]Mr Bullock], I did not think much could be done with his times in France, as they generally did not proceed from St Denys’ foundation but I took a different line, which I hope may suit you. Only can you fill in the name of the seventh Champion of Christendom[.] I think he was of Portugal, but I cannot remember, I wish he was St Maurice of Germany, but I fear he was not[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
