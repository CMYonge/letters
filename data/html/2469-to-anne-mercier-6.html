My dear [[person:990]Mrs Mercier] I tried to get your questions answered by sending them to [[person:198]Miss Bramston], the [[person:197]Dean]’s daughter, but unluckily female education at Winchester received a blow when [[person:85]Mr] & [[person:81]Mrs Awdry] went to Hurstpierpoint - and [[person:772]Mrs Johns], the only other lady who really cared, fell ill. The getting lecturers down needs some energy and there is no one to do it now.

I send you Miss Bramston’s note and that from [[person:1902]Mrs Richards], a lady who is trying to keep a school

yours truly C M Yonge
