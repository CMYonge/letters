Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

Many thanks for the [[other:160]payment]. The knowledge that the amount for [[cmybook:157]Unknown to History] is coming is quite sufficient for me at present. I have been kept waiting about the [[cmybook:48]Christian Names] by [[person:760]Mr H Jenner] of the British Museum who promised to set me right about the Keltic mythology but I suspect has forgotten all about it.[[footnote:1]] I am desiring him to let me have it again, and let me do my best with it. I think it is compressible into a volume. I took out an immense quantity of the First.

[[other:251]Clark] made no sign though no doubt there was a stamp with his name. I was surprised at the uniform obliteration of my capitals which I thought I had taught [[other:173]Clay] to observe. I observe printers have an inveterate hatred to capitals, even when they have only to copy type. I suppose it gives them more trouble - or they lay snares for corrections[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
