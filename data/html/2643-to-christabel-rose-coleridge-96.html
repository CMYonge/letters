'My dear [[person:329]Christabel] I do not think I have ever answered you about [[otherbook:902]Cherry], but I have had my cousin [[person:1599]Duke Yonge] here, and then a fierce Saturday of harvest decorations which raged from morn till dewy eve and by means of bulrushes, berries wheat, and those arching grasses at whose gathering I think you assisted turned out well.[[footnote:1]] I begin to think now that I did write, but I cant make sure, and so I go on to say that I shall welcome Cherry after [[otherbook:878]Heriots choice] and all the more if you can cut Ruth down. Are you looking for a curate, for there is rather a good Irish friend of [[person:512]Miss Finlaison]s a Mr Arthur Young going to leave Alresford for the odd reason that the old Rector does not like his doing so much in the schools. your affectionate C M Yonge
