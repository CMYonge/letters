'My dear [[person:2029]Miss Forrest] I am sending off your notice for the November Packet, thank you for it. I must not let you come in a vain search for [[person:1325]Edith Simeon], though I should be very glad to see you. She left me on Friday morning as she is going to help [[person:116]Miss Barter] for a few months in the school she is managing at Shipton. The being close to [[person:114]Mrs Henry Barter] is the great attraction, and she is really very much better for her stay in Ireland - both in strength and spirits.[[footnote:2]] I am so glad that the notice in the [[other:52]Packet] was so effective[[footnote:3]] Yours sincerely C M Yonge
