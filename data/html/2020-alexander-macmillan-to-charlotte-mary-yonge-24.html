Dear Miss Yonge The [[other:160]cheque] for £200 was sent you last night. My clerk reminded that former remittances were so made. The monthly payments will be sent the same way.

[[person:939]I ]am going to have the second [[other:177]chapter] as the first set up in type. I will venture to go over it and make any suggestions that strike me.

Yours ever faithfully A. Macmillan
