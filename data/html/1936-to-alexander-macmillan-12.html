Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan]

I am not certain whether it was mentioned that sheets of the [[cmybook:53]Trial] were to be sent to [[other:165]Messrs Williams and Norgate] to be forwarded to [[person:1396]M. Tauchnitz]. He has just written to me to ask for them which makes me mention it.

Yours faithfully C. M. Yonge
