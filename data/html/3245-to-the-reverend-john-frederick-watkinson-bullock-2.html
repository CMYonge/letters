'Dear [[person:221]Mr Bullock], I think [[person:2090]Gordon] must have been confirmed, as he was chiefly brought up in England. I see you have marked Bishop Andrews.[[footnote:2]] Can you tell me of any short biography of him, I have to recommend books for our [[other:48]Higher Religious Education Society] - books for that era of James I I doubt [[person:329]Miss C Coleridge] having time to write for you. But [[person:28]Miss Anderson Morshead] - Industrial School Andover - knew [[person:578]Bishop Gray] well, and could write of him Could write of Abp Sancroft[[footnote:3]] or [[person:929]Bp Mackenzie] - whose sister [[person:930]Anne] was one of truest saints I ever knew - I think you should have [[person:2091]Frances Havergal] - She was most devout and good, though not High Church, but most attached to the church. Also [[person:2011]Agnes] - the devoted workhouse nurse, and I do think [[person:2092]Mrs Trimmer] and [[person:1836]Hannah More] ought not to be omitted. Also Lady Falkland[[footnote:4]] I have a few doubts whether [[person:1294]Miss Sellon] realised this saintly ideal. However she fought a great battle Yours truly C M Yonge Would [[person:1418]Miss M Trench], the writer of Charles Lowder’s [[otherbook:614]life] write him for you?
