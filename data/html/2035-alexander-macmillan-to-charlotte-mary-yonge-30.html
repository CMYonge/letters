Dear Miss Yonge [[person:939]I] quite hope to write to you about the [[other:177]Bethlehem] this week. I have the duplicates, but have not got time to read them at a sitting as I meant.

Will you kindly send the Cawnpore [[cmybook:59]notice] to me. [[person:1943]Mr Trevelyan] has just been in. He was much gratified by your estimate of his [[otherbook:406]book].[[footnote:1]] For long your books and name have been familiar and dear in their family.

Yours ever faithfully A. Macmillan
