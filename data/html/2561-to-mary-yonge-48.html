My dear [[person:1626]Mary]

I am with [[person:1379]Miss Sturges Bourne] till Saturday and then poor [[person:37]Mildred] has written of the sad end of their anxieties, a letter direct from [[person:31]Beatrice] came after, with more hope in it; so I suppose [[person:32]the poor boy] must have sunk in one of those fits of suffocation. It is very sad, and will half kill poor [[person:41]Mrs Morshead], who seems to have been able to do so much less than Beatrice.[[footnote:1]] I wonder how things will be managed now, had not much been left to Henry which can hardly go on to poor [[person:35]Leslie]. It is very sad indeed; I hope it has not been a great shock to [[person:1623]uncle Yonge]. I hear of many colds, from chills in this heat. I went to the Palace at Salisbury on my way from Salisbury, and found [[person:1019]Mrs Moberly] quite ill with a chill, but getting better and going to Sidmouth next week. We had a little thunder here yesterday which has cooled it nicely. Poor [[person:2016]Charlotte Dyson] is perfectly broken hearted and bewildered about that wretched [[person:599]Dr Gully], whom she had looked on all her life as people used to do on [[person:1614]Uncle James], leaning on him for everything, and [[person:464]her aunt] is very anxious as to the effect it may have upon her health.[[footnote:2]] She would believe nothing against him at all, till his own words left no doubt. I go home on Saturday, hoping to find a tolerably habitable house, but I have been having papering done so I dread the lingerings of carpenters. [[person:1587]Charlotte] sent me such a delightful series of photos of the children Little [[person:2306]James] is so like the first face I remember of [[person:1599]Duke], and how old and nice [[person:1365]Katherine] looks.[[footnote:3]]

I had a happy time at Tyntesfield,- The new Chapel was opened. It holds 80 people and will serve the people in the lodges, & gardens.- wh are very far from Church, but on the 1st Sunday in the month there is to be no Service & all go to the parish Church[[footnote:4]]

your most affectionate C M Yonge
