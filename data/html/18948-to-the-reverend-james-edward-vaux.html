'[To the Rev. [[person:2403]James Edward Vaux], probably in response to the first edition of his book on [[otherbook:1113]Church Folklore]] [Tells him that around Otterbourne blacksmiths explode gunpowder on their anvils on St. Clement\'s Day. Describes the services she could remember in old Otterbourne Church.] [The people] made the responses in a full harmonious cadence as if it were the tradition of a chant. [Tells him that at Otterbourne it was the custom to decorate the church with greenery at Easter and Whitsun as well as at Christmas.][[footnote:1]]
