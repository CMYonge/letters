'My dear [[person:722]Lord Bishop] A great many thanks for [[otherbook:925]the poem]. I will mark the MS so that the printers should send the proof to you I hope early in January. The story is very touching as you have made it. I wonder how many such tragedies there are going on, unknown.[[footnote:1]] I shall always be grateful to it for having brought me your Christmas blessing With kind regards to [[person:721]Mrs] and [[person:2328]Miss How]. yours respectfully C M Yonge
