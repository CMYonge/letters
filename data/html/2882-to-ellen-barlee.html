My dear [[person:108]Miss Barlee]

The [[person:2344]Bishop of Madagascar] has sent me this sad history of devastation asking to have it made known in [[other:94]the Net], so I send it to you direct.[[footnote:1]] It has wandered a little as I am making visits in Devonshire but I hope to be at home by the end of the month.

yours sincerely C M Yonge
