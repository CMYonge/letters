Dear Miss Giberne [[otherbook:708]Uncle Peter\'s Fairy Tale] was written by Dr William Sewell, but the preface was not of my writing.[[footnote:1]] I am afraid it has been long out of print, and I have no copy, but it would be well worth reprinting.

yours truly C M Yonge
