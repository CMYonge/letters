Dear [[person:378]Mrs. Craik], Your note and enclosure have followed me to the home where I am staying a few days (in spite of the address on my paper). Many thanks for it.

I read with much interest the account of the [[other:197]Little Sisters] in the Magazine.[[footnote:1]] It is curious how they make their way with all kinds of people, even at Plymouth, the last place where I should have expected them to thrive.[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
