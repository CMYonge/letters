My dear [[person:237]Miss Butler], Two lines to say that here I am at home, & shall be delighted to have [[otherbook:248]Likes & Dislikes] if they are ready. I go to [[other:121]Dogd] on Monday week, but if I could have them in the interval, I should be glad. But I am afraid it is of no use to try for two chapters in [[other:52]one number] I am engaged to so much, I have been reading Peothès[[footnote:1]] in English and delighting in him.

In great haste Yours sincerely C M Yonge
