Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

Thank you for seeing to the books. The thanks for them from Wantage arrived the day before yesterday, but I could not write before as I only came home from Oxfordshire and am paying the price of my four days holiday by having more letters to answer than I can manage[[footnote:1]]

yours truly C M Yonge

Did I say how charmed I was with your bright little sister in law?[[footnote:2]]
