My dear Miss Yonge The photograph is from a picture by [[person:500]Mr Farren] the young artist whom [[person:939]I] wanted to [[other:167]illustrate] your [[other:177]Bethlehem]. I was anxious to know that you thought him up to the mark. The picture was from Kingsleys [[otherbook:398]Saints Tragedy], Saint Elizabeth of Hungary carrying her child to the altar in the early morning. I think it indicates a power considerably above average of entering into a spiritual conception. But I should be glad of your opinion.

I am having all you sent of the Bethlehem set up. I would like to go over it altogether, and when I do I want to write you about it. I think there are two or three points which might [be] worth considering again. But I will try to write at some length when I have the three chapters in type.

I am glad you find the [[otherbook:384]Shipwreck Volms] interesting.

I enclose a [[other:160]cheque] for the month. I think as far as I can guess that the story is liked.

Yours ever very truly A. Macmillan
