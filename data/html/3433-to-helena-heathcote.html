My dear [[person:663]Ellie]- Thank you for your letter. We have heard nothing more, and hardly look for anything, and indeed there had been only one letter from [[person:1610]him] since he joined [[person:2202]Baden-Powell], but that was enough to leave us no doubt that it is himself. [[footnote:1]] I am so glad he had that year at home after the Matabele War.

He was very much loved here. There was to have been a ‘Social Evening,’ but the people begged to put it off for they could not enjoy it.-

Your affectionate C. M. YONGE.
