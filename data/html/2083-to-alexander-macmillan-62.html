Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] Will you direct this on if you know the address - if not perhaps [[person:1964]Mr Williams] would kindly do so. [[person:1172]M. Pichot][[footnote:1]] wrote to ask my sanction for translation of the Dove, and I answered that I had already given permission to [[person:1354]Mlle Souvestre]. But the next thing I heard was that Mlle Souvestre had given it up for want of time so that I must write to him again, and I had made away with his letter.

Messrs [[other:183]Spottiswood] have sent to me for the preface to [[cmybook:61]the Prince & Page]. I think I enclosed it to you about a month ago, but if you do not find it ready to hand, I can easily write another.

The book is smaller than I thought it would have turned out, and must I suppose be less costly than the [[cmybook:20]Little Duke].

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
