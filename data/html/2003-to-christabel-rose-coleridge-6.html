'My dear [[person:329]Christabel,] Here is the first Golden egg that a Gosling has yet laid, though I am afraid it is only a paper one! Many thanks to you for the Eagle that contributed to it[[footnote:1]]. You know that I have got a new [[other:104]Gosling]- [[person:1603]Elizabeth Catharine Yonge], Eton - to the brood the Cobra di Capello- (because she wears spectacles)[[footnote:2]] She is only 15, but will be a capital one in time. She is staying here now, so your Sidney questions will be the first she will see. I hope to take her up to be introduced to [[person:887]Kittiwake]- Have you got the [[other:89]Barnacle] yet? your affectionate C M Yonge
