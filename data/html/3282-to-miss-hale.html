Dear [[person:605]Miss Hale] Thank you for your very interesting letter. I am amused at what you say of [[person:798]Mr Keble] calling his father the Governor.[[footnote:1]] I should think it was a domestic name, invented before the slang term arose. I know the family were fond, in their old Fairford days- of pet nicknames and that J K himself was called by his sisters ‘Charles’ for no reason that their friends the Dysons could trace. I knew all the family like relations after they were at Hursley and especially loved [[person:796]Miss Elizabeth Keble].

yours sincerely C M Yonge
