My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I send a second instalment of the [[cmybook:62]Dove in the Eagle’s Nest], I suppose about enough for another [[other:78]number].

I hope I am to have the proofs, for there is a mention of Maximilian in the first chapter as grown up which I must alter.

I have done two more chapters of [[other:177]Bethlehem], - that is I have written them, but I am not satisfied with the second of them.

Many thanks for the four copies of [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds].

What an unusually glorious spring.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
