My dear [[person:1046]Mr Moor] I feel very guilty at not answering you yesterday, but just as I had sat down to write notes, I was interrupted, and all the time after wards went away

We shall be very grateful if you can kindly delight the parish this day week, Friday 4th - and we hope you and [[person:1044]Mrs Moor] can come to /i e dinner\\ luncheon - so as to have time to arrange the room.[[footnote:2]] I think [[person:688]John Hicks] had better give his boys a half holiday and come to help you. I am sorry Rhoda has failed you

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
