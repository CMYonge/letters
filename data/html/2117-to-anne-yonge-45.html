My dear [[person:1582]Anne] Most peaceful, most gentle has the day been.[[footnote:1]] The Psalms suited perfectly, and while we said ‘In His pleasure is Life,’ a butterfly flew about in the sunshine in church. I had a short talk with the [[person:516]Bishop of Brechin] and told him your abode, and he hopes to come and see you about the end of the week. You had not sent me the Hursley letter about our dear [[person:616]Louisa]. When you can I shall indeed like to see it. [[person:1539]Mrs. Wilson] of Rownhams and I have been clinging together all day.

I did go to the early service, and stayed the time between at the park. Is not [[person:669]Sir William] to be pitied to have to supply such a place?

Such a [[other:184]gathering of the good]. Some said it was like Paradise, but oh! there tears and mourning will have fled away.

Your most affectionate C. M. Yonge
