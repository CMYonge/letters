Dear Miss Yonge Your letter has only reached me this morning. February will do very well for my Fairy tale to appear, as far as I am concerned—I only wanted to know its fate, & shall be glad to have it in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] rather than worked into one of my books.[[footnote:1]]

I have directed Macmillan to send you [[otherbook:798]my new book], which I hope will please you

yrs truly [[person:819]E. H. Knatchbull-Hugessen]
