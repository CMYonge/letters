'My dear [[person:1513]Fanny] Chambers\' [[otherbook:1106]Book of Days] gives another prophecy of [[person:2399]Mother Shipton] but no account of her, and I do not know where else to look among my own resources, but I will look out for some of the people who know everything and see if they know I have an idea that people used to go and see Mother Shipton among the wax works when they went to London and saw the Lions in the Tower. But I dare say I shall be able to find out more. I am to dine at Bishopstoke on Monday to meet [[person:1515]Sir Richard] so I shall hear of you then.[[footnote:3]] It is a pleasure to hear of you indeed when I can get the chance. We go on much in the same way and I hope [[person:1462]my invalid] is not beginning the winter badly. What a loss the [[person:516]Bishop of Brechin] is, he is another gone from the old Hursley friends.
