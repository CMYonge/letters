My dear [[person:1025]Alice] Jill is without a doubt Gillian, which is Juliana, and can be traced in genealogies all around the country. Of course that has the same derivation as Julius, whatever that may correctly be.

In France there were two Saints, popularly called Josse. One was Jodocus apparently Welsh, who came to Brittany, then was a hermit at Ponthieue, and Jodocus Venator appears in Doomsday, so probably Josse with diminutive Josceline was our name. There was another Josse made out of St Justus or Juste of Lyons, and no one knows the rights of the contractions or which is which!

Joyce - feminine is Jocosa, but I much doubt Josceline being feminine properly, I never met one except old Countess Joscelind in the [[otherbook:678]Antiquary], not an agreeable namesake
