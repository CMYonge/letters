My dear [[person:675]Cobweb]

Your book is come, and as it will not travel by the post well, I am going to send it to London by [[person:1184]Mr] and [[person:1180]Mrs Pode][[footnote:1]] - Will you let me know whether they shall leave it anywhere for you or send it on to Chelsea by parcels delivery

I have not been able to settle the prize questions yet as all the answers have not come in.

Owl[[footnote:2]] sent me a very tough set of questions the other day, but I have since heard that her brother is very ill, so I do not know whether she will have time or spirits to reconsider them

your faithful

Mother Goose
