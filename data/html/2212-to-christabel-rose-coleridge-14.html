My dear [[person:329]Christabel] If it is copyright, I think they ought to give you at least £10, you did not sell your copyright to the [[otherbook:365]Eagle] and it may sometimes make a difference in case of one’s wanting to collect short stories together into a volume[[footnote:1]]

I do not know if this is the right sum, but it seems to me about fair

yours affectionately and much hurried C M Yonge

I shall be here till the 30th so the answer had better come here.
