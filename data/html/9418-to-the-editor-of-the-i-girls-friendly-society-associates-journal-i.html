'THE WOMEN’S REBELLION Dear Madam,- In the criticism of the Christmas number of the [[other:52]Monthly Packet], [[otherbook:435]‘The Women\'s Rebellion’] is said to be incorrect because parish priests are always secular clergy.[[footnote:1]] What may have been the ecclesiastical arrangements I do not know, but the [[person:198]author], who well knows the north of Italy, has seen several parishes in charge of Capuchin friars; for instance Bordighiera. The incident really happened. I remain, &c., C.M. Yonge.
