Dear Miss Yonge,

[[otherbook:1091]New Eng. Dictionary]

Mr. [[person:2388]Fitzedward Hall] has given me a reference for Clidders to [[cmybook:15]Heir of Redclyffe] ch. vii., which we cannot find. Would it be possible for you to send me the quotation & with the proper reference? Literary quotations for the word are not very easily got.

We know your hand well in the [[other:151]earlier Dictionary material], though it has not been my fortune to see it often in later times.

Yours very truly, [[person:1074]J.A.H. Murray ]
