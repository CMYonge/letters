My dear [[person:1025]Alice], The Times was quite right, [[person:1733]Lucien] was at the camp,[[footnote:1]] though I cannot remember him. [[person:1735]Montebello] told [[person:313]Lord Seaton] that he is very sorry to see our troops in such excellent order. [[person:1694]The Queen] looked in great good-humour, and was determined to see the men have their dinner. She came to Virginia Water with [[person:1736]Prince Albert], who was sneezing and looking as if he had the measles. Lord and [[person:314]Lady Seaton] are at the Palace again to-night.[[footnote:2]] They have a great luncheon in marquees to-morrow. So much for camp news. . . .We have had a famous party from Fieldhouse; you should have seen [[person:1021]Robert], [[person:1017]Johnnie], [[person:81]Emily][[footnote:3]], and Rover all at tea at the little round table. I wish you could have been at Hursley yesterday. We had a nice sight of the [[person:578]Bishop] and [[person:579]Mrs. Gray][[footnote:4]] in the Vicarage after service, and Mrs. Gray brought down some beautiful drawings of the Table Mountain . . .
