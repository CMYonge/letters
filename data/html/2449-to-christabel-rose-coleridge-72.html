My dear [[person:329]Christabel]

I think if you told [[person:1433]Warne ]that you had had an offer from [[other:238]Smith and Elder] he would be sure to take your story. The stupid man has just refused Florence Wilford’s [[otherbook:816]dominie] and ought to be scarified.[[footnote:1]] I shall have [[person:1154]Joanna ]and [[person:1154]Fanny Patteson] with me on the 20th, and it is also the time of one of the [[other:240]lectures], so it will depend on a good many things whether I shall have the pleasant sight of you.

your affectionate C M Yonge
