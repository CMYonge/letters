Dear [[person:1243]Madam], I send the proof of your very pretty June [[otherbook:225]garland]. I am sorry that by some mistake of mine, May 25th was omitted in its right month. I suppose it was from its following Whitsunday. Do not you think that as Trinity Sunday is a moveable feast, it might be better to give that title instead of June 6th to the paper on the Hearts Ease? I suppose that the Dianthus Deltoides ought properly to be considered as a garden flower, but it is said by [[otherbook:185]Withering] to grow wild in Scotland, and he gives its English name as Maiden Pink. Should you object to alter the expression that St Norbert was converted? I think that it is better to use that form of speech only for conversions from heathenism.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
