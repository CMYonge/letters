My dear [[person:237]Miss Butler] Here is [[otherbook:242]Aunt Louisa]’s Berne[[footnote:1]] chapter, which with its Ogre fountain will, I think, be considered very amusing. I hope you will be able to let me have her conclusion next week, as she will then finish with the volume, always satisfactory, as not leaving straggling chapters for another. However this may be asking much in this week, and when I suppose next month is already bringing you preparations and at least engrossing your thoughts. I have not put my Scandinavian questions in order yet, but when you have time you may expect a long list[[footnote:2]]

yours sincerely C M Yonge
