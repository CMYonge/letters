My dear [[person:1025]Alice], Would [[person:1014]George] mind being the Colonel? He is never on the stage with Edmund, and a cloak and blue scarf would turn him into a Roundhead. I do not see what else is to be done, for altering the part now would spoil the dinner scene. I am glad you are not more perfect in your parts. I say mine every evening when I am going to bed, but I cannot leave off laughing in the wrong places, especially when I have to congratulate Edmund on his alteration since I saw him six years ago. Mr. Dunderhead[[footnote:1]] lives in the little dressing-room, to the amazement of all beholders who come suddenly upon him in the twilight.
