'[To [[person:939]Alexander Macmillan]]

I would think that [[person:853]Mr Lea]’s proposal was a very good one, and that the [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds] might be very useful as such a class book. But I imagine that to give it currency, it would be advisable to get it placed on the list of books recommended by the Council of Education people. I suppose there would have to be some omissions to make it into so cheap a book.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
