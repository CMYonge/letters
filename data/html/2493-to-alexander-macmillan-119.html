Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] I send the two vols of [[cmybook:102]Bishop Patteson] by train today, having done all the abridging that I think they will bear, I believe it is about 100pp in each volume, and a good many lines besides so that with smaller type and thinner paper I think it will be reduced enough.

Since I began this has come the enquiry after the author of the [[otherbook:844]Children of the Forest or the Three Knights].[[footnote:1]] I cannot recollect anything about her, nor that the story of that title has been in the Monthly Packet

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
