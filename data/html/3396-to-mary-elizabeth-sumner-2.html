My dear [[person:1381]Mrs Sumner] I hope you had the bundle of materials for the April [[other:82]Mothers in Council] that I sent to Claremont, Rudleston, Manchester on the 31st January, for it is time [[other:114]Wells Gardiner] had them. If there is nothing to alter, perhaps you would leave them at his office, writing Mothers in Council on them. I only want to add a most excellent book of The Sunday School Institute [[otherbook:599]‘Christ in the Catechism’] but that can be done in the proofs.

Here is [[person:1668]Mrs Bartram], Ramsgate Vicarage writing to ask leave for their Organist to set to music ‘God Knows’ out of our October number - I suppose there is no objection, but I do not now remember where it came from.

I shall wait to answer till I hear from you.

I have not had [[person:1669]Mrs Guslett]’s paper

Yours affectionately C M Yonge
