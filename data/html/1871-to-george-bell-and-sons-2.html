[[other:135]Gentlemen], I am greatly obliged for the copies of [[person:549]Mrs] & [[person:494]Miss Gatty]’s Books. I find that my not having sooner received the former ones was owing to my directions for the forwarding of parcels to me me [sic] not having been attended to. I am sorry that I have thus been the innocent cause of so much inconvenience and of having thus received more than my due of copies.

yours faithfully C M Yonge
