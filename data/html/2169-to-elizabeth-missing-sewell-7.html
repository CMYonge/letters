My dear [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] My senses have returned for thinking and writing indeed for anything but walking. It seems to me that we might settle a great deal in a June council, [[person:351]Mary Coleridge] is to be here for a week at the beginning of the month otherwise we are quite clear. I will send you [[otherbook:482]Freeman] tomorrow. It seems to me that his first seven pages, with perhaps his 4th chapter abridged would be very useful. I have his leave and [[person:1124]Mr Palgrave]’s.[[footnote:2]] I will have the introduction rewritten by the time you come. I think our critics will be puzzled whose style it is. We shall be very glad indeed to see you when you have time for a séance.

The Simeons have no [[person:485]Queen Emma]s left but [[person:1283]Savage] may have and I will try there.[[footnote:3]] Poor [[person:1322]Captain Simeon ]lingers on but is worse every time we hear of him
