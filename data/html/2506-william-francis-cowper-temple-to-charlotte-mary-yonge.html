Dear Miss Yonge, I hope you will excuse me for writing to you without an introduction under the following circumstances.

The most eminent Clergyman in Holland, Revd [[person:827]Dr. Kuyper] told me that he is very curious to see you to thank you for the effect the [[cmybook:15]Heir of Redclyffe] had upon him.[[footnote:1]] In Philip he saw a mirror of his own character, at least in its deficiencies & the change which you described in that character had such an effect upon him as to result in what he calls his conversion. He intends to come to Broadlands on 17 July—Saturday to meet [[person:2298]Mr.] and [[person:2297]Mrs. Pearsall Smith] & some others who are interested in the views which they have adopted as to Sanctification by Faith.[[footnote:2]]

My[[person:1058] wife] & I would be very much pleased if you would come to Broadlands at that time and stay with us for a few days.

yrs faithfully [[person:1059]W. Cowper Temple ]
