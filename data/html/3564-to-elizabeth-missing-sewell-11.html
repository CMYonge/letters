My dear [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] (What a horrible pen) We shall be very glad to see you whenever you can come to us in the autumn. We have no engagement in the way. I will try to get the life of St Bernard – but the [[other:29]London Library] but I cannot say it is better than other libraries for the ease of getting books.[[footnote:1]] wonder if he is among Lamartine’s [[otherbook:578]biographies] some of which are telling, and I do not think they are very badly translated. But as you say this is a period when eloquent histories are scarce – Pray let us know as soon as you see your way to us. We have no engagement between next week and December.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
