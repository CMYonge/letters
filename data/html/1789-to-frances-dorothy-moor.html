My dear [[person:1044]Mrs Moor],

I am afraid Otterbourne will be unrepresented at [[other:124]Ampfield][[footnote:1]] on the 21st, as [[person:1606]Mamma] and I are going on the 17th to London for a few days, chiefly to meet the Colbornes on their return from Ireland.[[footnote:2]] I did not write before because our plans were not fully fixed. We shall not be long gone, but it is a pity we just miss your celebration.

yours sincerely

CMYonge
