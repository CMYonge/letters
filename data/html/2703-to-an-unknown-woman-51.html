My dear Madam

I am very sorry that [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] was so disappointing. He might as well have looked at the drawings I have been waiting to hear from [[other:236]Mr Ward], 67 Chandos St, Covent Garden He will gladly see you, and I sent him the MS of the Bear and the Goblin.[[footnote:1]] You had better make an appointment with him mentioning my name; and shew him some of your drawings I think it might lead to something as his line is illustration

yours truly C M Yonge
