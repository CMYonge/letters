Dear [[person:1239]Mr Riley] Have you Mr [[person:558]H H Gibbs]’s name-? He is either at St Dunstans, Regents Park or at Aldenham Elstree I should think no one would be more earnest in the cause than he. I would also mention the publisher - [[person:744]A D Innes ]31 Bedford Street Strand. Old Mr [[person:939]Alexander Macmillan] I am sure would but I am not so sure of his sons though his partner Mr [[person:379]G L Craik] would, I believe I am much obliged to [[person:2121]Mrs Riley] for writing to me, I am sorry I was so stupid.

Of course you know Mr Gibbs’s name as MP for the City in the last Parliament and as a Bank Director[[footnote:1]]

yours truly C M Yonge
