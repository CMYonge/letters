My dear Miss Yonge, If I am not mistaken, you have issued lately several books. Do you not think that there is one that would be suitable for republication in our [[other:224]Series]? If so, we should be glad to receive a copy, in order to judge how best to arrange our edition.

I sincerely wish that you may enjoy your usual good health and, hoping to receive your kind reply at your earliest convenience, I beg [you] to believe me,

with [[person:1396]my father’]s and my own best regards to you, Yours very sincerely [[person:2124]Tauchnitz]
