My dear [[person:263]Madam] Many thanks for [[otherbook:257]Wishop], which looks much improved by the omissions.

The M P was [[person:669]Sir William Heathcote] MP for Oxford, perhaps you will even better like to hear that [[person:798]Mr Keble] could not help listening to the [[otherbook:253]Thorns and Roses] with great interest in the middle of his work. I have put out the beginning of Wishop for March, but I cannot make sure of it, as there is a short story which must come in in Lent, and I cannot tell what room there may be. Will you be kind enough to convey my thanks to [[person:71]your sister] for the note and proof received this morning. It is hardly worth while to trouble her with a note merely to express my thanks.

yours truly C M Yonge

I forgot to add my thanks for the return of the postage stamps.
