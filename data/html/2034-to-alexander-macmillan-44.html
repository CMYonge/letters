My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan],

Thank you for Mr Trevelyan’s [[otherbook:406]Cawnpore], which will I am sure be terribly engrossing reading.[[footnote:1]]

I waited to write both because I was trying to satisfy myself with the beginning of [[other:177]Moses], and because I wanted to see what the [[other:29]London Library] would send me. And the latter is just at present - Nothing, so I should be very much obliged if you would lend me Stanley’s [[otherbook:402]Sermons in Palestine] and [[otherbook:404]Thomson]’s and [[otherbook:401]Kitto]’s books - which would be a great help. I have been writing and re writing the early part of Moses, and trying to settle how much about Egypt to put in, and at present I think it seems safest not to meddle with the uncertain chronology by trying to identify the Pharaohs in such a book as this.

All that can be said for them /now\\ has been so well said in Miss Keary’s [[otherbook:372]book]. I am trying to illustrate the religion of the desert tribes of Moses 40 years waiting out of the Book of Job. Then I don’t think I shall say much of the Israelite wanderings, except marking what in the Law was preparation. Children /or young people\\ know the narrative but do not know the core of the history - the preparation.

I believe a parcel of books is coming to me from [[person:423]Mme de Witt], directed to your care. Will you kindly let them come through [[other:178]Messrs Nutt].

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
