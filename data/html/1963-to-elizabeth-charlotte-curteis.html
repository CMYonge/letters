My dear [[person:396]Miss Curteis][[footnote:1]] Your queries shall go in next time but is your Eagle & Goose story ready.[[footnote:2]] If it is I should be thank [sic] to have it next week, as I am going from home, and I meant to get the number made complete before I go. I direct this to Leasams as Exmouth seems too large to find a stranger without further address.

I am dismayed to find that I have lost your queries. I must have torn them up by mistake for your note. Could you send them again though I am ashamed.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge

I have found your queries—but about the story? Can you let me know about the chances of it? If you have not finished it, I fear I must not have it, as the numbers are full.
