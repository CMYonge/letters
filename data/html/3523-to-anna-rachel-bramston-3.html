My dear [[person:195]Anna ] I am only just in time to catch the post and thank you very much for the photographs. The outside of the house is charming, and [[person:1024]Annie Moberly] is venturing to ask if she might have one of them and of the group. She and [[person:1209]Miss Price] came out exactly like and she is like half her sisters. She would like them unmounted.
