My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith], Many thanks for your kind answer, I think these [[cmybook:43]ladies’ biographies] will be very nice work to do together, and I believe that to look into real life minutely is the best school for one’s own mind or for fiction. If I write nothing but fiction for some time, I begin to get stupid, and to feel rather as if it had been a long meal of sweets - then history is a rest, for research or narrative brings a different part of the mind into play.

I wish there were a few people in humbler life at whose biographies one could get at as thoroughly, but I fear this is impossible. As to the size of the book that must be settled by the size of the biographies.

I will do as you wish about the [[otherbook:275]Websters]. The blunder between [[otherbook:267]Wynnes] & [[otherbook:450]Winds][[footnote:3]] was often made in our house- but I am afraid authorship is a secret that won’t keep much better than marriage and in spite of the change of name, Woolwich will be very betraying.
