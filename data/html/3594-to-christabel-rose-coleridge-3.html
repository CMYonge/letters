My dear [[person:329]C C] We cannot find your porcupine, I think he must be shut up in a MS. Susan, the cook informed me yesterday that she is going to marry in July, rather frantic for she can only get through easy work here with [[person:359]Bessie]’s help however I am glad to be spared the break down that there will be- and Bessie has a sister who will probably come so all will most likely do well, those Collins grandchildren being all of the ‘old retainer’ kind.[[footnote:1]]

I found the [[other:48]Higher Religious Education] wanting to know about giving lectures to the [[other:211]Reading Unions], which they were asked to do- so I wrote to [[person:1682]Mrs Chester]. I was attacked at once for not coming to luncheon! The young ladies were away so they forgot to ask me. I settled the [[other:82]M in C] business with [[person:1381]Mrs Sumner] You must be glad to leave the real summer heat in London

your affte C M Yonge

I wish I could find the Porcupine. How remarkably your correspondent spells.
