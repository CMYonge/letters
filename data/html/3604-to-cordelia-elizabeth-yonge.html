My dear [[person:1593]Cordelia] I shall be very glad to see you and [[person:1581]Alethea] on Wednesday - tomorrow afternoon if you can come out. I am sorry I can say no more, but Thursday is [[other:31]Mother’s Meeting] day. Friday School, Saturday a Sunday School meeting at the Vicarage.

And [[person:1462]Miss Walter] is so ill that I dare not make arrangements for more than that short afternoon time

your affectionate cousin C M Yonge
