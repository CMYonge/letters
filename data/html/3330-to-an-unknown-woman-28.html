Dear Madam I am sorry to say [[cmybook:35]‘The Mice at Play’] is gone out of print and I do not know how to recover it.

There are some nice plays for children by [[person:762]Miss Jenner] called Terra Cotta plays published by Innes, and another set by [[person:653]Mr A Heathcote] called Ragged Robin published by Allen. The second among the latter is good for little children[[footnote:1]]

yours truly C M Yonge
