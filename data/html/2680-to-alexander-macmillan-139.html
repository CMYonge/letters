Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan]

Old proofs of these Christmas stories have fortunately been discovered so I send them off herewith.[[footnote:1]]

I find I must ask you to be kind enough to let me have [[other:160]£200 on account], or else £100 now and another £100 before Christmas. There have been some unexpected calls upon me which drive me to ask this favour of you, though I am sorry to have to do so so early in the year

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
