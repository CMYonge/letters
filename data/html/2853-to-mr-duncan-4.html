Dear [[person:448]Sir][[footnote:1]]

I think it would be well to send me a set of sheets of What to Read, that I might add to them or cancel them on occasion. I know there are some good books of this winter not added, and [[otherbook:1024]Goldhanger Woods],[[footnote:2]] and I have not seen a sheet since before Christmas. I am afraid the books may be forgotten and omitted if I do not set them down soon

Yours &c C M Yonge
