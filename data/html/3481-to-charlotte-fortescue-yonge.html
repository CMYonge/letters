My dear [[person:1588]Lottie]- How are you getting on ; I am afraid there is not much change any way and that your hands are full.

I believe [[person:1613]Helen] is somewhere either in the Bay of Biscay or the Chops of the Channel; she sailed on the 18th, and in a nice cabin with her goldfinches, and after to-morrow I may have a telegram any day to say she is in the Thames.

[[person:329]Christabel] talks of coming on the 7th or 8th; 1 will let you know when, as you might be able to come over and see them.

Poor [[person:189]Reggie] has never been well since he went to St. Leonards; [[person:182]Alethea] is going over to-morrow to see about him and bring him home.

[[person:1364]Cordelia Steer] was to come to her[[person:1587] grandmother] for good, as soon as she has fully recovered on the Blue Mountains from her fever. [[person:1656]Charles] and [[person:2256]Ada] are in their new house.

Fancy a girl writing to me for my autograph and saying she had got [[person:2258]Lord Roberts], and hoped to get [[person:2257]General Buller] when she knew his address. I could not help telling her that I thought it very impertinent to worry a busy General about a young lady\'s fashion of collection. I wonder if she will heed.-

Your affectionate C. M. YONGE.
