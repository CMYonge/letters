Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], Thank you much for your kind answer. The second set of [[cmybook:93]cameos] is nearly all written, but the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] must have the first turn of them and that will take a good while - about a year at shortest.

I am afraid you think me very idle about the [[cmybook:80]Worthies]. The truth is that my [[person:1606]Mother] has grown so infirm of late that I have less time than usual, and that time I thought it wise to spend in completing Monthly Packet Material, Cameos and a [[cmybook:81]story] (of James I of Scotland in Captivity) that in case of being able to do no work, there might not be a stand still. I hope however to return ere long to Alexander the Great - whom I have left a full year on the Banks of the Granicus

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
