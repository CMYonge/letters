'My dear [[person:475]Mrs Elgie] Thank you for this precious little bit.[[footnote:1]] If you are parting with the books or any of them, I should very much like if you would let me buy the Wordsworth and the Lightfoot’s comments on the New Testament.[[footnote:2]] I think I remember the sermon that was founded on these notes. I have done the deed with Davis. I hope he has places yours affectionately C M Yonge
