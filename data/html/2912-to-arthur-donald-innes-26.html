Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes]

Next year’s programme is my own [[cmybook:216]Two Penniless Princesses] - historical longish chapters. Miss [[person:561]Agnes Giberne] has promised a story - and the [[otherbook:1085]Storm in a Teacup] has to go on another half year[[footnote:1]] I tried doubling the chapters, but there was not room. I think it is a mistake to sacrifice the story to the volume, as often in those I read, I see the development has been injured to get it to the prescribed length.

But I should like to see [[otherbook:1087]the Molyneuxes] unless they are quite bound to begin in January.[[footnote:2]]

I had been thinking of writing to you about translating and abridging Madame Necker De Saussure’s [[otherbook:1088]Education Progressive] really the best book on Education I know. It is too long to be done entire, but it is admirable in the practical part and the [[other:31]Mother’s Union] would recommend it I know, indeed it has been doing so by the cumbrous French edition I could undertake it easily, and get it into shape for use.

[[otherbook:1086]The Guiding Principle in Art] is by

[[person:662]Mrs Evelyn Heathcote][[footnote:3]] Sparsholt Rectory Winchester

Yours truly C M Yonge
