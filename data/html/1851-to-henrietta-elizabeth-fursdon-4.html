My dear [[person:538]Irene]

I send back the translation with many thanks for the sight of it. I was very sorry to see by the paper that the sorrow you feared has come,[[footnote:1]] and that the year is closing on you in sorrow and mourning, perhaps of a kind that one feels doubly, through one’s parents as well as oneself. I do not know whether you are feeling able to care for these Gosling matters now but I think I told [[person:973]Pena] all about them.

The [[person:1168]Firefly] and [[person:675]Cobweb] had each three votes so they will each have an equal prize. Many thanks to you and [[person:534]Glow Worm] for your photographs, hers is one of the best, and I am glad to have seen her brown eyes, and know the likeness

your affectionate

Mother Goose
