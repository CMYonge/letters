My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith] Thank you for the Acrostic, but I had decided on leaving that field to the [[other:79]Magazine for the Young], which always has a batch of Acrostics at Christmas.[[footnote:2]] I have so often refused them on that score that I could not take these. We guessed all except the two Es, which I do not quite understand, unless it means Edward and Elizabeth - and for Idiot too we had to go to the answer.

yours sincerely C M Yonge
