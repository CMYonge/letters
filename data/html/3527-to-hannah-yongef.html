My dear [[person:253]Hannah][[footnote:3]]

At last I have got the direction you wished for - it is after all where it was and the address should be to

The Committee 32 Southampton Street Strand[[footnote:4]]

I think they ought to make their advertisements clearer and better known, and I believe the clergyman who used to manage it is dead, and that his wife is as much in want as any she used to interest herself about, but that the organisation is just recovering the loss. There was a letter from [[person:1180]Augusta] yesterday speaking of poor [[person:2073]Charlotte] as still lingering -

We much hope that if you should find it a convenience, if Stoke Lodge is let before [[person:2072]Mrs Rogers] goes to Ryde, that you will come to us - you know nothing can be quieter than we are, and we should have such pleasure in trying to make you comfortable. Do not think of answering this -

Your affectionate cousin C M Yonge
