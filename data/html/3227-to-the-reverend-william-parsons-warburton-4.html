Dear [[person:1466]Canon Warburton ] I will send £1 for dear Bishop [[person:213]Harold Browne]’s memorial but I am away now from cheque books. I go home on Saturday however having partly come to hear the history of the [[other:225]wonderful surprises] of Aug 11th with which the Moberly’s had much to do.[[footnote:1]]

[[person:1569]Mrs Wordsworth ]is very cheerful, I hope to see her this afternoon[[footnote:2]]

Yours very truly C M Yonge
