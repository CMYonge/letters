Madam, I am very sorry your last letter was so long unanswered from my having lost it. I fancy I must have torn it up, deceived by the general appearance being like that of the letters of a frequent correspondent. The [[otherbook:460]Lutheran First Communion] is at p. 544 of [[other:52]Vol 30]—the [[otherbook:459]Bells at Weisbaden] at 441 of 29. The amount due for them is 30/ which I will send if you will send me your Christian name to enable me to get a post office order. [[otherbook:461]Lord Moorlane][[footnote:1]] is very amusing, and I will gladly put him in as soon as I can.

Yours faithfully, C M Yonge
