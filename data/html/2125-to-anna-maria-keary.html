My dear [[person:793]Miss Keary] Here I am in the depths of Devonshire which must account for my not having sooner answered your letter. We both got out of sorts and wanted a change so here we are, thinking this beautiful county infinitely more beautiful in spring than in autumn, the red earth and young green contrast so beautifully together.

I think it does seem wise to complete the [[otherbook:467]Scandinavian sketches] with Magnus, and the Siamese bits that you propose instead sound very charming, better I think than the review, unless one could be so lucky as to have both.

I read [[otherbook:465]Clemency] all the way down here, and was very much delighted with her, - her personally I specially mean, she is so noble and grand, and comes in so well after the trumpery women it is the fashion to put into stories now. But I think Mrs Edgecombe is the most personally engaging of all - though I am very fond of Aunt Bessie. And the way the poor Colonel was caught is excellent. I shall be here (at the [[person:314]Lady Seaton]’s, Beechwood, Plymton) till this day week, when we go to the [[person:1623]Revd J Yonge]’s [[other:123]Puslinch] Yealmton

yours sincerely C M Yonge
