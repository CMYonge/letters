Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], Many thanks, it is very agreeable to get anything out of [[other:172]America]. I have a [[cmybook:81]story] of the time of Henry V and James I of Scotland - about half out in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet], and all written. I was thinking of proposing it to you for Christmas when it will be finished in the Monthly Packet. Might not some arrangement be made about it with [[other:204]Scribner]. I could either send them the numbers already in type the MP - or they might be printed at once, and the duplicate sheets sent up to the end. I do not think we ever came to any agreement about the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet] itself - whether you give me £200 as before for the first edition.

I do not know what has become of the proof of the [[cmybook:89]Parallel History]. The printers sent me the copy, evidently printed from but no proofs with it.

What do you think about a volume of essay kind of papers? I have done a few at times for the [[other:59]Christian Remembrancer], [[cmybook:302]one] on Miss Sewell’s [[otherbook:548]principles of education] - one on [[cmybook:65]Folklore], an [[cmybook:303]account of the Sanitary Commission] in the American war, a [[cmybook:66]life of Lord Seaton], and a few more which are in the [[other:76]Literary Churchman] - they seem to me some of them rather worth saving, but they would keep. I have likewise in MS a nearly completed essay on or rather history of literature for children, which I should add to those. It is too long for the [[other:78]Magazine], and not at all in the same tone.[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
