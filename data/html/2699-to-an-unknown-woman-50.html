Dear Madam

I am today sending your book to [[other:111]Macmillans] 29 and 30 Bedford Street, Covent Garden, and asking if they can give you one of my books to illustrate. It might be a good plan for you to call there, tomorrow or next day. Send up your card and ask for [[person:379]Mr Craik] or [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], mentioning my name.

If you cannot call, write, and say how the book shall be returned asking if they can give you a commission to do some illustrations.

After that I will write to [[other:236]Marcus Ward], who deals more in illustrations, and is going to publish the Bear and the Goblin in a book of poetry[[footnote:1]]

yours truly C M Yonge
