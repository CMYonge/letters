My dear [[person:237]Miss Butler] Thank you for your message. I do not think Rudolf requires to return to you for he stands so much alone that he only needs to be taken out.[[footnote:1]]

Thanks too for the derivations, I shall trouble you with plenty more, I have no doubt, when I am at home with my list, and see my way out of the Latin derived names.[[footnote:2]] I am to go home this afternoon after a very happy visit here. [[person:113]Elizabeth Barnett] is writing on the opposite side of the table, I wish I was going to take her back with me. My [[person:1606]mother] is reading your [[otherbook:154]philosophe] with great delight

yours sincerely C M Yonge
