My dear [[person:329]Christabel]

The elder sister may be a good modification and pray do you – or [[person:1160]FMP] invent a love story to work in.[[footnote:1]]

No Robber appears so do pray write him, also Geo III and the beggar. I have done a White Ship. There ought I fancy to be about 30, and I have 28 in my eye or written out ie if I take two Whittingtons.

What do you think of the baby Pretender in his nurses’ arms under the Church wall in his mother’s flight?

In haste your affectionate C M Yonge
