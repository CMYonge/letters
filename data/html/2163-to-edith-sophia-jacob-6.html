My dear [[person:749]Miss Jacob], I have the [[otherbook:486]Récit d’une Soeur], and if you like to read it, I could leave it, either at your house in the Close, at [[other:187]Jacob & Johnsons] or any other convenient house of call at Winchester. It is a beautiful book - the family are so good and charming, though not equal in intellect or poetry to the Guérins.[[footnote:1]] The great matter in reading it is not to judge it by the first half of the first volume. I like the second much the best. I was sure you would come under the spell of [[otherbook:484]Gerontius]. What a wonderful [[person:1084]mind] it is that thought it out. I see it said that it was a real dream of [[person:1542]Cardinal Wiseman], but I believe much more that the [erasure] old man ‘Gerontius’ was himself. That adjective correction is thoroughly a man’s.[[footnote:2]] Any day before the 20th, will be quite in time for me to have the [[otherbook:483]Dream].[[footnote:3]]

yours very sincerely C M Yonge
