'My dear [[person:1143]Sir] If you are writing to [[person:1396]Tauchnitz],[[footnote:1]] I hope you will be kind enough to express my best thanks for the very curious and prettily got up book which I am much obliged to you for forwarding to me, the binding is beautiful, and there is much interest in the comparison of the contents. I thank you too for the enclosed review of [[person:1244]Miss Roberts]’ [[otherbook:270]book]. I have not heard from her for so long that I begin to think she must be on her way home, and so do not write to her Yours faithfully C.M. Yonge
