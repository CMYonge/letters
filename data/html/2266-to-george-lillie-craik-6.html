Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] I send the lacking [[cmybook:73]Cameo].[[footnote:1]] I have found things needing correction in three sheets of the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet of Pearls], and I am searching for a sentence that I know was spoilt for want of a not - but the whereabouts of which I can in no wise as yet discover. If I do not find it before tomorrow’s post, I shall send the sheets to you which I have corrected for you to judge as to the importance of the matter.

The unlucky thing was that none of the books I had told me Sir Francis Walsingham’s age, and thus I made him an elderly man when he was but six and thirty and his daughters young ladies instead of small children. I antedated Sidney’s knighthood, too, and in these days any anachronism does not do. I have only just got Fox Bourne’s Sidney which cleared it up[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
