Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan]

I am sorry for the mistake, it was not wilful for I looked at the stamp, and found it so pale that I saw nothing but the Cl which I took for [[other:173]Clay].[[footnote:1]]

Will ‘By-words in action’ do for the name of [[cmybook:135]this collection of stories], as all illustrate some saying or other. I have recovered one from America, and will send it in a day or two, but I think it will be safer to send these last three together

Perhaps I may add two semi-translations from the French

Yours truly C M Yonge
