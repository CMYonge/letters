Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I see [[otherbook:469]Old Sir Douglas] is living and thriving once more, so I write to ask whether you wish for the first instalment of the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet of Pearls] for October, or whether you wish to wait till the end of one of the present stories.[[footnote:1]] I have written almost to the end, but it wants plenty of re-touching. I have had so much interruption that I /often\\ could not go into work that took more study - however I have made a beginning for the [[cmybook:80]Worthies] and have finished the first.

I hope your party are pretty well, and [[person:941]Mrs Daniel] in a less anxious state

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
