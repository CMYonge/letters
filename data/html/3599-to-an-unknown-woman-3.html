Dear Madam I send two of the books you wish for. [[cmybook:221]The Cross Roads] is I find being read to a class of [[other:23]G F S] girls, but will be finished by the time you have done with these two. [[otherbook:211]John Brent] is by S Baring Gould, one of the penny SPCK series. I know I have it and have read it aloud, but I cannot lay my hand on it this moment.

I have only one copy of the [[cmybook:232]Chimes], which is being read every week so I cannot well send it. Our Mothers are fairly punctual, and the accounts cover the little irregular time so we begin with the Litany provided by the [[other:31]Mother’s Union] and then have the religious reading. It is a better time for the [[person:184]Vicar]’s being able to come as he does sometimes that[sic] at the end, when the children are apt to get restless

Yours truly C M Yonge

There is a very good SPCK tract called ‘The Black Copse’[[footnote:1]]
