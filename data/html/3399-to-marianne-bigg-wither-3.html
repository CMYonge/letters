'[To [[person:1546]Marianne Bigg Wither] about the death of her brother the Rev. [[person:1544]William Bigg Wither].]

I had not heard for a fortnight, and had just made up my mind to write to ask [[person:1221]Raby] whether you knew anything, and when I saw your writing I knew how it must be. This gradual, gentle sinking is the most merciful way of going one can think of, though I hope that there may not be the restlessness that belongs to weakness and is so very distressing. I shall ask [[person:184]Henry Bowles] to pray for him, especially on Sunday when the people are there. I hope it is peaceful sleepiness, and that [[person:1089]your niece] or one of the nephews can be with him. The last letter I had, now more than a fortnight ago, spoke of going to church at eleven and preaching from his chair.
