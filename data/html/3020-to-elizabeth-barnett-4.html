Thank you, I have seen the Times.[[footnote:1]] Sir [[person:669]William Heathcote] told me there was such an article, but he had not had time to read it, so I had to wait till morning in doubt whether it would be knock-down one, and it was rather a relief that it was not all abuse. It is very amusing to see how Miss Wellwood[[footnote:2]] comes in for exactly the same abuse as if she was alive, and with the same discrimination as to facts. It seems to me exactly the world\'s judgment of Guy and Philip - loving Guy and not understanding him, and sympathizing with Philip as more comprehensible. However, [[person:464]Marianne]\'s son[[footnote:3]] cannot be disliked, in spite of his principles - a great triumph for her.
