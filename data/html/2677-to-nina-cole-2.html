My dear [[person:326]Miss Cole]

I have been a long time answering you, but I waited to see [[person:1042]Mrs Moore] and hear more about the matter as it is one in which I hardly felt competent to judge. I am sure it is an excellent purpose, and I send you my shilling, though my country life is so utterly unconnected with anything of the kind that I can be of no use[[footnote:1]]

yours sincerely C M Yonge
