My dear [[person:1626]Mary] I hope your Dynamite explosions will soon cease Are they through Sheepstor or through the wood above Hanover Green[?] [[person:1559]Major Woollcombe] and I have been mourning over them. He does not think the Auckland people are RCs. You remember one Lord Auckland was Bishop of the I of Man.[[footnote:1]] A RC named [[person:2167]Capes], who takes one of the houses on the Winchester Road and the village people say his rough terriers name is Pope, which I cannot believe. Major and [[person:1558]Mrs Woollcombe] have been here for a week, sleeping at the Vicarage but she up here all day. [[person:1462]Gertrude] was so ill when she came that I should have sent for her, if she had not been coming but she rallied on Wednesday and is nearly in her usual state, so Mary is gone – just now, to be able to come when she is more needed. [[person:184]Henry Bowles], with [[person:189]Reginald] is gone today to Guildford the [[person:190]Grandfather] there is so delighted to have Reggie[[footnote:2]] [[person:182]Alethea] and the Baby move up to the Post Office, and the nurse takes [[person:183]Cicely ]and [[person:185]Margaret] to Dorking while repairs and cleaning are going on. The [[other:231]Church Army] van is at Allbrook, and is to come up here in a day or two. It shows a magic lantern, and I do not quite know what more it is to do. The captain of it read the Lessons at Church on Sunday, very loud and not in an educated voice. I wish gentianella would grow here as yours do! But I never saw the garden prettier, and the nightingales are singing with all their might Banksias are coming out and I brought in a Gloire de Dijon this morning I hope yours have met [[person:1042]Jane], but uncertainties always attend the Colbornes, as we well know. I thought she was to come home this year for good.[[footnote:3]]

your most affectionate C M Yonge
