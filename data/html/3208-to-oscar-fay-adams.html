Dear[[person:16] Mr Adams] I think such papers as you kindly propose would be very valuable but my colleague, Miss [[person:329]Christabel Coleridge] must be consulted, and likewise the publisher Mr [[person:744]Arthur D Innes], 31 Bedford St Strand[[footnote:1]]

I think as you are in London it might simplify matters if you would call on him, as he would tell you about length of papers and terms and I believe we would find the papers very interesting

Miss Coleridge lives at Cheyne Torquay so is rather out of reach unless you have yet to visit Exeter, Ottery St Mary Church and Truro

yours truly C M Yonge
