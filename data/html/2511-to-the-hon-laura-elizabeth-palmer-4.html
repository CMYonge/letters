My dear [[person:1238]Miss Palmer] The printer has put your name in full upon this proof - but if you do not wish it to appear, you had better scratch it out, though of course the Packet would be glad to have the story owned.[[footnote:3]]

I am glad it did not come while you were occupied with the wedding bustle, they like to put the Christmas number in hand a long time in advance. I am now at Mr [[person:523]Edward Freeman]’s, but to morrow I go to [[person:560]Mr Gibbs]’s at Tyntesfield and thence to Heaths Court.

If it will suit [[person:2299]Lady Selborne], I should be glad to come to you on Friday the 4th, for a night - it is a day later than I intended, but I have had to squeeze in another visit before coming to Crookham, and I shall not get there till the 27th

yours sincerely C M Yonge
