My dear [[person:1046]Mr Moor]

I wrote the paper you asked me to do for the [[other:60]CQ] but I am sorry to say it was not good enough, and was rejected. I rather suspected that it would be for it was too long and too like a report. Do you like to have it for use in any other way? I have had an article from Mr Donald Mackay sent me for the [[other:52]Packet] for August on the Centenary[[footnote:1]]

I shall come home on the 16th, and I will then send your books back with many thanks, and wishes I had done it better

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
