Dear [[person:939]Sir],

My brother will call on you on Wednesday morning, the 2nd, unless he should hear from you to the contrary. His address is [[person:1624]J.B. Yonge] Esq

9 Montague Place

Bryanston Square.

I have just heard that there are 50 copies of the [[cmybook:23]Lances of Lynwood] ordered, and that there are none in stock.

I think a new edition should be put in hand at once, it is a childs book, with designs by [[person:159]Mrs Blackburn] engraved by [[other:163]Dalziel], I will send up a copy of the 1st edition by my brother. I have not one of the more recent, but I know it was smaller and less ornamental though with the illustrations - Perhaps you will judge how the new one should appear.

I shall send by him the account of “[[cmybook:38]the Young Stepmother], which I think should be taken into consideration as a basis for arranging the terms for the [[cmybook:53]Trial].

Yours faithfully C.M. Yonge
