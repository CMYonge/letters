My dear [[person:1520]Florence],

Many thanks to you and your friend for your kindness about the pantomime. I am sorry however to say that I do not think it will quite do. I suppose it is the sort of thing to be done not so much by a regular narrative of the scenes as by some clever sketch such as perhaps takes an older hand. I hope it will not be a disappointment, but I think from your letter you have prepared her for it. I am glad you like some of the [[other:69]Events of the Month], I think it will improve by practice. You have not told me the fate of Christina with the [[other:62]Churchman], I greatly think that serial is improving under the new [[person:1328]Master] - there is a very good story beginning.[[footnote:2]] I am so glad your foot is better - I have some friends staying here and not much time so I must finish

yours sincerely C Yonge
