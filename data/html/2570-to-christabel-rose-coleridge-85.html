My dear [[person:329]Christabel]

Here are a not very brilliant set of answers I rather doubt whether the question about the fall of Constantinople was understood I expect there will be a rush at [[person:25]Gridiron]’s ghost question.[[footnote:1]]

I shall be very glad if [[otherbook:741]Fernando] ends by coming to me.[[footnote:2]] [[person:1483]Miss Weber]s [[otherbook:879]At Sixes and Sevens], a conclusion to [[otherbook:855]I wonder why] is very disappointing[[footnote:3]] Grace is so stupidly in love without being asked, and everybody goes on at weary cross purposes from first to last, and the sundial motto is so repeated that we grew sick of it I have my doubts whether she can write a long book. Our religious education field day at [[other:107]school] is coming off tomorrow, and the pupil teachers are in great excitement they themselves have both come out first class. What a capital story [[person:1244]Miss Roberts] has got in [[other:71]Friendly Leaves]. I suppose the point in the Penzance court is that Convocation was against having his court, and that submitting to an old usurpation is not like being handed on to a new one, but I am sure I do not know whether this is the right view[[footnote:4]]

your affectionate C M Yonge
