'Dear [[person:263]Miss Smith] I hope that in a day or two you will receive the proof of your [[otherbook:262]Royal Household], which I should be much obliged to you to look over and return to [[other:108]Messrs Mozley] Friar Gate Derby I am setting off to see the Manchester Exhibition[[footnote:1]] and from thence most probably to Ireland, so that you may judge my time and head are not in the most favorable state for correcting yours sincerely C M Yonge
