Dear [[person:942]Mr Macmillan] I hope to send all but the three or four concluding chapters of my story, [[cmybook:239]A Release or Caroline’s French Kindred] tomorrow

I should very much like them to appear in [[other:88]St Nicholas], but the difficulty is that the really first part is in the Christmas Number of the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] for 1893, and if this story appeared separately, I must rewrite the mise en scene, as I suppose the republication of Caroline’s Defiance would be objected to. I cannot lay my hand on the Xmas number, perhaps you could procure it from [[person:744]Mr Innes]. I doubt however whether my story has enough of the child element in it for St Nicholas

Yours very truly C M Yonge
