My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith]

I have not the letter, at least I do not believe I have but I am almost certain that it was to be the 4 couples at 2lb; and I think you may safely so apply it. I will wait a week to write to [[person:482]Mrs Elphinstone] in case you should be able to tell me anything specially about any of the people.[[footnote:1]]

I do not know whether I forgot to finish my story or only put it stupidly, the point of it was the staring of the strangers at hearing such a grave polished man as [[person:629]Dr Harris] proposing the worst street in Torr as an allurement to a lady. That Torr Churchyard is indeed a strangely mournful place, with all its records of early deaths among strangers and its graves so close together I wish we had more time to spend with the kind cousins there, but I was hurried away by having to meet [[person:1297]Mrs Selwyn] before her return to New Zealand, and I could not go to Cockington though I have a whole houseful of cousins and a godson there.[[footnote:2]] I did once walk there from Livermead many years ago.

[[person:981]Masters]’s corrections and criticisms amuse me very much - it was great fun to see how he presided over the [[otherbook:315]Maiden of our own day]. I wish you would have a good fight with him it would do him good

yours sincerely

C M Yonge
