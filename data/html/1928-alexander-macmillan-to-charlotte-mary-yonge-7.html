Dear Miss Yonge [[person:939]I] will see whether we can find some one who would do a few vigorous outlines for the [[cmybook:20]little Duke]. Something like those to Kingsleys [[otherbook:358]Heroes]. Reproductions of foreign books are not generally successful.

We send the [[cmybook:24]Daisy Chain] to press today

I am very glad indeed that you take to our idea. It would do admirable [sic] for a Christmas book.[[footnote:1]] I think you have quite caught the idea I had & I think also what my [[person:941]sister] had. We will talk over the matter tonight. I would certainly have some obscurer stars of this kind brought to light, if possible, so as to include some who have not sent down a \'Roaring voice\'.[[footnote:2]] But when I have talked over the matter with my sister I will write again, or perhaps I may venture to get her to write herself. I suppose ladies should understand each other best.

I am dear Miss Yonge Yours very faithfully Alex Macmillan
