My dear [[person:2034]Mrs Curteis]

First I must tell you of what perhaps you have seen in the [[other:75]Guardian], my dear [[person:464]Miss Dyson]’s death. She had been growing feebler and feebler for the last two years and had for months been wholly confined to bed, and unable to read even a letter, though she was constantly read to for the last fortnight she was almost unconscious, and slept herself away. She leaves a great blank behind her!

I am afraid it is too late to protest against [[otherbook:907]Helen’s babies], except by word of mouth as I have done, for I can’t bear them[[footnote:1]] But I think they have had their day - and the bad imitation will do more to disgust with them than my protest. Armine does not by any means die so you need not be afraid for him.[[footnote:2]] I have him now in the midst of a naughty fit, quite sufficient to save his life.

Thank you so much for your kind thoughts about the game

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
