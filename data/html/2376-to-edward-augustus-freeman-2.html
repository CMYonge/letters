Dear [[person:523]Mr Freeman] [[footnote:1]], I waited to thank you for your kind note to see if there was any chance of so getting rid of the old edition of [[cmybook:48]History of Christian Names], as to make it reasonable to put an abridgment much corrected in hand - But unluckily the book was printed in the anarchy of the [[other:152]J W Parker] establishment and the edition was too large, so that I do not know how to get through the dregs of it, and [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] does not give much encouragement to selling it off en masse.

It is a pity for I would like to improve it myself, but on the other hand, though some part - i.e. the Teutonic, I could mend, all I know about the Keltic is that it is all wrong! I long to sweep away the letters of the alphabet which I blundered out without having ever heard of Grimm’s law.

I have always wished to tell you that it was on the authority of [[person:1071]Professor Münch] of Christiania that I made those Frank names like Hadweh &c come from wehe - consecration, instead of wig - man. [[footnote:2]] He died just as the book came out, and I have never been able to find out if this was a crotchet of his own or not.

Two or three people busy in education want the old [[otherbook:790]Stream of Time] renewed, corrected and modernized.[[footnote:3]] I believe it would be very desirable if only one could indicate the doubtfully a haze of some kind.[[footnote:4]]

Yours very truly C M Yonge
