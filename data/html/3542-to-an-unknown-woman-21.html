Madam I was in hopes of being able to tell you of such a training ship as you desire, knowing that there was one under the management in part of a [[person:2074]Mr Chambers], an excellent Churchman but I find that is a Reformatory Ship and that I suppose would hardly be what you wish, but if you would like further enquiries to be made, I could procure Mr Chambers address.[[footnote:1]]

Yours truly C M Yonge (Ed [[other:52]M P])
