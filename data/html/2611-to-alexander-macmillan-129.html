My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan]

What I have by me in the way of authorities are the [[otherbook:139]Universal History] [[otherbook:891]Lady Calcott’s] in English and in Spanish a life of the Cid - [[otherbook:892]Perez de Hyta] (whence Washington Irving took his material, but which is only romance) and old Madiema - who is alarming - as he hardly ever has a paragraph, and when he does put a date, does not use figures![[footnote:2]]

I have picked out a useful list of books mostly French from the [[other:29]London Library] and if they will take the trouble to find them for me, I shall do well but I sometimes think they don’t choose to hunt up an insignificant book out of date for a person in the country. If I do not get the important ones, I will write to you about them

Yes, [[otherbook:894]Longfellow] does fall later than my period for I must end with Boabdil’s last look at Spain

I must try to work history and romance side by side - and I think I can keep within the bounds

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
