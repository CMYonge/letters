My dear [[person:423]Mme de Witt]

You are quite welcome to make any use you please of my [[cmybook:87]Scripture Readings]. I have just completed them, though the last volume is not quite out. You must miss the spring after this severe winter Your [[otherbook:916]En Quarantaine] seems to have been a prediction of your winter of nursing. I am glad all is now well. We have all been having very severe colds, but are quite repaired now except my brother who has been much pulled down. I am expecting [[person:351]Mary Coleridge] here tomorrow. I made her a visit in the autumn in a very pretty house at Ottery, which [[person:343]her brother] has put into repair for her

yours affectionately C M Yonge
