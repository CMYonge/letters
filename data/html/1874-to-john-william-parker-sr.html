My dear [[person:1144]Sir],

I did not mean to publish the two volumes of the [[cmybook:48]History of Christian Names] separately.[[footnote:1]] The second is in a state of forwardness, but I thought I had explained when I saw you that I thought it would save time to have the 1st in the press while I am going over the 2nd again.

I imagine 2000 copies will be sufficient for a first edition.

My direction till the 20th will be the [[person:244]Revd W Butler]’s

Vicarage

Wantage

Yours faithfully

C.M. Yonge
