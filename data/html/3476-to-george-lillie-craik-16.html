Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] I see you are advertising [[otherbook:780]Highways and Byways in Spain]. I should be much obliged if I might have a copy as my [[person:1604]Sister in law] and her daughters are at Algeciras, where the [[person:386]husband] of [[person:388]one] is Vice Consul. I am expecting the [[person:1613]elder one] to return before the end of the month, and the book would be much enjoyed by us together.

I am glad [[cmybook:251]Modern Broods] is doing well. It is more than I expected, for I do not think I can attempt any more of the kind after a long spell of success.

yours sincerely C M Yonge
