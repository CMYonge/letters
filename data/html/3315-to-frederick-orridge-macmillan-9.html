Dear [[person:942]Mr Macmillan] Some years ago [[person:379]Mr Craik] wrote to me that the sale of the [[cmybook:73]Cameos of English History] had so fallen off that it was not worth while to give me £100 for the copyright of the volume after it had appeared in the[[other:52] Monthly Packet], and I therefore discontinued thinking of the separate publication. I think the volumes ended with the [[cmybook:213]Restoration]

Since that, and especially lately, so many people have asked for the volumes to be continued that I venture to ask whether you would go on with them, giving me a smaller sum for the copyright

Yours truly C M Yonge
