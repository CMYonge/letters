My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], Here is the [[cmybook:59]notice] of [[otherbook:406]Cawnpore][[footnote:1]] but I shall not be surprised if you do not think it the thing. The whole was too overpowering to say many words about and I have run into mere narrative more than I meant at first from the very force of the events. I am obliged to send it without any other eye over it, as my [[person:1606]mother] cannot bear to think of the horrors. I wish every school boy would read the book these holidays, it would pale the excitement of Captain [[person:1944]Mayne Reid]. I have sent a much shorter [[cmybook:60]notice] of the book to the [[other:76]Literary Churchman] in pursuance of a [[cmybook:307]paper] on boy literature I have done before.

I have received the volumes of Kitto’s [[otherbook:401]Pictorial History] and much enjoy reading them. Thanks for the loan. I have sent the proof to be looked over by a clerical friend whose criticisms have always helped me much.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
