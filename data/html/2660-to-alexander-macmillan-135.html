It is rather difficult to arrange the order of these books.[[footnote:2]] [[cmybook:19]Heartsease ]was out before [[cmybook:24]the Daisy Chain] indeed those four that I numbered first were meant to answer to the four Seasons, and ought to go together[[footnote:3]]

In point of date, the Daisy Chain and [[cmybook:38]the Young Stepmother ]are the next, but the Daisy Chain [[cmybook:53]Trial] and [[cmybook:109]Pillars] ought to come together

What is to be done about the lesser historical ones, [[cmybook:61]the Prince & Page], and [[cmybook:20]Little Duke] are children’s books but [[cmybook:63]the Danvers’ Papers] are not, Would it not be well to pair some lesser ones together?

What is the size and price to be

I have classed historical ones according to date except [[cmybook:110]Lady Hester] which would bind with Danvers Papers

Please have a copy of the Daisy Chain & Trial sent to [[person:2319]Mrs Beresford Earle] the Soke, Winchester Also one of [[cmybook:120]the Three Brides] sent to [[person:873]Miss Lewis] - 710 Pine St Philadelphia US.

Yours truly C M Yonge
