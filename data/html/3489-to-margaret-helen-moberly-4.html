My dear [[person:83]Maggie] I wish you many happy returns of the day, and I wish I was sending you a [[cmybook:24]Daisy Chain] with it, but I did not write a letter in time to get it from London, so I am afraid you must wait for it till the next day, but it will be something to look forward to then, and I will write your name in it, if you like on Friday evening

your affectionate godmother C M Yonge
