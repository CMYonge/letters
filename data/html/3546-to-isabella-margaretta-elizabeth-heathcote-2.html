My dear [[person:1690]Bella]

I think the day had better be Friday, as [[person:1462]Gertrude] has been rather out of order these last few days, but is picking up again. It will be a great day of church decoration, but I shall be sure to have done by 4 o’clock, and I shall be very glad to see you and [[person:2395]Miss Fawcett].[[footnote:1]]

Yours affectionately C M Yonge
