Dear [[person:1232]Sir][[footnote:1]], I hope I am not taking a great liberty in addressing you, but I am doing so in the hope that you will excuse me in the circumstances.

These will be best explained if you would glance at the paper entitled ‘[[otherbook:263]The Little Patient]’ in the October number of the [[other:52]Monthly Packet]

The little girl there faithfully described is again in the hospital for Children with another attack of disease of the heart, now so far mitigated that she may live some time longer with care. She must be returned to her wretched home in a week’s time unless some asylum be found for her, and that in London, as her parents and her medical attendants both object to her being removed into the country. [[person:1807]Sister Ellen] of [[other:20]Clewer],[[footnote:2]] suggested that it was possible that the Home attached to [[other:43]All Saints] might afford a refuge for her, and in this hope I have ventured to apply directly to you upon the subject. If any small sum should be required towards her maintenance, I am encouraged to think that something could be raised towards it among those interested in the case

Yours respectfully Charlotte M Yonge
