'Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik], Thank you for the ₤25. I should much like to have [[person:423]Mme de Witt] as a contributor to the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] and am much obliged to [[person:378]Mrs Craik] for mentioning it – I could easily get the translations done , indeed I am beset with people who want to translate and could clearly do it well. The only doubt is in the matter of payment, which we cannot make so good as you do, but I will write to her about it[[footnote:2]] Yours sincerely CM Yonge
