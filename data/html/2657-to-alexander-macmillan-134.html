Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan]

I am today sending [[other:173]Clay] the last copy for the [[cmybook:129]Scripture Readings], I suppose we may consider now whether the five volumes can be made more compact and profitable, and I hope Clay will finish off the present one quickly.

Thanks for sending all these notices of the [[cmybook:353]Primer].[[footnote:1]] It is odd I thought I had written up to MacMahon’s election, but perhaps [[person:582]Mr Green] thought the siege made a better conclusion - and that I could not grasp it.

I hope soon to send the Spanish corrections[[footnote:2]] As a matter of curiosity is [[person:523]Mr Freeman] still mending up [[cmybook:99]that other unfortunate history], I have kept all the proofs of mine to compare[?].[[footnote:3]]

With all good new year’s wishes Yours sincerely C M Yonge

Thanks for the primers. [[otherbook:914]English composition] is very good. Though I wonder at the condemnation of pell mell and topsy turvey.
