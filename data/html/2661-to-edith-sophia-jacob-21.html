My dear [[person:749]Edith]

Your [[other:47]scheme of Intercession] is grand. It is rather like some of the Gilds mentioned in the [[otherbook:915]SPCK History of the Diocese of York], which seem to have devoted themselves to special objects. One was in honor of the Lord\'s Prayer, and they even made a mystery play of it which they acted once a year.

The care they took of the poor made it a cruel thing to break them up.

your affectionate C M Yonge
