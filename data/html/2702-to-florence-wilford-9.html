My dear [[person:1520]Florence]

You have a birthday so seldom that one recollects it, so pray accept my best wishes and this tiny book.[[footnote:1]] I am afraid there is a little black speck on its cover, but do not look to reject it on that account I hope you received the second volume of [[otherbook:928]Thrupp on the Psalms]. I do not like all of him, though I have learnt a great deal from him, he does not tread in the beaten lines.

I hope this warmer weather is refreshing you, it has brought up a great crowd of snow drops, and some primroses, but the Lent Lilies lag behind Lent.

With love to [[person:1519]your sister], and kind regards to [[person:1521]the General]

Yours affectionately C M Yonge
