Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

[[other:137]Mudie]’s books have come at last, after spending ten days on the road. [[otherbook:1002]Greville] is there. And the [[otherbook:1009]Queen’s Journals].[[footnote:1]]

Shall I return you the latter from the [[other:29]London Library].

I ought to have the Proofs of [[cmybook:185]the Quest of Ulysses]. I know I put Lord Balmerino into the wrong rebellion which is as bad as putting Dunfermline on the wrong side of Scotland as I did in [[cmybook:157]Unknown to History].

So much for not verifying!

There is a horrible misprint in the [[cmybook:171]Armourers’ prentices]. Alderman Ityllio.

It is in the two complete editions, but not in in [sic] the [[other:68]Magazine]. I believe it arose from my trying to change his name in the proof, and not having a revise. I meant it for Hillyard, but I have never yet discovered how to make an H that the printers do not take for It or a z that they do not make into a y[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge

After all Mudie has only sent the second Highland Journal so I must keep the first
