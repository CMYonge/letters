My dear [[person:772]Mrs Johns], Thank you for your kind invitation but I never know how to get out early enough for a one o’clock luncheon. [[person:1613]Helen]’s lessons last me till half past eleven, and if I do not work from that time till two, I can get nothing done, and as I am going from home the week after next, I am more hurried than is convenient, so that I cannot well spare the morning hours. I will bring in some spare [[other:52]M P]s with the story and hope to appear between half past three and four on Saturday.[[footnote:2]]

If it were not for more than usual work, I would gladly come to your early dinner.

I will send the papers to [[person:1301]Miss Sewell]. With many thanks Yours sincerely C M Yonge
