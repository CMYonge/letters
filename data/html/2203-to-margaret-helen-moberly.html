My dear [[person:83]Maggie], Your book marker [is] a great beauty, and I thank you very much for it, particularly admiring the beautiful little shy bee. [[person:1606]Mamma] thanks [[person:1025]Alice] for her [note] and good news of you all. I hope we shall see you before long I am much better, and feel quite proud that I am sitting up to my work this morning while [[person:1520]Miss Wilford] is lying on her back. It is ten weeks since her strain, and it still gives her pain, so that she cannot walk many steps nor sit up a few minutes, and when I fetched her on Friday [[person:88]Mrs Bacon] took away her bonnet that she might not go to church on Sunday. [[person:1244]Miss Roberts] looked very weak and ill while she was here, and [[person:1544]Mr Wither] might well say my friends are all screws. Is it not a good thing that [[person:1996]Harry Young] is taken into Mr [[person:1503]Latham Wickham]’s school on his lower terms instead of the [[person:1995]poor little Humbert][.][[footnote:1]] Hursley disagrees so much with [[person:451]Mr Dundas] that [[person:930]Miss Mackenzie] went off to Bournemouth after a week, and says Bournemouth air is not like Hursley rain, so I suppose it is something like that at Brighstone[[footnote:2]] [[person:462]Mrs] and [[person:464]Miss Dyson] were obliged to put off their visit to us, but we hope now to have them the first week in September. I think one of the Simeons will come to us the week after next.[[footnote:3]]

your affectionate godmother C M Yonge
