Dear Madam

I wish I could promise a ready admittance to My English Servant into the [[other:52]Monthly Packet], but I am afraid that sending it to me would only involve an almost endless waiting. Some stories I have had by me for two years, and I think yours deserves a better fate, I wish the [[other:40]SPCK] would take it, I can’t understand their principles, for I am sure there are two or three stories in their last set of no use to anybody!

yours truly C M Yonge
