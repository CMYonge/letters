Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

There is [[cmybook:134]a new volume of Cameos] just ready to come out, ending at Queen Elizabeth’s death. I suppose it must be called ‘Wars of the Reformation’ I do not like the term Wars of Religion.

I am also finishing off [[cmybook:170]Stray Pearls], which will be completed in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] in June, in 32 or 33 chapters - so I suppose it may come out about that time.

It is a sort of continuation of [[cmybook:69]the Chaplet of Pearls] in the time of the Fronde, when the cavaliers were in exile.

Will you kindly desire a copy of [[cmybook:131]Magnum Bonum] and of the [[cmybook:58]Clever Woman] to be sent me

Yours very truly C M Yonge
