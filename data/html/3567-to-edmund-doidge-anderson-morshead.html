My dear [[person:42]Edmund], Here is an entreaty I have had from [[person:28]Mary]. I can’t worry the old [[person:1304]Warden], and I don’t know what niece is with him.[[footnote:2]] I don’t think there is much to see extra at New College, but would you send her a note to make it possible. I think one sees the Hall naturally, and there was not much in the Library to see, but I suppose William of Wykeham’s staff is only shewn by special favour

Your affec C M Yonge
