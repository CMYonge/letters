My dear [[person:121]Mrs Lennard][[footnote:1]] It shews indeed that it is long since we heard of one another, that you did not know that my dear [[person:1462]Gertrude Walter] died, on the 20th of last May, after a winter and spring of much increased suffering, nursed by her sisters. You will be interested to hear that she left her stamps to be sold for the [[other:30]Melanesian Missio]n. Of course they did not bring in what she expected, but they did come to £106, in which we rejoiced

I gave [[person:512]Miss Finlaison ]your message, and probably she will write to you. She is just collecting her flock again.

I am very sorry to hear of your troubles but I am afraid I can see nothing to suggest to you, and I do not know of anyone needing to reside in Germany

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
