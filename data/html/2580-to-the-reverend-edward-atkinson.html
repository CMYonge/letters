My dear [[person:2309]Sir][[footnote:2]]

Your were so kind to me when I was at Cambridge that I am venturing to ask you to give your attention to the letters and testimonials you are about to receive on behalf of [[person:1624]my brother], who is very anxious to obtain the Secretaryship of the Governing Body of [[other:50]Winchester]. I think that from his experience in many ways, he is very well fitted for it, and that as an Eton and Balliol man, who has always had a neighbour\'s knowledge of Winchester matters without being a Wykehamist, or committed to any line there observed, he would have many special advantages of usefulness.[[footnote:2]]

I hope therefore that you will give a favorable consideration to his papers when he sends them in

yours truly Charlotte M. Yonge
