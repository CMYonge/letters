Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] I shall be very happy to have the [[cmybook:20]Little Duke ]and [[cmybook:62]the Dove in the Eagle’s Nest ]manipulated for schools. I should think there would be little to do to the Little Duke as it was written for children. I should have thought the [[cmybook:23]Lances of Lynwood ]more suitable than the Dove as being on English history.

I know the Eversley country is full of beauty, I once drove through it. I believe there is rather a complication of trains for coming to Winchester or Shawford, our nearest station, but if you and [[person:378]Mrs Craik ]could come and lunch here it would be very pleasant I shall however be at [[person:1025]Miss Moberly]’s The Close Winchester all next week

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
