My dear [[person:1046]Mr Moor]

Thank you very much. There is one thing more that I want, when you come to the Confirmation, & that is the last [[other:41]SPG] report. Subscribing through [[person:1462]my friend] here, I don’t get it direct I have made a beginning but I go from home for nearly a week after the Confirmation I do not know what to do about the Chandlers Ford children. There really are not many - & when a child has everything right it would be hard to refuse it a trumpery little reward because it was not of the parish. Indeed I suspect coming to Otterbourne is sometimes made an excuse for not coming at all, it is much the way with borderers. One or two families came before there was a school and have kept on

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
