My dear [[person:1180]Augusta]

I don’t know how it is but there never seems to be room in the [[other:52]Packet]. I cannot get in my own [[cmybook:134]Cameos], nor finish up [[cmybook:120]the Three Brides] as I meant to have done by two chapters at a time. When I began the [[otherbook:867]York & L Rose] I thought both it and [[otherbook:859]Dt Cecil] would end at Midsummer, and now I find that they will last on into next year. Then [[other:173]the new printer] will not let me put things in type and keep them till I can find a good niche, but only prints just when will make one number so that I do not get my way. I hope as [[otherbook:868]the Winter Story] will finish I shall have a little more room after December, but to get in your paper has really been impossible hitherto. The [[cmybook:119]Beginnings of Church history] are the short chapters that I have had in the now collapsed [[other:81]Monthly Paper] finished up on to the present time, for there are so many Church histories stopping with the three first centuries. The [[cmybook:117]Stories] are only the old ones like the Xmas Mummers &c reprinted in one volume. [[cmybook:112]My Young Alcides] is a sort of Modern story built on the lines of the Labours of Hercules. I have had to finish it up to be a Christmas story and have been so hurried as to have no time for anything else in the way of writing for the last fortnight but I sent off his preface and title page today and must bring up arrears

Ones heart aches to think of Puslinch but I think those times of worry and unhappiness are not constant, and that this probably was the effect of [[person:1599]Duke] being obliged to go instead of choosing a good time as he did in the summer I think the brain always was an excitable one and that anxiety has grown on him with the inability to control it, but one certainly does dread extreme old age. I do not know what day [[person:512]Miss Finlaison]’s break up is to be. They are all in a state of excitement about a concert which is to come off this week in the girls’ school room, two days of it, between the choir,[[person:1604] Frances], [[person:784]Mrs Bateman] and the governesses. I wonder whether even to the musical the pleasure or the worry predominates, though to be sure the Managers and performers have all the worry to themselves. [[person:1462]Gertrude] is beginning the winter astonishingly well, and is taking quite a start of strength though I do not suppose she will go out again

I hope [[person:1184]John]’s cold is better and that it will not be the beginning of such a series as you had last winter

your most affectionate C M Yonge
