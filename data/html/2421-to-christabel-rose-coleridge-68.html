My dear [[person:329]Christabel] [[other:104]Goslings] do multiply overmuch, but I cannot refuse to make a two headed one, like [[person:1161]Double daisy] out of these two damsels of [[person:793]Miss Keary]’s about whom I send her letter.[[footnote:1]] Will you send them the Gosling list, and standing orders I am sending them the rules. Who asks questions next – none have come to me to choose from for December- and it is January that is the holiday.

I have given up the cats in grey. I find they come from Moliere and may have an objectionable meaning.[[footnote:2]]
