Dear [[person:1548]Mr Wodehouse]

I shall be delighted to see another story of [[person:569]Miss Gordon]’s.[[footnote:2]] I am in hopes that her [[otherbook:353]icelandic one] will appear in the [[other:52]Packet] in February or March, and indeed I was thinking of writing to ask where she was that I might send her the proofs, it is indeed a long time since we have met, and as my [[person:1624]brother] and [[person:1604]sister] have gone out and left us their cavalry, we are meditating driving to Easton some day, when we might bring home the MS. Would you or [[person:1547]Lady Elinor] be so kind as to let us know whether we should find you at home if we presented ourselves at luncheon the first fine day next week?

Yours very truly C M Yonge
