'My dear [[person:1297]Mrs Selwyn] I have been begged to find out whence came a beautiful prayer that was given to all your friends at your first start to N Z. It really seems like going into the last century, or the primitive Church, and I do not suppose there is anybody who could tell me now, not even [[person:1151]Fanny Patteson], so I am venturing to ask whether you can remember. The new [[person:85]Bishop of Osaka] (Japan) has had it printed on his farewell cards sent to all his friends. He was to land either last Monday or on Good Friday at Nagasaki, a considerable difference in speed since your time![[footnote:1]] I hope you are well. This warm winter has been very kind to many and we have hosts of primroses for the decorations to which I am just going yours affectionately C M Yonge
