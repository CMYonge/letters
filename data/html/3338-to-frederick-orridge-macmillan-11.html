Dear [[person:942]Mr Macmillan] I like the appearance of the proof copy of [[cmybook:243]the Ben Beriah] very much and perhaps it would be best to work out the edition. The doubt in my mind would be whether it will be favorably [sic] received as ranging with my tales and novels. I suppose there is no possibility of adding a preface now, I had not put one because it would not have suited in [[other:88]St Nicholas], and I thought to prepare it afterwards. I had also thought of asking if it could have a few landscape illustrations, the plain at the base of Mount Sinai especially but perhaps all this had better wait for consideration till we see how the first edition succeeds.

I think it will ultimately become a book for school rewards

But if you think it best to publish it as it is, I fully consent

Yours truly C M Yonge
