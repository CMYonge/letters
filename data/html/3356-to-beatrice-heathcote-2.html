My dear [[person:655]Bea], I think I might add these two bits from[[cmybook:218] ‘Old Times at Otterbourne’] as the thing turns out so short[[footnote:1]]

I heard a very odd thing from [[person:195]Anna Bramston]. She says she has seen a sword said to be old Oliver Cromwell’s in the possession of old [[person:2176]Mr Comely]. He said that at the sale in ‘the old home’ a blacksmith had obtained it, and it had descended to him. Could the old house be the Cromwell house? It was a curious sword,, sharpened on both sides towards the point, but all the Comelys are dead, and Anna does not know what became of it I think they must have been blacksmiths for the smithy as I first recollect it was in charge of a certain [[person:363]‘Betsey Comely’] who used to preface her speeches with ‘I am but a fay-male, sir’

Is the water found yet?

Your affectionate C M Yonge
