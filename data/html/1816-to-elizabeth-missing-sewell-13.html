'My dear [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] Many thanks for you kind & pleasant piece of intelligence. I shall certainly try to get a sight of the number of [[other:146]Blackwood], it is rather an antidote to the [[other:147]Morning post] which kindly says that no young people read my books, though their Mammas wish them to do so, because they are so unnatural.[[footnote:1]] A good journey to you, I hope you will discover the En[or u]ilian provinces, which was one of the first things that came into my head when the Yards told me of your journey.[[footnote:2]] I am so glad you are going, and what a curious state of things you are going to witness - it is well worth the journey. I do not think you have been ever told that your [[otherbook:296]Passing Thoughts] was one of the last books [[person:796]Miss Keble] enjoyed. Yours sincerely C M Yonge Some people have asked me if you wrote [[otherbook:297]‘My Life’]. You would have made a much less bitter and better balanced book, but this is a very clever one, though it might be wiser.
