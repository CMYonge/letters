I want to tell the [[other:47]SSW] of a lovely book that has been sent to [[person:1462]Gertrude] by another invalid.[[footnote:1]] It is called \'[[otherbook:1023]the Sermon in the Hospital]\', and can be had from Kegan Paul for 3d or 20s for 25 copies. yours affectionately C M Yonge
