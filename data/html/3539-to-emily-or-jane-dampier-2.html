My dear [[person:1345]Miss Dampier], I am sorry to do no more than leave the parcel but we have to be at home in time to take [[person:616]Mrs Halliday] to meet the train, so I can only deposit this at your door. I am much better thank you, and the nightingales have begun to sing in hearing from the house though not as near as they sometimes are. I enclose the 1/6 for Montrose.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
