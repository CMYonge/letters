Dear [[person:2234]Madam] I have found my [[otherbook:619]Puzzle for a Curious Girl] - 2d edition, 1805, given to my mother ‘Miss Fanny Bargus’ as her name is written.[[footnote:1]] The illustrations are cuts, very tightly dressed, the father in knee breeches, the servant opening the carriage door in a cocked hat. I was guilty of painting the first cuts very badly indeed

I have also ‘the Little Queen’ in the [[otherbook:620]Children’s Miscellany] where are also Little Jack and Philip Quarl.[[footnote:2]] I did not know that it had been attributed to Addison or an imitator.

[[otherbook:615]‘The History of a Great Many little boys and girls’ ]has been the favorite [sic] of 4 generations. Where Tommy Piper was put into a tub by Mr Makegood, and Miss Mary Anne Selfish placed in the pig stye- Miss Jenny Meek first appears in the [[otherbook:616]Village School], in my books. Was there a previous one? These I have in a reprint, and I know an original Mrs Teachem, belonging to my cousin.[[footnote:3]] I have also in a tiny gilt book, really old [[otherbook:618]Memoirs of a Pincushion] also [[otherbook:617]Memoirs of a Peg top] and [[otherbook:621]The Holiday Present] where the virtuous girl keeps her feet in the stocks when her mamma leaves the room, but the naughty one breaks her back and never grows any taller

[[otherbook:239]Mrs Davenport] I have seen, but it did not belong to me. I think the West Indian girls were always naughty, being spoilt by their slaves

yours sincerely C M Yonge
