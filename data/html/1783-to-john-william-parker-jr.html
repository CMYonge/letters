'My dear [[person:1143]Sir], Many thanks for the three critiques on [[otherbook:270]Mlle Mori ]which I hope to shew [[person:1244]Miss Roberts]. I think there is a good deal of one sidedness in the [[other:143]Literary Gazette]- as though written by a person who saw from only one point of view. I cannot judge whether the characters are too English but I fully believe Miss Roberts had authority for some of the things here represented as impossible and I wonder whether the writer has any grounds for the ‘simply not true’, that the hero and heroine were unlikely to receive alms because not Roman Catholics. yours with thanks CMYonge
