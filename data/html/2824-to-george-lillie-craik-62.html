Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

I am very happy to accept your offer of [[other:160]£25 ]for the Australian edition of [[cmybook:186]Chantry House].

I wish I could disinter A Citizen’s account of the Bristol riots of 1832.[[footnote:1]] It is referred to in the [[otherbook:995]Life of Bishop Gray] and said to be excellent; but I could not get it from the [[other:29]London Library], and I wish I could see it before the proofs of that Chapter of Chantry House are irrevocable.

Yours very truly C M Yonge
