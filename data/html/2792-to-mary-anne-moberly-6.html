'[To [[person:1019]Mary Ann Moberly]]

How little I thought when I met dear [[person:1154]Joanna Patteson] in your drawing-room that it was the last time I should see her! [[person:1151]Fanny Patteson] had come back, and is sure Joan knew and was thinking for others to the last. . . . I hope you are profiting by the splendid summer weather. I never knew a year of sweeter smells: the sheets of wild honeysuckle are quite amazing . . .
