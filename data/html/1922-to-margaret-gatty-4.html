My dear [[person:549]Mrs Gatty], In haste, with all the [[other:52]proofs] on my hands, and going out to tea - here are the sonnets[[footnote:1]] - I left out the first as being holy day, rather than Sunday, and belonging to next year’s series

Please let them go straight to [[other:108]Mozley]’s and I should be glad of more for February.

I hope you are not overwhelmed with [[otherbook:354]M. Macé] in his second volume,[[footnote:2]] I am sure the work must be hard. You are gathering your Christmas flock, I hope all well

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
