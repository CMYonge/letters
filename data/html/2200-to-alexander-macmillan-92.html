Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I am getting well again, and gone to work again.[[footnote:1]] Clement of Alexandria is the first authority for the robber story, but Eusebius takes it from him. I have a queer old [[otherbook:504]translation of Eusebius], Socrates and Co - from which I really did expand the account, and so I think I had better leave Eusebius, as it looks less as if it all came out of [[otherbook:389]Smith’s dictionary] as in fact much necessarily does.

I am glad you and [[person:980]Mr Masson] consent to the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet of Pearls] - it really was a material chaplet, and if it were coming at once in a book by itself, I should begin by quoting [[otherbook:219]Froissart]’s old French about it. I suppose by your account there is power in the [[other:193]Scotch girl] that wants toning down.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
