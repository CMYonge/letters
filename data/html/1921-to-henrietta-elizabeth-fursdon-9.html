My dear [[person:538]Henrietta ]

You have indeed had much sorrow during these two years, and you must be feeling it deeply, a kind aunt is such a treasure in ones life.[[footnote:1]] It has been a strange year of death – the same post brought me tidings of the death of a little [[person:170]godson] of mine who had long been hovering between life and death from the remains of scarlet fever, - his little [[person:169]sister] two years younger previously died. She was about six years old, and was born after her [[person:168]father]’s death. I don’t know whether you knew them, the Bogues of Tenbury.

I wonder what piece you are transcribing of [[otherbook:77]Marco Visconti], it is a very interesting but a very melancholy book.[[footnote:2]] I hear of many promising things for this [[other:89]Barnacle].

I am not sure of the Fursdon post town so I must send this to Dawlish.
