Madam, The school that I should think more likely to answer your purpose would be [[other:185]St Michael’s, Bognor], which is under the Provostship of [[person:1551]Mr Woodward] who has done so much for education – I cannot tell what the terms are but I know they are on a less expensive scale than those of schools equally good as education goes, and the doctrine is thoroughly high – The person to address for information is

The [[person:1973]Lady Warden] St Michael’s Bognor

[[person:900]Miss Lowden] – Frome Selwood and [[person:1145]Miss Parkinson] at Brighton have excellent schools, but the terms are high.

[[person:134]Miss Bell] [[other:186]Winnington Hall] Northwich

has a school on a very large scale, where young ladies receive an excellent education on moderate terms. In fact it is the locality of [[person:1268]Ruskin]’s [[otherbook:477]Ethics of the Dust] and I believe he is often there, though I do not know whether that is a recommendation. The Author of [[otherbook:478]Mabel’s music lessons] is musical governess there[[footnote:1]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
