My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith],

You must have begun to wonder if the Indian £10 was coming I am sure I did, but here it is at last, in good time I hope. It seems that [[person:482]Mrs Elphinstone] has been very ill, and that has prevented her from sending it sooner. I will write to her to acknowledge it as soon as I have heard from you. I am glad to see your three Ks in the [[other:62]Churchman].[[footnote:2]] I augur well of them, and the [[otherbook:275]Banks of the Thorne] are much liked. You will have some sheets of [[cmybook:43]Mrs Grant] very soon, but I had to abridge her a little there was so much more of her than of the others.
