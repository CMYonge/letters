'A BLIND GIRL Dear Madam,- Would any Associate or [[other:23]Member], accustomed to cheer the blind, kindly get into communication with a Member, aged fourteen, a cottage child who is not in health for a blind school. She can knit, and read a little by Moon’s method, and has a mother and sister who can read letters to her. Her address is [[person:917]Annie Nargate] [sic], Fergern Hill, Otterbourne, Winchester; but it might perhaps be best to write to her Associate, [[person:1962]Miss Jacson], Yew-tree Cottage, Otterbourne, Winchester. I remain, C. M. Yonge.
