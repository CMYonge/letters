Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] I see that your [[other:111]‘house’] appears in the list of subscribers to the [[other:16]Surgical Aid Society]. Do you think one or more letters could be spared to me? A little child was sent from the Society for protecting [[other:2]Waifs and Strays] with a lame foot paralysed after measles- to be boarded at a cottage here. She is now in the [[other:9]Orthopaedic hospital], but she requires a boot and irons, and the expense is heavy, so that we should be very thankful for any letters to the surgical aid society to help us with it

Yours truly C M Yonge
