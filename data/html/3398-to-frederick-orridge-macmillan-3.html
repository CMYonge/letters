Dear [[person:942]Mr Macmillan] I believe I wrote before and asked whether you thought it worth while to revive two or three books of mine that have gone out of print. I have been asked about them again, which leads me to write. ‘[[cmybook:13]The Two Guardians]’ and ‘[[cmybook:11]Henrietta’s Wish’] were published by [[other:112]Masters] and went through three editions at least, then I heard no more of them and his business has been given up. The copyright is mine.

They are both ‘stories for young people’ and were a good deal liked and honestly I think there is good work in them.

[[cmybook:10]Kenneth or the Rear Guard of the Grand Army] was published by [[person:1142]Parker of Oxford]. I believe he has let it drop, but there must be some merit in it since Mr E Arnold wrote to ask permission to make extracts in a [[otherbook:601]Reading book]

I thank you much for the beautiful series on Natural History. The birds delight me much

Yours very truly C M Yonge
