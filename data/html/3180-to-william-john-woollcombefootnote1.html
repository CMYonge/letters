Dear [[person:1560]Mr Woollcombe] I am just come home from Devon. I will send your letter to [[person:942]Macmillan], I think he will consider that admission to a school will make it quite worth while to cheapen it. I will ask one of the partners to write direct to you. I hope your brother [[person:1559]Robert ]has improved since I left Torquay, on Saturday, but the weather, keeping him indoors, is much against him

yours sincerely C M Yonge
