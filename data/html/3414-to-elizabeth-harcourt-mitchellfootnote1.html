'My dear [[person:1007]Mrs. Mitchell] Thank you for your [[otherbook:596]conversation].[[footnote:1]] It reminds me of what I tried to impress on some of the promoters of [[other:6]Lady Margaret Hall], that the Old Colleges began with training for the church the first object, and the secular work a sort of appendage, the Christian training running through. And I tried to shadow it out in that drawing of Geraldine\'s in the [[cmybook:109]Pillars of the House], of the Christian School of Athens. If you happen to have the book you will see the ideal. I think the Talbots would have been glad to have such a college[[footnote:2]], but times are too strong, and [[person:1566]Elizabeth Wordsworth] and [[person:1024]Anne Moberly] at [[other:14]St. Hugh\'s] do make their colleges in many respects training for the Church. Yours sincerely C. M. Yonge
