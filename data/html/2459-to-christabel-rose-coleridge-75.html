My dear [[person:329]Christabel] Here are the [[other:104]Answers] All to one question! I have read [[otherbook:829]Hanbury Mills] once to myself and am now reading it to poor [[person:1462]Gertrude] who enjoys it exceedingly – I like it very much indeed – there is so much character in the three degrees of refusement, and all the people seem to me very successful- Wilfred and Frank especially & Mrs Bridgewater and Joe. By the by in 108 & 9 you have twice made Prince Rupert speak, if one goes by the Antecedents & Nominatives - I think the hitch is why Scott never suspected Foster first or last till Joe discovered it. But the way Patience is raised is very touching and charming – I think it has more life in it than [[otherbook:788]Lady Betty] Pray send me more [[otherbook:437]Face]

your affectionate C M Yonge
