Dear [[person:706]Mrs Holbeach][[footnote:2]] I am much obliged for your book which I should have acknowledged sooner but I was just going from home

I chiefly took the account of the battle of Wakefield from Sir James Ramsays’[[otherbook:679] history], and as it was a hired book I cannot see whether any bit of old spelling of his could have misled me. I am afraid it is too late to get it altered in the second edition[[footnote:3]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
