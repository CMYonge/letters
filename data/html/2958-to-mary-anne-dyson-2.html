My dear [[person:464]Driver][[footnote:1]] I never expected [[cmybook:11]Henrietta][[footnote:2]] to produce such pretty fruits. I am delighted with it. I wish you would give Linny[[footnote:3]] [[otherbook:102]Sintram][[footnote:4]] to read, and see what she would make of it. Ours are hearing it with great satisfaction. The Tree was very successful; the gentlemen would come to look on, which made the children very silent, but they were exceedingly happy. [[person:1544]Mr. Wither] cut down the fruit, and there was much fun, They had calf manners exactly, merry and joyous, whispering to each other, and never pushing forward, altogether very nice. They had two pomegranates for tea, which Fanny told them came from Spain; then they looked at certain Indian birds of which they are never tired, and at my shells, some of which were so little that Lucy marvelled how a fish could be got into them. And the evening was filled up with dissected maps.
