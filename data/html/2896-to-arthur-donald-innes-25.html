Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes]

The copies to [[person:1466]Canon Warburton] were for presentation to the Central libraries of our [[other:48]Diocesan Society for Higher Religious Education].[[footnote:1]]

I imagine that half a sheet will be quite enough, giving me 4 pages besides those for index and title which I always grudge but I cannot tell till I get the list of make up.

I see Chapman and Hall’s M S will not do. I much dislike stories in dialect and indeed I think everyone does except the author, and philologists. What shall I do with it?

I like [[otherbook:1072]Pen] much better than [[otherbook:1070]Tipcat] or [[otherbook:1071]Our Little Ann].[[footnote:2]]

My own feeling is that we can hardly be accountable for respectable advertisements I see [[otherbook:1069]A South African farm] among them and I believe that is far worse than [[otherbook:631]Robert Elsmere][[footnote:3]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
