My dear [[person:329]Christabel], I should have said that I think you must specify your stones, there are such a frightful number- Pray have opal and turquoise- One for its weird history, the other for its nature- but exclude all the stupid ones, like garnets &c or we shall be swamped - I shall send the parcel on Monday - Meantime I send you the grand Goslings acceptance[[footnote:2]] - I think [[person:1160]Fernseed] must be her name - [[person:455]Ladyfern] & [[person:896]Magpie] keep on after all. This Fernseed is to be directed to as

Miss Peard Sparnow Torquay

your affectionate Mother Goose
