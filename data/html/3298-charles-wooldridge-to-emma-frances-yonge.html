My dear [[person:1604]Madam] I sent you the Policy on Saturday & I hope by this time you have received it The Policy now is transferred to Miss Yonge she being entitled to it having paid the £1000 & I believe all the interest & premiums, however as she wished me to send it to you I did so. I understood from Miss Yonge that she had certain intentions with regard to the Policy, but of course I am not at liberty to mention them without her consent

I am yours faithy [[person:2094]Chas Wooldridge]

Mrs. Yonge
