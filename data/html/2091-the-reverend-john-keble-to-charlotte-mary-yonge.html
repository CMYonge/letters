My very dear Child, This comes first to say that, to my very great relief, [[person:798]I] found this morning the two letters in a drawer in which I myself had specially lodged them for safe keeping, and herewith they come, saving something to myself which [[person:1155]he] would not perhaps like to have shewn.[[footnote:1]] How precious it all is, and what thoughts it brings over one (among others) of one’s own ways. . . . We wish your dear [[person:1606]mother], and the two households with their belongings, a happy new year and many of them.

Yours very affly. J. K.
