My dear [[person:113]Lizzie]-

. . . It is of no use to debate about [[person:1653]W. E. G.] You know even dear [[person:464]M. A.] and I had to avoid the subject, so I am not likely to be more convinced now of anything but that he deceives himself most of all, and takes love of power and popularity and hatred of Conservatives for love of right.

I have the outline of a story for the Xmas number (begun before your letter about High Arts, etc.) about a girl who abandons her mother to study it. I don\'t know if it will come to good, for I am slipping behind the modern world.-

Your affectionate C. M. Yonge
