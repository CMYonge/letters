Alice Bean Gray Miles Hoskins boy May One other whom I cannot remember.

Now can you tell me what you would like us to do on Christmas day-? Our parties are so many that I want you to say candidly whether it would be too much for you for us to come down, the 3 of us to midday dinner or whether we had not better eat it at home and come down directly after- What do you really think and feel about it?
