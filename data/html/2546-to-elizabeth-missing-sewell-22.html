My dear [[person:1301]Miss Sewell],

There is only one chapter of [[cmybook:45]Heartsease a conversation]. It was privately printed twice, and now people are always asking for it, so I am going to put it into the June [[other:52]Monthly Packet] though I do not think it is at all worth all the curiosity about it[[footnote:1]]

The publisher sent me [[person:1110]Miss Owen]’s book just in time for me to answer a person who wrote to enquire whether the chapters were separately published. I will certainly mention it

I am afraid I have no autograph of [[person:1155]bishop Patteson]s that I could cut off, his letters all being closely written there is no cutting one off, and as our correspondence did not begin till he was in New Zealand I have no notes, so that I am unable to find one for you

With my kind regards to [[person:1305]Miss Janetta]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
