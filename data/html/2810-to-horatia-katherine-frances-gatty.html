My dear [[person:469]Miss Gatty]

‘[[otherbook:987]A bit of Green]’ was only in the number preceding ‘[[otherbook:988]the Blackbird’s Nest]’- July 1861.[[footnote:1]] I had been on the like quest for the sake of the few words I have said in a conversation on books in the August number, I should have done so in the July one only that it was another persons conversation into which [it] would not fit

I did not write to you for what can I say but how grieved I am, as if a bright light had been taken away, or more truly a lamp become a star, as in that poem of BM’s.

Almost every letter I get has some note of mourning for [[person:494]one ]who had become dear to so many

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
