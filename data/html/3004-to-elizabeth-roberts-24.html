My dear [[person:1243]Madam], The wreaths for these autumn months have been so much smaller that I am sorry to say that there is [[other:102]only 7/6] to send you for this quarter, and here are P.O. stamps to that amount. I have not yet heard what we shall be able to do next year. I think that \'[[otherbook:234]the Lesser Holydays]’ is the name that best approves itself to me, what do you think of it. I know the poem of [[person:1729]Mary Howitt]’s you mention, but we have it not in the house nor do I know where I read it. If ever I come on it I will send it to you.

With many thanks for your [[otherbook:225]Garland], which has been one of the choicest possessions of this years [[other:52]Packet]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
