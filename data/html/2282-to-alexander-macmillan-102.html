Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] The lack of a tiger is serious. I mourned it as I read, and yet I thought it shewed some strength of mind to have avoided any ordinary ingredients of excitement. And when we were reading many books aloud, I certainly found [[other:202]this] answer better than many with a more direct selling element did, and the freshness of descriptions to me compensated for want of incident. Will you kindly direct it to [[person:1437]Mrs Valentine], care of [[person:1433]Mr Warne], and send it in. I think that they are beginning a system of one volume tales into which it might fit.[[footnote:2]]

The other matter was a mistake of mine. I imagined that the lady wrote to me on behalf of a branch institution just set up at Grimsby instead of which it turned out that she was only staying there on a visit. It was one of the confusions I made during the last months of illness.

The right direction is

The [[person:2020]Sister in Charge] for the Grimsby connection. Kings College Hospital Portugal Street Strand

and if you will so send the unfortunate parcel, I should be much obliged.

I find it is a library for the lady nurses’ own reading that they want - not as I fancied only books for the patients’ use. So I think I had better send a copy of each of my tales, instead of only the three I mentioned before if you will kindly give orders accordingly.[[footnote:3]]

Having lost the first letter I will not lay the blame on it, but I certainly did not understand it. A much more practical one has now come

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
