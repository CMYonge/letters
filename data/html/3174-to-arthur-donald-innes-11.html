Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes], We shall have a good Christmas Number. All I have is excellent, and would nearly suffice

But will you consider whether it would not be better to change the printer of the [[other:52]Packet] - [[person:309]Clowes] has never been attentive since it was transferred to them. You know I have made continual complaints of inattention to directions, and [[person:329]Miss Coleridge] says the same. Promises of amendment have been made, but without effect, and since the dual editorship, the confusion has been greater[[footnote:1]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
