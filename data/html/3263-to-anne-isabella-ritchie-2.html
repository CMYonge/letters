Dear [[person:1240]Mrs Ritchie] How kind in you to write me such a pleasant letter. I like to know that the companions of my life have been beloved by other people. I had actually begun for my own pleasure another link in the Daisy Chain for the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] but it was decided that it would weary the public.[[footnote:1]]

I am much enjoying your [[otherbook:698]recollections]. I hope you have a great many more still to come.

I was sorry this morning to find that the so called [[person:802]‘Sara Tytler’] is in so much need of a pension. [[person:1652]Mr Besant] is sending round a petition for her.[[footnote:2]] It seems strange that she should never have been able to make more than a £162 a year, when everyone knew her name, and her books are always wholesome and often clever, though in awkward language.

But publishers are inscrutable! I have just seen a MSS full of ability, and by a practised hand rejected by the [[other:230]publisher] of [[otherbook:699]Pretty Miss Smith] where the uncle of an heiress tries to drive her out of her mind by letting owls loose in her bed room at night

However, I did not mean to write so much shop, only to say that if you are in these parts I should be delighted to see you and I am generally at home

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
