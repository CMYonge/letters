My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], Thanks, I should prefer having the [[other:160]monthly sum] in cheques here, but the £200 to be paid to [[other:169]Messrs Hoare]. I hope the proofs are coming though it is rather late if the [[cmybook:62]Dove] is to come out this [[other:78]month]. I am afraid there are some anachronisms in it, and I did not give it the looking over that I should have done if I had thought of not seeing it again.

Here are these two chapters of [[other:177]Bethlehem], I would have sent Abraham, but I could not get him finished today. His chapter will be more descriptive.

I think I must do the preparation through the Israelites first before that of the heathen world, because in point of time Israelite history comes before Gentile history. I thought of trying to give a highly drawn out picture of each stage of Jewish prophecy - the next after Abraham to be the death bed of Jacob.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
