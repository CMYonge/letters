My dear [[person:253]Hannah] Thank you for your kind note. It will be a great pleasure to have [[person:182]Alethea] here but she cannot come till October, as the curacy cannot be left and the house has to be put in order.[[footnote:2]] I suppose they will hardly be come by the time you come home. It is too dusty today for almost anything.

Yours affectionately C M Yonge
