My dear [[person:869]Aimée], Many thanks for the Major Correspondence.[[footnote:1]] The account of the battle of Newbury is curious and so is the account of the week’s pay of the officers and soldiers The trumpeters must have made a good thing of it. Pray thank the possessor very much from me.

The woods are a perfect feast of colour just now, almost as lovely as in primrose time

yours affectionately C M Yonge
