Dear [[person:942]Mr Macmillan] I have been so often asked for a cheaper edition of my novels that I am delighted to hear that you are beginning on one of them, and I hope it will answer so well that you may follow it up with others

I hope by the 1st of July to send you the [[cmybook:247]\'Parishes of John Keble’]. It is being looked over by the Heathcote family now, and I shall not have it back till the 18th when I hope to put the last touches. A great deal of curious history has come out about the various old houses and the book will be of more general interest than I thought at first. I send another instalment of the [[cmybook:249]Cameos] for the printers

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
