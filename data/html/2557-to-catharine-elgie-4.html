My dear [[person:475]Mrs Elgie]

Many thanks for this dear little note. I always did think your Girls most thoroughly good and conscientious, and I think their love of home is the best and happiest thing for the place and people. I wonder when those lawyers will finish off and let you take possession.[[footnote:1]] I hope it is all right about [[person:447]Helena Dugay]

your affectionate C M Yonge
