My dear [[person:486]Mrs England][[footnote:1]] A basket of what we can get shall be left at your door about two o’clock on Wednesday.

I have told the children, but we do not live in the same profusion as heretofore Squires have grown so much more particular about trespassing than they used to be in the good old [[person:669]Heathcote] days that we are cut off from many of our best copses![[footnote:2]]

yours sincerely C M Yonge
