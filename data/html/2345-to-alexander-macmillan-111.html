My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] I shall see [[person:887]Miss Johns] on Saturday and have a consultation with her.

I have been prevented from setting about the [[cmybook:83]Storehouse] by the almost sudden death - the first day of this month - of [[person:1582]one] of my dear cousins - the one on whom I relied for looking out the copies of the books that I was to have from [[other:123]Puslinch]. I have not been willing to trouble her [[person:1626]sister] to hunt them out - and I am afraid the [[otherbook:557]Elements of Morality] cannot be had anywhere else. (I do not mean the [[otherbook:556]Blossoms of Morality]). I will however ask for it in a day or two.

One or two more questions before I definitely arrange. Shall we have [[otherbook:555]Goody Two Shoes]. It is more of a classic than any other, but has been often reprinted - spoiling the ghost, and can it be made out if [[otherbook:549]Philip Quarl] is Defoe’s - I cannot find it in the list of his works.[[footnote:1]]

Also after how many years is it safe to take no notice of copy right. What is to be done about [[other:167]illustrations] and in borrowing the books that cannot be bought. What assurance can I give of their being returned uninjured or must I get them copies. [[person:379]Mr Craik] shewed me a copy of Blossoms of Morality with cuts by [[person:2050]Bewick]. I thought of taking a few of the stories, can the illustrations to them be copied.

I think I must ask to know all this before I can fairly arrange the list of books, but I am writing for some that I can borrow. The post has not yet brought [[person:2051]M. Huard]’s designs. I must write again when they come. I believe the [[cmybook:82]second part of Historical Selections] is almost ready

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
