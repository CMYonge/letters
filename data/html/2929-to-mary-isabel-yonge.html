My dear [[person:1628]Mary][[footnote:1]]

In the autumn a Mr Yonge in America, an Engineer chose to send me the photograph of his little daughter whom he had named Charlotte and he sent a description of his arms, wanting to know whether he could be of the same family.[[footnote:2]] They were not the same as ours, nor as those to which we failed to shew our claim but [[person:1624]Julian] thought they were the same as yours. Now, American as he is, he has written the long letter which I send in case your husband can gratify him with an answer tracing his Philip and Mary. I am sure he would instantly claim you, if he knew that you had a Philip.

I hope you are quite well. The measles have started in the parish here most inopportunely just before the Government Inspection We rather expect that [[person:388]Joanna] will have them, as her great playfellow is in the midst

your affectionate cousin C M Yonge
