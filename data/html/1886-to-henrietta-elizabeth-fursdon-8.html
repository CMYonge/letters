My dear [[person:538]Irene]

Many thanks for your sister’s pretty little poem. I daresay it took great effect at your tea drinking. How much more beautiful [[person:1875]Aytoun]’s is than [[person:1874]Tennyson]’s and what a glorious day that was![[footnote:1]] I could see the bridal train rush by on the LSW, and hear the guns fired at Southampton. [[person:282]Our squire] gave beef and beer to each family at home, and there was a grand bonfire on the hill

Thank you so much about the answers to the Sunday questions

your affectionate

Mother Goose
