My dear [[person:1587]Charlotte] How shall I say how I grieve for you in this fresh sorrow so doubled as it is by all you have to care for, the poor little [[person:1364]Cordelia] I can only say. May comfort come to you under this fresh shadow which God has thought fit to send to you and which must thus have infinite blessings within it[[footnote:1]]

your loving C M Yonge
