'My dear [[person:2120]Miss Wilcox][[footnote:1]] Fisher Unwin has sent me this letter asking me to forward it, and as you are the only person I know likely to be able to do so, I send it on to you, taking the opportunity at the same time of returning your book which has been most useful to me, I kept it so long because I found I had to lengthen out [[cmybook:228]Grisly Grisell] and I wanted to refer to it Yours sincerely C M Yonge
