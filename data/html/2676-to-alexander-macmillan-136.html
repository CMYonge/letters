'My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] I am here for a day or two, and I hardly think there is any need to take up your time by calling, but I am going to beg for a copy of Dean Church, [[otherbook:918]Spenser], and of Professor Sharpes’ [[otherbook:919]Burns].[[footnote:2]] I have never thanked for the English history lectures, of which most of the substance is excellent, but there are a few slips of detail in names &c[[footnote:3]] Yours truly C M Yonge
