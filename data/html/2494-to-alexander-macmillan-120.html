Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], This is all very satisfactory, thank you much. One thing more. Should not there be a facsimile of the autograph[[footnote:1]] - I should like a page of that exquisitely neat distinct writing which never altered from the time it formed itself at Eton - and which spreads into sheets upon sheets of mine and yet I never stumble at a word.

I must put in a page with a list of the islands arranged in their groups for reference. I can make one from the Reports. Most of the islands have two names native and discoverer’s, and a good many three native, English and Spanish - and as everybody is always varying the spelling of the native names one gets distracted

But I will make a list and get the [[person:1295]Bishop of Lichfield] to correct it

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
