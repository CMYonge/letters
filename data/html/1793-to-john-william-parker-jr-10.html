My dear [[person:1143]Sir], I have authorized [[person:1396]M. Tauchnitz] to republish “[[cmybook:20]The Little Duke],” and Mr [[person:1964]Sydney Williams] tells me that he is about to apply to you for a cast of the frontispiece - I am afraid however that the lithographs can be no longer renewed, and I must reply to him that only the vignette of the little page is still to be had. I believe [[person:159]Mrs Blackburn] had the stones broken up after the 2nd or 3rd edition.[[footnote:1]]

From what I hear, I cannot but hope that [[otherbook:270]Mlle Mori] is in a more prosperous state than when last you wrote, the [[other:99]Saturday Review] was so favorable[[footnote:2]] [sic], and after that and the [[other:75]Guardian], the private opinions I have heard certainly became much more laudatory - I believe [[person:1244]Miss Roberts] is just now in England, and I cannot but hope she will find her book a greater success than seemed at one time likely.

I hope by Christmas to have a [[cmybook:38]story of my own] ready for separate publication - now coming out in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet]

Yours faithfully C M Yonge
