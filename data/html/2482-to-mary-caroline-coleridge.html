My dear [[person:348]Mrs Coleridge] Alas, I have had so much Tyrol in the Monthly Packet as I am writing to tell [[person:1893]Mrs Prichard] I find no series of travels ever answers, people do not keep up their interest - though single papers succeed well.

I hope [[cmybook:102]Bishop Patteson’s life] will be out in a few week’s time. How soon do you return to Mapledurham for I should like my [[person:332]‘Cousin Edward’] as he insists on my calling him to have one of the first copies sent out

yours sincerely C M Yonge
