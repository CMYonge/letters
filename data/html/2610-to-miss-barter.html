My dear [[person:116]Miss Barter],

I should think such a school as [[person:707]Mr Holland] proposes would be a very good and useful thing.[[footnote:2]] I do not see how I can help about it though I know so few people in London and I do not think there is anything for me to write about Mr Holland further than to wish him success, so I will ask you to do so for me.

I saw [[person:1025]Alice Moberly] last week, when [[person:1238]Mrs Ridding] drove her over. It is a long time since I have seen Salisbury not since the opening of your nave[[footnote:3]]

With my love to [[person:114]your sister]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
