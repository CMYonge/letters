Dear [[person:458]Miss Durton]

Your [[otherbook:1076]Good Friday] has been a long time on its way, owing to the misdirection, but I have it now, and I wish I could have got it into the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] for Easter, though even if it had come direct it would not have been in time for April.

I am very glad to hear more of [[otherbook:1075]Ottavia]’s family and will insert this when I can.[[footnote:1]] Perhaps another time you had better write direct to me. My paper is so thick that I will only put in this one half sheet

Yours truly C M Yonge
