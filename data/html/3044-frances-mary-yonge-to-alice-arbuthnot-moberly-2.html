My dear [[person:1025]Alice], [[person:1606]I] wrote instantly to thank [[person:1015]Dr. Moberly] for his good news, but the cart was missed on Sunday morning. Tell us if [[person:83]Margaret] has seen [[person:1026]the brother][[footnote:1]], and what she said of him, and tell us who the boy is like and whether he is large or small, dark or fair. Three days of well-doing make us think you will soon be ready for ‘[[cmybook:19]Heartsease]’; there will be plenty for you to begin upon. I am bent upon Charlotte seeing all she can of [[person:1295]Bishop Selwyn], and trust to Dr. Moberly to manage it for her, and must have her see [[person:1297]Mrs. Selwyn] somehow.
