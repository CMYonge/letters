'Dear Sir, Many thanks for your paper, which shall certainly go into the [[other:52]Monthly Packet]. I have been asked to call for some such spoon meat for [[cmybook:211]a packet of small books] for [[person:1433]Mr Warne], and as your Elsie’s Dream seems to me just the right thing in length and material for the purpose, I venture to ask if you would object to its so appearing instead of in the Monthly Packet.[[footnote:1]] Mr Warne promises £2 for each tale, and I think in this manner it will probably sooner appear. yours truly C M Yonge
