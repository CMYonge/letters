Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik],

I return the Agreement signed with many thanks. I shall be quite satisfied with the arrangement about [[other:160]the £1000 for the copyright] being paid half next March and half the March after.

One book you do not mention, the [[cmybook:48]Christian names], I should be very glad if the end of that could be so got rid of, that I could cut it down correct it, add some recent discoveries and something about surnames, and make a small saleable book of it.

Yours truly C M Yonge
