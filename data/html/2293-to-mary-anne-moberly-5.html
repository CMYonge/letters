Many many thanks for [[person:1019]your] most kind letter, and for telling me of the happy prospect before [[person:968]Dora][[footnote:1]] . . . I have asked [[person:1539]Mrs. Wilson] to send on to you a letter of [[person:1155]Bishop Patteson]’s, which I think you will feel refreshed by reading; please send it on to [[person:930]Miss Anne Mackenzie]. You are so very kind, and it would be very delightful to make one in the migration; but I have only seen [[person:464]Miss Dyson] for a day since her sorrow, and not at all since mine,[[footnote:2]] and the meeting at Testwood has become such an institution that I could not fail in it, so I must wait until you are at Brighstone again, and I have always had a wish to see that in spring. I have just returned from [[person:314]Lady Seaton]’s It is a [[other:126]house] of shadows and memories to me.
