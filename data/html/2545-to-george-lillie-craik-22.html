Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

Thanks for the [[other:160]estimate] of the copyrights, which is a good deal what I expected, as I thought they must be more valuable to me than to anyone else.

I cannot think what [[other:173]Clay] is about. A month ago he wrote to me in a great hurry for more copy of the [[cmybook:124]Cameos] saying you wanted the third volume finished, I sent him up at once all but the last two or three chapters which are now ready, and nothing more have I heard of them - no proofs nor any sign of his going on.

I do not think it was quite settled what I was to have for [[cmybook:112]My Young Alcides]. I should be glad to know.

Yours truly C M Yonge
