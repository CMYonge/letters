My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith],

I have at length obtained the account for the [[cmybook:43]Biographies] and am glad to be able to send you the enclosed as your share of the proceeds. Your [[otherbook:342]account] of the Procession was in time, and is going in this month with an [[otherbook:343]Illumination story] of the last time London was in a blaze.

After this week please to add Elderfield before Otterbourn [sic] in our address. It is a cottage in the village we are going to move to, as [[person:1624]my brother]’s family are beginning to outgrow our house

yours sincerely

C M Yonge
