My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith]

I did not write yesterday because the parcel was not come, but it is here all safe this morning though I can only testify to the beautiful clear MS as yet. It was rather over weight, which perhaps was the reason of the delay. I think it is possible this may account for the lamented loss of the [[otherbook:267]Wynnes], as the post will not convey things beyond a certain amount overweight. I once nearly lost all the [[other:52]Packet] proofs that way, the stamp having fallen off, but luckily there was a direction inside, so they wrote to me and I redeemed it. I have sent your first two chapters to Derby to be printed, though I don’t much think there will be space in April, as the [[otherbook:300]Easter story] must come in then It was that sent me by [[person:259]Mrs Carr] about whom you wrote to me, and whose [[person:1259]friend] seems to be a mine of Russian curiosities. Do you know anything about evening sewing classes - a lady wants counsel in the notices to correspondents in the M P upon setting one up, and as I know nothing myself, I shall be grateful for any experience you can give or direct me to. I sent [[otherbook:268]Aggesden] to a friend in America who likes it very much as also the Wynnes.

yours sincerely

C M Yonge
