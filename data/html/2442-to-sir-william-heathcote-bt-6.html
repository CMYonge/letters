My dear [[person:669]Sir William] Would you be so kind as to look at page 9 of the \'Gleanings\' at the beginning of the [[cmybook:88]Musings on the Christian Year], and tell me whether you have any recollection of telling [[person:798]Mr. Keble] anything about your opinion of [[person:1768]King Charles]\'s truth?[[footnote:1]]

There is a new edition called for, and [[person:464]Miss Dyson] wants me to take it out. Her letter coming while I was at Salisbury, I asked whether it was there thought that I ought, as it was not according to my impression lightly thrown out, and I put it in because I thought it disproved blind admiration, while showing the real tenderness.

[[person:1015]The Bishop] advised me to ask whether you remembered having said anything of the kind to him - if you did, to leave it; if not, to take it out. So I am acting by order in troubling you about it.

Your affectionate C. M. Yonge
