My dear [[person:2293]Misey] Here is a note from Mr Gurney.[[footnote:1]] I send it in case you should be changing your address, in which case it might not be so safe to let him write direct even under the care of the Angels.

I was at Rownhams yesterday, and was sorry to hear of the new sorrow.[[footnote:2]] [[person:1539]Maria] had had a cold, but was nearly well, [[person:1541]Mr Wilson] has left off crutch in the house

yours affectionately C M Yonge
