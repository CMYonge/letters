Dear Miss Yonge The parcel for [[person:873]Miss Lewis] reached Tooting safely, and will not in the least inconvenience [[person:939]me].[[footnote:1]] I will endeavour to deliver it early after my arrival.

I have read the [[other:193]Story of the Scotch girl]. It is very interesting - rather sensational in fact. [[person:2420]Robespierre], [[person:2421]Danton], [[person:2207]Napoleon], the Guillotine \'La Lanterne,\' a sprinkling of smugglers with a slight dash of wreckers, highly toned Irish, Scotch, French nationalities, a mad woman, suicide, a murder or two &c &c. make up a dish that certainly cannot be considered savourless. There is no great reach of thought, as [[person:1994]Mrs Lewes], or [[person:1965]Mrs Gaskell] have, nor anything like grace & delicacy of touch of [[person:1240]Miss Thackeray] but it looks to me vigorous firm drawing. I am getting a friend, a critic by profession to look at it. My reading was too much in novel reading vein to enable me to judge. Besides it was done between 11p.m. and 2 a.m. after a [illegible] of day

We shall communicate direct with her.

Yours very sincerely A. Macmillan
