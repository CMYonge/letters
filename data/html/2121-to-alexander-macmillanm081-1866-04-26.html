Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I enclose the list of the other copies of the [[cmybook:62]Dove] to be sent out besides my own dozen. Our day of departure is still fixed for Monday, but it is very likely to be put off till Friday. I suppose you naturally send copies of my books to the [[other:75]Guardian] but it is odd how it has ceased to notice them.

If [[person:793]Miss Keary]’s new [[otherbook:465]book] is finished at the same time, I should be glad to have a copy in the parcel.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
