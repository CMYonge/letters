Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes], I hope there are still some [[cmybook:18]Castle builders] left for a lady in Portugal wrote a few days ago to ask me if it was still to be had, and I answered that it was.[[footnote:1]] I suppose the Modern Landmarks are put out by later books.[[footnote:2]]

I am concerned about a long series of scientific papers on the physical structure of the Earth. I accepted them some time ago from [[person:552]Miss Gaye] and spoke of beginning in July 1890.[[footnote:3]] Then came this great effort to make the [[other:52]Packet ]more popular, and I do not know what to do about it. At that time people were crying out that there ought to be more improving papers and fewer stories! I do not know if it would be best to put in the papers not as a series but individually.

Yours truly C M Yonge
