'Dear [[person:1858]Miss Benson] I have sent up your joint story, for which I thank you much, to have the length of pages calculated. If it goes in, it will stand first before a sad and true [[otherbook:316]scene in a secularised hospital] at Paris by [[person:423]Madame de Witt] so as to shew the fruits of the cult of humanity without its Master. But I am not sure that I can get it in, and I also think that though it is rather long for the purpose, it might for its own sake and that of the idea be longer so that I shall not so much regret if it has to be sacrificed Yours truly C M Yonge
