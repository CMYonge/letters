My dear [[person:1517]Margaret]

So many thanks for sending me [[person:2346]Miss Smedley].[[footnote:1]] I had a good deal of her in the [[cmybook:150]Readers], but I shall borrow more now.[[footnote:2]] I wish her verses had been more known but I think they will grow into part of the common stock of knowledge of poetry in our youth. I sincerely rejoice at [[person:2348]your brother]’s appointment not only is it good for him, but what a load it takes from your shoulders which are used indeed to bearing family burthens.[[footnote:3]] I filled in the address of your letter and I hope [[otherbook:525]Canon Bright’s poems] arrived all right

[[person:301]Mrs Langton Clarke]’s death is a terrible blow[[footnote:4]] There was something so bright and charming about her, which left a long impression though I have seen so little of her. I should think it must greatly dim the brightness of Whitburn for years to come though there will be every help that kindness can give, but poor [[person:2347]Mr Clarke] with his weak health has so depended upon her that I cant [sic] think how he will every hold up his head

Yours affectionately C.M. Yonge
