Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] Happening to have an excess of M.S. luggage, I am rather glad to send off an instalment of “the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet of Pearls]” instead of packing it up. It threatens to be longer than the [[cmybook:62]Dove], and there is a good deal more still to be written, and probably rewritten.

I believe I omitted to say that it would be the most convenient way to me if you could [[other:160]pay] the £235, the balance of my account to my name at [[other:169]Messrs Hoare]’s.

Yours very truly C M Yonge
