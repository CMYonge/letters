Dear Madam I beg to acknowledge the receipt of your cheque.

I think that as your money is invested & producing interest you may as well wait until a short time before 1 April & then give your Bankers instructions to sell sufficient to produce £1000, so that it may be ready a day or two before the 1st[[footnote:1]]

I am yours faithy [[person:2094]Chas Wooldridge]

Miss Yonge
