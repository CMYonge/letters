My dear [[person:1143]Sir],

I am obliged for your letter received this morning, and will decide on bringing out [[cmybook:37]Hopes and Fears] as soon as possible.[[footnote:2]] I will send the chapters from the [[other:65]Constitutional Press] to be reprinted as soon as I have looked over them. I think that the first edition should certainly be in two volumes uniform with the others, but after the experience of [[cmybook:27]Dynevor Terrace],[[footnote:3]] I am not inclined to make the number of copies more than 3000 at the most.

Sheets should be sent to [[person:852]Mr Layton], Messrs [[other:142]Appleton]’s agent.[[footnote:4]] I had made arrangements with him before, but a periodical by some other firm has been bringing the story out, without any permission from me, and I rather rejoice in its being disappointed.

I will send the chapters early next week

Yours faithfully

C M Yonge
