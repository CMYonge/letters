[[person:1512]My Lord], Allow me to express my gratitude for the kindness of your invitation and of the arrangement for the journey. I must however beg to decline it until another year when I hope I may have the pleasure of making your [[person:1218]daughter]’s acquaintance under more cheerful circumstances.

I cannot at present feel inclined to enter on a large party, nor to leave my [[person:1606]mother], and I should be much obliged if you would allow me to consider the visit as deferred until some future time.[[footnote:2]]

With many thanks for your kindness Yours very respectfully Charlotte M Yonge
