Dear [[person:2234]Miss Walker] I read a review of [[otherbook:749]Miss Winslow’s diary], which must have given a fair idea of it. Most [[otherbook:362]Grandisonian] days those were! Did you see [[otherbook:750]‘Like Another Helen’] in which very good imitation letters are written by a young lady who actually was the one woman in the Black Hole. I thought it the best novel I had seen of the season. I wonder whether [[otherbook:751]Gaffer Two Shoes] was an imitation of [[otherbook:555]Goody]. My cousin has a copy where our prudent Great Grandmother had turned the Ghost in the Church into a dog making arrant nonsense Did you ever come across [[otherbook:764]‘Juliana or the Affectionate Geese.’] My [[person:1626]Mother] once read a part of it, but never saw it again. Another recollection of hers was of a novel where there was a family of nine Miss Simmondses, and at some county races, on the box of each carriage there was perched a Miss Simmonds I think we once met a person who likewise remembered the nine Miss Simmondses

I am afraid I must put you off with this short letter for it is a busy day

yours sincerely C M Yonge
