My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith], Thank you for your kind, warm hearted letter. I know you too know what a great grief is, and how close one clings to the last surviving parent,[[footnote:2]] and the sense of being still a child at home. May you long preserve that blessing.

I should be very lonely but that my [[person:1624]brother] and his [[person:1604]wife] are only a garden’s length from me, and most kind, and just at present I have a [[person:1582]cousin] with me, who has always been like a sister to me. I think I shall go into Devonshire with her, and then return to face the solitude

yours sincerely C M Yonge
