Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] We are rather disturbed at the delay in printing the [[cmybook:71]Historical Selections] as [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] cannot do any corrections after the end of June, and as she has all the books and has looked out all the selections I could not supply her place.

I suppose the [[other:173]printers] are postponing it to something more interesting, but if they cannot go on at once and finish at the end of June it would be convenient to know it.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
