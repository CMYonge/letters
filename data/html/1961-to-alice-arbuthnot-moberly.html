My dear [[person:1025]Alice], I was thinking of sending ‘The Mice at Play’ to [[person:83]Maggie], but somehow I felt that the note must be to one who could remember the old days, when the three bright faces[[footnote:2]] it brings to mind were with us. If you had been people who shrank from such recollections instead of cherishing them, I would of course never have disinterred this old affair, but I know you will like the recurrence to those merry old days and bright remembrances.
