My dear [[person:1626]Mary] You will like to know that the funeral is to be on Monday- her 48th birthday. By her own wish, [[person:1462]she] is to be carried into Church at 8 for the Celebration. Burial at 3. Hymns ‘The King of Love’ and ‘Peace, perfect peace’ her own choice.

[[person:1604]Frances] is here I wish you could pay me a real visit a little later when the house has been set to rights, but I think I shall have to go out while it is doing- only for a few days. But I would make all fit to what you could give me of time, and there will be plenty of room now.

I can’t feel yet what the difference will be

your loving C M Yonge
