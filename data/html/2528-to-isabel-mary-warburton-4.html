My dear [[person:1465]Mrs Warburton ]

A great many thanks for your kind [[otherbook:860]present].[[footnote:1]] It is my favourite period of history and I am sure [[person:1466]Mr Warburton ]will have treated it in a more kindred spirit than I think has been shewn in all the books of this series, some of which have disappointed me especially the Wars of the Roses, but I am sure I shall enjoy reading this.

Yours very sincerely C M Yonge
