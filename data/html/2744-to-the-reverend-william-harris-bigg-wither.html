My dear [[person:1544]Mr Wither]

The news of the day is that [[person:1744]Lady Chichester] is to be doubly Lady Chichester She marries [[person:2335]Sir Arthur Chichester], a widower over 60, whose estate joins on to Arlington[[footnote:1]]

He joins her at Arlington and his two unmarried daughters remain in possession. One of his daughters is [[person:2336]Mrs Chichester] so they have a small choice of names. [[person:277]Mrs Johnstone]’s two pretty little boys have begun to come to Church

I suppose [[person:1391]the archbishop] is really going now and I fully expect we shall lose [[person:213]our Harold] but it will be a good thing for the Church, and if they gave us [[person:926]yours], it would be a consolation[[footnote:2]]

[[person:661]Gilbert Heathcote] has come to the park. [[person:663]Ellie ]and [[person:1690]Bella] drove over the other day and told us about him.[[footnote:3]] His daughter [[person:2337]Gertrude] rises to the occasion, she has had sole charge of the poor little [[person:2339]baby] every since it was born, and brought it and the years old [[person:2338]Caroline] home without a nurse Twenty years between her and Caroline is quite enough to give authority. Gilbert is going to spend the rest of his leave of absence in Scotland. [[person:1883]Charles Mackarness] is an excellent person and has been so all his life he is a Wykehamist

Your affectionate C M Yonge
