My dear [[person:379]Mr Craik], I am dismayed at what I never found out till the [[other:99]Saturday Review] called my attention to it - ie that the [[cmybook:73]Cameo] about the conquest of Wales was left out.

Such a Cameo there was written, it is at Vol IV p. 6 of the [[other:52]Monthly Packet], but it must have been neglected when I was collecting the Cameos and the printing was done so very slowly that I lost the connection, and so did not miss it. I am very sorry for it, but it must go in whenever there is a new edition - or would it be possible to add it as a kind of appendix to the copies not yet bound. I suppose it would make about 8pp. The name is the Last & first Princes of Wales

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
