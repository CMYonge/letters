Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] My London plans have dwindled down to the smallest span - i.e. - to running up for a day with my [[person:1604]sister in law] on Wednesday the 25th. I hope to call at 16 Bedford St[[footnote:1]] somewhere between 12 and ½ past 4.

Would you send me a line to say whether I shall find you, and if one time is more convenient than another. Please write by Mondays post as I shall have to start before the letters came[sic] on Wednesday.

My present address is at [[person:462]Mrs Dyson]’s, Church-Crookham, Farnham.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge

I should prefer the earliest hour
