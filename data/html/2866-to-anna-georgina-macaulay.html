My dear [[person:2352]Nina][[footnote:1]]

[[person:1462]Gertrude] desires me to say that we have never had an American edition of [[cmybook:120]the Three Brides] but she has taken measures for getting a second hand one and it will be sent to you. All my stories in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] do get published again Gertrude is tolerably well just now
