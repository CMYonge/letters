My dear [[person:751]Gertrude] I hope your chapter[[footnote:1]] will be of a length that will fit happily into July. I will do my best for its coming in. Here is your [[otherbook:834]Twilight][[footnote:2]] which please look over, and it will get in when there is room. I do not think I am likely to come to London I have had two outings - to Chichester in Easter week and last week to the laying the 1st stone of [[other:5]Keble Chapel] and I don\'t think I shall make much of an expedition till the opening of [[person:1544]Mr Wither]\'s Church I wish Crawley was on this side Winchester[[footnote:3]]

yours very sincerely C M Yonge
