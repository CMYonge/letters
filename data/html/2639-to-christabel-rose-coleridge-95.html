My dear [[person:329]Christabel]

The [[other:52]Packet] will be happy to take [[otherbook:902]Cherry] cut down when [[otherbook:878]Heriot’s Choice] is over. I am afraid it will last a long time – for I never can succeed in getting in two chapters of Heriot’s Choice- Our pupil teachers have had a paper sent in from Salisbury training college for religious examination, saying 2nd class – [[person:1257]Alice Misselbrook] 8, [[person:2316]Harriet Godwin] 18, but whether this means no from the top or number of marks we in our inexperience do not know. The [[person:156]mistress] has been nursing her sick husband at Portsmouth for a fortnight, and they have kept school by themselves with good success - the children have all been wonderfully good. I believe the poor man is dying, but he is in Haslar Hospital and Mrs Bishop has come back to be sent for any time.[[footnote:1]] The [[person:513]new master] promises well. I hope your parish troubles will prove to be an eruption throwing off ill blood. How often a worry comes after one has had a special pleasure

your affectionate C M Yonge
