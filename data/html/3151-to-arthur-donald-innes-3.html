Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes] Thank you much for what was useless trouble. I thought Stanford was sure to have had such maps reasonably cheap for travellers.[[footnote:1]] There is a good little geological map of England in Letts’s [[otherbook:629]atlas], but the book is very badly bound, and that one map in my copy has some how been lost.

I think there are small editions of [[otherbook:628]Keith Johnstone]- but his publisher is [[other:214]Blackwood], I suppose he has an office in London but I do not know where. I am astonished at the expensiveness in these days of chromolithography But unless Blackwood is near at hand, or you have a catalogue which would shew if [[other:216]Cassell] has a cheap one, pray do not take any more trouble

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
