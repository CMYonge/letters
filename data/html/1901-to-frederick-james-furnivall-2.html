Dear [[person:531]Mr Furnivall]

Thanks for the list of needs of the [[other:151]letter B]. I had been making the like, but found the wants so constantly supplied from the overflowings of your pigeon holes that I grew lazy and desisted.

The printer at Winchester charges 7/6 per 1000 for the titles he prints for us on show paper,[[footnote:2]] and we have had 4000 - which have chiefly been used by [[person:1606]my mother], as I have generally taken mine from the books that did not furnish enough to make it worth while to print their titles.

I am desiring [[person:1144]Parker] to send a copy of the [[cmybook:48]Christian Names] to the [[other:97]Reader]. I think he never sends his books widely to reviews, though I never could make out what was the principle of selection as some certainly are sent out. If you are kind enough to review the book, I shall look out for the notice with great interest. I have only had two really useful and improving reviews of it, one in the [[other:99]Saturday], and one in the [[other:95]Nonconformist], a good many besides civil and useless. I hope nobody thinks I know all the languages there in referred to! In fact I fear there is plenty of internal evidence that I only got up what I wanted as I wanted it, and that not in the most thorough manner, and that I very much broke down in the correcting of the press.

Yours faithfully

C M Yonge
