Dear[[person:1643] Mr Holgate] This is really only an abridgement of the [[cmybook:66]article] in the Christian Remembrancer, except that I added the going to Winchester College chapel in 1854. I signed the paper because once or twice I found myself writing I to a personal reminiscence, but it can be altered if you wish it.[[footnote:1]] I think [[person:313]Lord Seaton]’s personal appearance is fully described in the paper. He was the most striking old man I ever saw. The best likeness of him is one of [[person:1731]Richmond]’s Crayons. I have an engraving of it, I think the original is at [[other:126]Beechwood], but I am not sure. There is another print that I do not like so much, and a full length picture which I believe was done for the United Services Club. There is a print from that but I do not like it - it is in Life Guards uniform and that being cavalry - is apt to look awkward on an infantry man. There is another small portrait of him at [[other:123]Puslinch], a much younger man - but I never realized it, never having known him till his return from Canada.

My [[person:1606]mother] was [[person:106]his step father]’s youngest daughter, my [[person:1631]father] was in his regiment, and was first cousin to his [[person:314]wife].

Anything about the pictures might best be learnt from [[person:318]the present Lord Seaton], Beechwood Plympton

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
