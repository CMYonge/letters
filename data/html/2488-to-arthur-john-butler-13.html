My dear [[person:238]Arthur] I think the redingote is wrong- but the geens and gaskins were worth having. Why should I not mean the Aryan classifications to be based on Grimm.[[footnote:1]] I meant it for a pleasing exercise but I fancy only our [[other:116]spiders] of a superior order will attack such a web!

[[otherbook:841]Adams] is my authority for durst being an old verb like burst, and is he not generally trustworthy? Certainly I never heard the infinitive. Do I know about your translation of Aristophanes? Did I miss it, for I am free to confess that [[person:512]my sub editor] looks at the Spiders’ webs and reports on them

What does the [[other:170]Athenaeum] say to this, which I received this morning?[[footnote:2]] [[other:241]‘The Queen’ ] declared that the only war in this reign I had mentioned was the Abyssinian, a flagrant instance of never looking at the book.[[footnote:3]]

Did you see the Aurora last night? I never saw a better.

yours very sincerely C M Yonge
