My dear [[person:1379]Miss Bourne]

Indeed I would gladly if I could, but months ago [[person:523]Professor Freeman ]asked me to come and see Hannah Mores villages, and hear his recollections of her, and the time has fixed itself for next week, and after that comes harvest - and dedication feasts - and Mission Guild meeting, and I am afraid I cannot do it though I am very sorry not to see you again this year before you are shut up for the winter. If you were well enough I might manage a day or two later. My [[other:32]National Society ]story I fancy you will like being the history of a young school mistress and pupil teachers.[[footnote:2]] We have just had a grand wedding here of [[person:1256]our master ]and [[person:1257]Alice Misselbrook ]after being infant mistress nine years.[[footnote:3]] She gives up which is better for him than for us and we are expecting her successor.

Of worthy books, you should read [[otherbook:1062]the Dragon of the North]. It is like a possible Fouqué

your affectionate C M Yonge
