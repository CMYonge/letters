My dear [[person:1025]Alice], The [[person:118]Warden] has asked Charlotte and [[person:1582]Anne] to dine there to be ready for the evening meeting; but at all events they will come to you first, about 10 o\'clock, to go with you to the Cathedral. You would have enjoyed a walk with us last evening in a part of [[other:119]Cranbury] quite unknown to us, where we found some beautiful lady-fern and a dragon-fly surpassing in beauty. And so the dear people did come to us, [[person:1020]Mary], [[person:968]Dora], [[person:81]Emily], and [[person:1024]Annie], and we are only just come from walking to meet [[person:374]Miss Cowing][[footnote:1]] and the rest. Charlotte sends all that is finished of \'[[cmybook:19]Heartsease],\' and has no doubt there will be more by the time you are ready for it; she is numbering the pages with red, in case they get wrong. I do think [[person:1019]Mrs. Moberly] will enjoy little John Martindale, even more this time than before. [[person:796]Miss Keble] is very fond of him.
