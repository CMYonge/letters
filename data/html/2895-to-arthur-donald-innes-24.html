Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes]

I think you must have heard from [[person:121]Mrs Lennard] by this time, as she mentioned, in a letter I had yesterday, having heard from you. I was out all day and could not write.

[[person:820]Miss Hugessen] is ill and cannot do her paper this month but there are to be retrospective questions. I think the same notice will do. I will write the notices but I think I shall have to ask for an additional sheet as so much has to be finished
