Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I send up the two next chapters of [[cmybook:72]St John], in case it should be convenient to show [[person:60]Mr Armitage] the description of the scene with the Robber. Do you mean to have [[other:167]vignettes]? If you do the cup with the serpent should come at the end of the last chapter I send you, and the one I am writing now should have the golden candlestick, I should try for Diana for the first if the Ephesian Diana were not so frightful.

I suppose [[person:1301]Miss Sewell]’s call on you was not accomplished. It was to depend on whether she decided on going to the Paris exhibition

I am sorry she did not manage it, as it would have saved time

Yours sincerely C M Yonge

Thanks for the [[other:192]Pall Mall Gazette], I thought it the most dangerous so I hope the others will be as polite[[footnote:1]]
