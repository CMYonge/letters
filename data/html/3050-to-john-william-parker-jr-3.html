'Dear [[person:1143]Sir], I hope you will receive "[[cmybook:19]Heartsease]” tomorrow, as I shall send it by the South Western early in the day. I shall be glad for it to appear in the same style as the first edition of the [[cmybook:15]Heir of Redclyffe] but I do not think the volumes will be quite so large. You do not say what number you propose to print, I suppose not fewer than 1500 or 2000. I shall be quite ready to correct the proofs. One of the mottoes of the chapters was supplied from [[otherbook:167]Mr F Tennyson\'s poems] which you kindly sent me, and which arrived just as the lines were needed. I am glad to hear that the 4th edition of the Heir of Redclyffe has sold off,[[footnote:1]] I hope the cheap one may meet with equal success. Yours faithfully C. M. Yonge
