'[To [[person:1606]FMY] and CMY]

My dear Friends,

Forgive us for not having remembered to write to you yesterday. Our dear [[person:796]Sister][[footnote:1]] departed like a babe from its mother’s arms with hardly a shudder, seemingly in the midst of a sweet sleep which had lasted a great many hours. God be thanked for her, and may He pour His rich blessings on you and all who love her.

It was at 9.30 yesterday morning; [[person:795]my wife] was poorly, but both she and [[person:797]my brothers wife] seem comfortably rested to-day.

In haste and love, yours always

[[person:798]J. K.]
