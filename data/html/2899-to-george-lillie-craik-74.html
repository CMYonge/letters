Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

Many thanks for your letter and cheque. I am glad to see your writing again, and that the first dreariness of return has been faced.[[footnote:1]]

I am glad the cheap edition is doing so well, it is capitally got up and bound, and I regret nothing but [[cmybook:24]the Daisy Chain] [[other:167]illustrations] and those to [[cmybook:53]the Trial]. Those to the intermediate edition were much better.

I have had some correspondence about a story of the historical kind which I finished in the autumn, but it was thought better to let it wait till [[cmybook:201]Beechcroft at Rockstone] had had its start. Its name is a great difficulty A Seven Years Secret has been thought of but does not really suit and I now think that [[cmybook:205]‘A Reputed Changeling’] or ‘Peregrine the Changeling’ would be best as though it is by no means a child’s story, the whole turns on the hero’s life being influenced by a sort of half belief in his being a changeling imp

It is in the time of the English Revolution

Yours truly C M Yonge
