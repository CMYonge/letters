'Dear Madam, I have sent a good many times to the [[person:1065]publisher] of the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] but he says some of the numbers are out of print, and he hoped to be able to let us have the volume, but still we cannot get it. Can you kindly let us have your own copy? I am, dear Madam, Yours respectfully A. Macmillan

Miss Yonge Elderfield Otterbourne Winchester
