My dear [[person:329]Christabel] Here is the September [[other:89]Barnacle] – the binder has really made it so, though it ought to be the Midsummer one. I suppose that great MS ought to go round with it as I suppose no one would have patience to copy it out in the right size[[footnote:2]] It was very stupid and this thin Barnacle would be much the better for it. Will you put in a note to that effect when you write the orders. I have only the conclusion of [[otherbook:507]Two and Two][[footnote:3]] towards another and I go to Ottery on Monday and to Puslinch on Wednesday next for a month, so the answers should come to me at Puslinch (Yealmpton) if they are not ready before Monday –

In haste yours sincerely C M Yonge
