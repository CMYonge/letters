'Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] I see you are advertising a new edition of the [[cmybook:48]Christian names]. I hope the printing has not gone too far for the corrections to be put in. I have a copy perfectly full of them. The Greek has all manner of wrong accents. The [[other:99]Saturday ]complained of \'the frightful look of the Greek\'[[footnote:2]] I have the vols all corrected and have been longing for a chance to set them right. Then all that about the letters of the alphabet ought to be made away with, for I puzzled it out in ignorance of Grimm’s law, and am ashamed of it now, and there are hundreds of other blunders. So I trust it is not too late to correct them. There is also a mistake in [[cmybook:90]Pioneers and Founders] where I said [[person:2315]Thomas Scott] was a Dissenter which was not true I promised to rectify this when I could but there is nothing so hard as trying to catch an edition for alteration. [[cmybook:126]The Story of the Moors] is nearly ready to send you. I should like however first to see [[otherbook:901]Major Osborns Islam] (Vol 1) It might take a more philosophical view than the authorities I am using Yours truly C M Yonge
