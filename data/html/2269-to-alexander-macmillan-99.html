My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], The mistakes about Walsingham & Sidney had perhaps better be mentioned in the preface to disarm the critics, and that unlucky Amen must be made an erratum it is so ridiculous in its present position.[[footnote:1]]

Would you notify this to [[other:173]Clay], as I do not know the page.

What do you say to the [[other:202]story of Indian Life] I sent you? If you cannot do any thing with it, please write to me before returning it, for I think I shall ask [[person:1433]Mr Warne] to look at it, and it is a pity it should travel 140 miles between Nos 15 and 16[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
