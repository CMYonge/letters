The learned say the Easter moon is right at the place which fixes for all the world. It was not full before noon which is the time they count from. You see if the full moon as it is in each place were reckoned some countries would have it on a different day. I hope [[person:1624]Julian]’s affairs are mending, he is in much better spirits about them. One change is to be parting with the governess and sending [[person:182]Alethea] and [[person:1630]Maurice] to school. Alethea wants the discipline, and it is high time for Maurice who is a very big boy but very slow. Have you heard yet from [[person:1042]Jane Moore]. I think she must have had her good Bishop in her house for [[person:748]Mr Jacob]’s last letter said she has telegraphed to him to come to her at R.
