'My dear [[person:379]Mr Craik], I shall be staying at the Palace at Salisbury[[footnote:1]]  from Monday till Thursday of Easter Week, Could I meet you there and go round the Cathedral with you –Said Cathedral is in a state of restoration from the mischief that [[person:2424]Wyatt] did it and so not in present perfection[[footnote:2]] – I make another visit[[footnote:3]] afterwards and do not come home till Saturday, so unless I can meet you at Salisbury I am afraid I shall miss you, which I should be very sorry to do – Sunshiny east(?) which is at least more becoming than snow storms, and the primroses banks are not at all the worse for it Yours sincerely CM Yonge
