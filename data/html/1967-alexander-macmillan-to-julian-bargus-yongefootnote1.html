My dear [[person:1624]Sir],

Your paper on Bridge Bracing has not come to hand.[[footnote:1]][[person:939] I] am rather hesitant whether [[person:980]Mr Masson] has any article on American affairs for the September [[other:78]number]. If he has not I will ask him to see whether yours wd suit him. You know he is the chief who has authority. [[footnote:2]] I shall be very glad if it does.

In the mean time I tell you that the paper has not reached me. Your letter seemed to imply that you had sent it.
