Sir, I have much pleasure in acknowledging the safe arrival of your thank offering for the [[other:3]Hospital for Sick Children] in Great Ormond Street. I am this morning forwarding it to the Assistant Secretary [[person:1811]Mr S Whitford], 4, Porter Street Leicester Square. I am sure from the particulars I learn from ladies constantly in the habit of visiting there that it is a most valuable and well conducted charity; and that your donation will be well bestowed.

With thanks for giving me so pleasant a commission, as well as for letting me know the cause

Yours faithfully C.M. Yonge
