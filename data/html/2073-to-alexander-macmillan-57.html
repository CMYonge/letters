My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] I enclose the [[other:160]receipt] with many thanks. Your letter followed me hither this morning. I think we shall be in these parts about a fortnight longer - and shall then come to a place about a couple of hours of London - whence I hope to run up for a few hours to town, and I shall then be able to talk over matters with you. I fancy it will be somewhere about the 25th, but our doings are some what uncertain

I hope you had weather as fine as ours. I have scarcely seen a drop of rain since we left home this day four weeks.

Pray do not decide on the [[cmybook:62]Dove]’s illustration without looking at the queer old contemporary books. Ulm from the [[otherbook:421]Nuremberg Chronicle] which I could easily get etched exactly in pen and ink would be explanatory, being that I constructed the topography in the story from it. How intensely interesting the history of [[person:1960]Eyre] the explorer is.[[footnote:1]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
