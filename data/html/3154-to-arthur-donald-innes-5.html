Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes] I have the Unfashionable Concert somewhere it is one of those things that if they cannot be put in at once, get set aside for fresher papers of the charity kind I can’t make any promises about putting it in so I think I had better return it. I hoped to have found it this evening , but a visitor, and twilight prevented me. I have grown more wary at last about taking things that would overcrowd[[footnote:1]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
