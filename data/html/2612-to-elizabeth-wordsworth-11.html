My dear [[person:1566]Elizabeth]

I believe (reluctantly) that you are right and that [[otherbook:890]Ebb & Flow] is too apposite to the present fashion to bear postponement, I think [[person:939]Macmillan] would take such a story as that, and do well by it, I hope to send it off on Monday.[[footnote:1]]

[[person:1462]Gertrude] thanks much for the paper about the works She has sent off a parcel today Yes, [[person:597]M.Guizot] gave me that history of France as it came out and having lately achieved binding it, it becomes a most agreeable possession, and Gertrude is diligently reading it through. The pictures are delightful.[[footnote:1]] Have you read [[otherbook:895]Uarda] – It is very like being in Egypt allowing for a slight tincture of the German scholar. About the sling – if Frank once began with a solid one, I don’t think he would have taken to the awkwardness of a handkerchief – which is rather a thing extemporized for a day or two’s hurt.

What is that fresh zest of life made of - does it come from inside or outside or both. I fancy the outside ingredient must be something to surmount, and in the inward the spirit to try.

Yours affectionately C M Yonge
