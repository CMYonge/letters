My dear [[person:988]Miss Medhurst] I did hope to have put in [[otherbook:835]this sonnet] of yours in time for Ascension day, but I could not manage it, so I can only send you the proof and thank you for your kindness to [[person:1358]Emmeline Spratt], who after her visit to you wrote the happiest letter she had written at all since leaving home[[footnote:1]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
