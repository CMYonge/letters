Dear Madam

[[person:758]Bishop Jenner] tells me that I should apply to you for permission to use two of [[person:1078]Dr Neale]’s Songs of the Trades in a little [[cmybook:143]book of poetry] for children which I am putting together for Marcus Ward The Songs of the Engine Driver and the Silk Thrower are those that I mean, and I may perhaps also wish to have the Ironfounder, as well as the ballad of Aristomenes[[footnote:1]] in the Greek history tales. Hoping that you see no objection to my thus using them, I remain,

yours respectfully C M Yonge
