Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

I think I had better glance over [[cmybook:357]the Shells].

I have begun upon [[cmybook:17]the flowers] but I find there must be a great revolution there, as it is no use in these days to teach the old Linnean system. I can manage by transposition, but it will take some time to set it in order, and I think it had better come in time for the spring, as it starts with the flowers of the months.

I do not think ‘Householders’ need as much retouching, but I only have it in the magazine, and could look it over better in the pages to be printed from.[[footnote:1]]

I have been waiting to hear what the Liverpool lady says to the downfall of her plans - but she has not written.

My present idea is to put [[cmybook:189]the history of the Reign] into the [[other:52]Monthly Packet] and then publish it after wards if there seems an opening, but I have not yet had her answer so that I cannot quite decide.[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge
