My dear [[person:441]Mrs Donkin], As soon as may be I will get the [[otherbook:505]Summer Vacation] sent to the press, but it cannot be before October at soonest that I send it - as all these remarkable events have thrown other M S back. I have a Naval Review just come, to complete the series you began.

By the by, it was a Woolwich cadet’s uniform that [[person:1997]prince Arthur] wore - as the [[person:1520]daughter] of the late [[person:1521]commandant] specially begged me to observe. I wonder whether the Prince will criticise it too.

I was not sure enough where you were to send you the proofs, as there was no time to spend in their wanderings

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
