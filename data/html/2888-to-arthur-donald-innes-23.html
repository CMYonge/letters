Dear [[person:744]Mr Innes]

Miss Robertson’s two papers Home Daughters and Power are quite safe, only there has not been room for them yet.[[footnote:1]] Does she want them back?

I find I cannot get Plataea into the Christmas number, it must come in a regular one. I sent off the bill of fare for the Xmas no yesterday. The first story is [[otherbook:1068]Friday’s Child], which I think would afford the best illustration. The little boy listening to the old sailor-gardener’s stories would I think be best - To have him sitting in the wood with the dead dog would be too sad, or too like last year’s picture

Yours truly C M Yonge
