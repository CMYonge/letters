Dear [[person:2234]Miss Walker] Thank you for writing to tell me of the fate of the letters, I am afraid such has often befallen the like when they descend to a generation too young to care for them and not wishing for the encumbrance. I have a screen worked in that way by my mother, but I think almost all my other possessions of the kind have worn out.

[[person:703]Mr Hobart Hampden] has his eldest sister [[person:702]Sarah] staying with him, and I hope she will make her home with him, as the father died in the winter and the other two sisters are in India[[footnote:1]]

yours sincerely C M Yonge
