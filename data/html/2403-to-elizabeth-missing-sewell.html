'My dear [[person:1301]Miss Sewell], Could you be so kind as first to send on a card the name of anyone to write to at Freshwater about lodgings for a fortnight or so, or if that great Alum bay hotel is thought well of.[[footnote:1]] [[person:474]Mrs Elgie][[footnote:2]] wants a little airing after her winter of illness, and I fancy Bonchurch would be too relaxing for her, but if there are any lodgings anywhere you specially recommend it would be a kindness to mention them, and the sort of rate of price. It is only [[person:478]Mr] and Mrs Elgie, not the children. If you are gone from home, perhaps one of your sisters would be so kind as to set down particulars. Yours sincerely, C M Yonge
