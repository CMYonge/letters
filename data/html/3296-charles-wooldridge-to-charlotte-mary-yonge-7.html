Dear Madam I enclose the deed for [[person:1604]Mrs. Yonge]’s signature in one place only where you will see her name written in pencil. [[footnote:1]] This signature should be made in the presence of a witness who will sign where shewn adding address & occupation, a householder is best, but a servant will do. You must not, of course, be the witness. Please leave the date blank & return me the deed as soon as possible as it has to be stamped &c.

I am yours faithy [[person:2094]Chas Wooldridge]

Miss Yonge 70 Kensington Gardens Square
