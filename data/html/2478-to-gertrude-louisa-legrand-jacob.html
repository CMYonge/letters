My dear [[person:751]Gertrude] Thanks for your chapters.[[footnote:1]] If anything strikes me, I will tell you, but I like all I have read very much. I think [[person:1160]Miss Peard] does know Arcachon, so I am desiring her to write to you, but I do not believe that [[person:1244]Miss Roberts] has ever been on that side of France. She /MR\\ is at the Crescent Hotel, Filey, sadly laid up by a strain of the hip, which she neglected and now has grown to be a bad business. It hindered the party from going to the Dolomites, and now [[person:844]Dr Latham] has forbidden it for fear of cholera.

I have been longing to exchange some word of the piteous load on all our hearts with you and [[person:749]Edith] ever since that great shock.[[footnote:2]] I am glad to know that about [[otherbook:834]Twilight].

Haven\'t you often thought of his having no twilight- the sun gone down while yet day.

I hope all will agree on one Memorial and not fritter up into quantities of little things, everyone making it an opportunity of getting what he wants. I think a Surrey Cathedral & Bishopric would be the grandest, and it would be national[[footnote:3]]

yours affectionately C M Yonge
