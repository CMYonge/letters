Dear [[person:443]Mrs Drew], I am almost certain that the [[cmybook:20]Little Duke] and the [[cmybook:23]Lances of Lynwood] are published in chapter form for school children’s reading and our schoolmistress told us she had seen [[cmybook:10]Kenneth] advertised as abridged for School reading - I know [[person:2211]Arnold] wrote to me for permission and I told him he might use it, if he could arrange with [[person:1142]Parker of Oxford], who published it when I knew nothing of arrangements.[[footnote:1]] [[other:111]Macmillan] has all the rest; I have made some attempts to get them reduced but the Daisy Chain [[cmybook:62]Dove in the Eagle’s Nest] was published at /6 and they will reduce no more unless 50,000 are ordered, so I fear nothing can be done.

The [[other:32]National Society] might make its reward books cheaper - it has a good many historical tales of mine.

Certainly that catalogue of reward books must have been constructed on considered principles. I hope your little girl is arriving at recovery rapidly
