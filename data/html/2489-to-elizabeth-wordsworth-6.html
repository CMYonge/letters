'My dear [[person:1566]Elizabeth] Did you send me [[otherbook:842]Lady Anne]?[[footnote:1]] If you did, thank you very much- you must know I had never seen her, but my dear cousins the Colbornes had her with them in Canada and have told me the story, but never could find the book again - I shall be so glad to send it out to [[person:1042]Mrs Moore] in India - not my own copy I mean but one for her self, and to read to her soldiers wives.[[footnote:2]] What a splendid Aurora we had last night, like the loveliest circle of moonlight with a rosy cloud beyond, and streamers at times You can’t think how often I have been asked where Angela found her song, and if it is Original![[footnote:3]] Your affectionate C M Yonge
