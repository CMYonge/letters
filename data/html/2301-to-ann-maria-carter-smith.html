'My dear [[person:263]Miss Smith], I hope you and the Incurable List are safe together somewhere![[footnote:2]] I had no list of my own so I asked a friend to send you hers, and owing to some blundering of mine I really do not know whether she sent it to Wilden manor or to Charlton. Will you kindly when you have looked it over return it addressed [[person:749]Miss Jacob] Crawley Rectory Winchester Yours sincerely C M Yonge
