'[To [[person:113]Elizabeth Barnett]]

I have been reading [[otherbook:1101]an article] in the [[other:259]National Review], showing how utterly [[person:1972]Carlyle] misquoted Cromwell’s speeches, and absolutely neglected shoals of contemporary papers which would have spoilt his conceptions of his idol. It is curious, but really, poor old Carlyle must have been a good deal of a humbug for all his bluster.
