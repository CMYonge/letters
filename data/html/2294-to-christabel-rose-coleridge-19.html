Have you written to [[person:1467]Miss Arnold], or shall I accepting [[person:300]Miss Clarke] I find [[person:2024]Grace Latham] has not time, so perhaps we had better accept [[person:219]Miss Budd] and [[person:2025]Miss Llewellyn]. Scotland has sunk away from us, so I suppose Wales is coming instead The other perhaps we had better reject on the grounds of not being a round dozen. I think twelve is the right number.
