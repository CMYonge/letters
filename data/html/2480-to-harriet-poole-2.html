My dear [[person:1187]Miss Poole][[footnote:2]] I suspected that you would have to change to Monday. I wonder if the enclosed will make any further change. We shall be very glad to have you here on Sunday, and the carriage with [[person:930]Miss Mackenzie] in it will meet you at Bpstoke at the same time.

I suppose - as you do not say so - [[person:464]Miss Dyson] has heard no more from Heaths Court[[footnote:3]]

yours affectionately C M Yonge
