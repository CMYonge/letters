Dear Miss Yonge We shall deal with the [[cmybook:58]Clever Woman] as we did with the [[cmybook:53]Trial] as you wish it. Will it be safe to begin printing it or will you prefer waiting till we are a little further on. If we cannot publish before the end of November it would be better to wait till February.

We will be very glad to have all the copy for the [[cmybook:51]golden Deeds]. The little extra will not matter. [[person:939]I] only meant or \'thereabouts.\' You will have a proof of the title page very soon. [The rest of this letter illegible]
