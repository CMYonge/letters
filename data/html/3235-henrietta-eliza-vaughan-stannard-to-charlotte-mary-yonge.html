Dear Miss Yonge, I do think it was so kind of you to write to [[person:2132]me] with your own hand; it is an honour that I very highly value. By an accident I was prevented from signing your [[other:225]birthday roll], as I was to have done so at the Writer’s Club but was unavoidably prevented by a business appointment from being there soon enough. But no one would have signed it with greater pleasure and sincerity than I.

I feel very strongly about the birds, especially the ospreys.[[footnote:1]] I convicted and converted a very fashionable lady down here the other day on that point. She said in an airy way ‘Oh, well, I always say it does no good to hear about those things and one must have a proper finish to one’s bonnet.’ I promptly told her that every woman who knew how ospreys were got would brand her as a cruel unwomanly blood thirsty wretch and I made her listen till I could see she was feeling quite sick. I dont think she will ever wear an osprey again.

Believe me with much admiration, Ever yours, John Strange Winter (Henrietta E.V. Stannard)
