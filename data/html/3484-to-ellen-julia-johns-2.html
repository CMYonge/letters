My dear [[person:772]Mrs Johns], I am very glad there is a prospect of our seeing [[person:793]Miss Keary]. Would you or [[person:887]Kate] be so kind as to let us know when she comes, and how long she is likely to be with you, and then we may fix a day for meeting or trying to meet. Next week and the week after too we have friends coming to us just so invalidish that I dare not make engagements till I have seen how they are, and the length of the second visit also, but at any rate, I hope we shall not miss seeing you and Miss Keary

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
