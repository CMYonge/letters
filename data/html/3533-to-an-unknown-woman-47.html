My dear Madam,

I fear you must almost have despaired of receiving the renderings of the names, but we have been so constantly either seeing sights or travelling that this is the first time I have been able to set them down, and now I am afraid they will prove very disappointing, and not too susceptible of illumination - I am afraid names are too often mere adjectives to be very manageable for the purpose. I added the Stem as the only way in which many could be illuminated, unless figures of the Saints of each name were to be introduced[[footnote:2]]

Regretting the long delay Yours faithfully C M Yonge
