[[person:263]Madam], I delayed to thank you for [[otherbook:253]your M S], till I had had time to read it. It is a very well told story, and I shall have great pleasure in inserting it in an early number of the ensuing volume, either in July or August. The only criticism I should make, is that the boys are rather too old, even at that date, for a schoolroom tea, especially Johnnie, if he had already been to Otaheite.

Allow me to add that [[otherbook:31]Lucy and Christian] have been universally liked, and I have been many times asked whether there were any foundation of truth for the story[[footnote:1]]

With many thanks yours sincerely C M Yonge
