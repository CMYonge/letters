Dear Miss Yonge The colonial conscience exists as you see in relation to [[other:172]copyright.] If you will kindly sign the enclosed receipt we will be able to recover for you munificent sum of 11/8d. for the wrong done you by the sale of Yankee reprints in British territory.

[[person:939]I] am not going to America after all, & will, I trust have the pleasure of seeing you at my house when you come to London. Will you kindly give me as long notice as you can. I am going for a holiday, but could regulate it as to time.

Yours ever faithfully Alex Macmillan
