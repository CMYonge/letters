Dear [[person:1264]Miss Routledge]

King Charles will come - if the [[cmybook:213]Cameos] and their author and the [[other:52]Monthly packet] survive so long - in his due season - which cannot well be anticipated.[[footnote:1]] But I am afraid a defence out of the Monthly Packet would not satisfy his enemies. Would it not be better to go to the fountain head? Clarendon’s [[otherbook:35]History of the Rebellion] is not a book hard to get at.

Or Guizot’s [[otherbook:1100]history of the English Rebellion] which is a very fair book, and being the judgment of a statesman cannot be sneered at.

Yours truly C M Yonge
