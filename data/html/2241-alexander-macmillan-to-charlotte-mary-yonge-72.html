Dear Miss Yonge, [[person:939]I] enclose the [[other:160]monthly cheque] with best remembrances.

I hope the [[cmybook:73]Cameos] will get out by & by. The volume proves thinner than I had expected and I am making calculations as to price. This first edition at 4/6 or even 5/- will not yield much. Are you disposed to sell us the copy right?
