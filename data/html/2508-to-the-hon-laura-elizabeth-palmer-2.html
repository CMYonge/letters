My dear [[person:1238]Miss Palmer] I enclose the order. I wrote Hants after Sep upon the paper, so I hope they made it out to the right one It will be a great pleasure to me to come to Blackmoor [erasure] Thank [[person:2299]Lady Selborne] for her kind invitation.[[footnote:2]]

What would suit me best would be to come when I leave Crookham, on the 3d or 4th of September, as then I could take the train at Alton and go home from you instead of back to Crookham Would this be convenient to Lady Selborne?

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
