My dear [[person:184]Henry] [[person:1680]Mr Steward] has written at last. He says two very nice girls [[person:906]Mildred Luckett] and [[person:212]Kate Brown] will be ready at Christmas, so I am writing to him to engage one of them, and she can talk it over with [[person:1681]A. Diddams] who can tell her all about the place.[[footnote:2]]

He also gives me a list of ex pupil teachers who could keep school after the 1st of October till the mistress is ready. They all have homes near at hand and I think we can get one of them.

[[person:783]Amy Bateman] is going to Winchester tomorrow, so I am sending her the names and asking her to enquire.

Your affectionate C M Yonge
