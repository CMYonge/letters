My dear [[person:1046]Mr Moor] I think it will be much better to throw the prizes open to the whole schools [sic]; it is so discouraging and troublesome to be put out of the competition, and everyone has not a garden who has children at school

My [[person:1606]mother] says she meant her prizes instead of the home prize - of course they would chiefly concern the first - not the second - if all the extraparochial people are necessarily in the 3d class, she would extend it to them.

I suppose it will not wait till we see you

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
