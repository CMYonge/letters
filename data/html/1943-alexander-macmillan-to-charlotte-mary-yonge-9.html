Dear Miss Yonge .

What you received is an original drawing - not a proof. [[person:939]I] am glad you like it so far. I want to reproduce it by the photolithographic process which is at once cheap and accurate. The [[person:500]artist] is a very promising young man who is almost self taught & who has done some charming oil[sic].[[footnote:1]] He wanted to try his hand on book[[other:167] illustrations], and this seemed a good chance for him, if it did not please you, no matter, and if it did then we could have something to make this new [[cmybook:20]edition] look lively. Will you kindly send me back the drawing, I shall send him your criticism.

I am dear Miss Yonge Yours very faithfully A. Macmillan
