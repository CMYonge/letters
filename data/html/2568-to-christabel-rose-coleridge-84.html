I should like of all things to have the dear [[otherbook:741]Fernando], but alas! I don’t see a scrap of room for him before Christmas[[footnote:2]] [[otherbook:877]Orina] will last till the summer [[otherbook:876]Our Young ladies] till Christmas, [[cmybook:131]Magnum Bonum] 2 years - (I fear) Then [[person:254]Miss R N Carey] begins [[otherbook:878]a long story] in July, and I can\'t see a chink to put any thing in till Our Young Ladies are done, & I suppose you cannot wait.
