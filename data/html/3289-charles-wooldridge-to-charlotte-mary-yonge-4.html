Dear Madam I have received all the documents from the Insurance Company & also the policy on [[person:1604]Mrs Yonge]’s life. As I presume the £1000 is still due to you from Mrs Yonge shall you wish to have the policy transferred into your name so as to afford you a security for the amount of any interest or premiums you have paid, or how would you like to have this arranged.[[footnote:1]]

I am yours faithy [[person:2094]Chas Wooldridge]

Miss Yonge
