Dear [[person:1254]Madam]

I am exceedingly obliged to you for this kind gift of good old [[person:1146]Mrs Pascoe]’s letters. She was indeed a most amusing and clever correspondent and her letters were always a great pleasure to [[person:1606]my mother] and me. I never saw her, but I heard of her from two old neighbours, [[person:795]Mrs Keble], who used to see her at Penzance and Mr Arthur Johns who was an old neighbour.[[footnote:1]]

Pray thank [[person:1251]Mrs Rogers] much for me

Yours truly C.M. Yonge
