My dear [[person:329]C C] I am glad there are some signs of life or of decay more properly of poor [[person:744]A D I]- ! I do think there are more and more signs that a 6d phoenix would be welcomed.[[footnote:1]]

I find the booksellers make all sorts of excuses for not having got it – The [[other:44]Authors Soc] want to see my agreement before advising me. I could not find it easily before I left home and was too hot for a hunt. [[person:1160]FMP] has written to offer a visit on the 17th or 18th so I have written to her to meet me and come with me on the 18th I have got the MS of [[cmybook:251]Ducks Eggs], I think I can make them much more interesting and longer. We are nice and cool here now, but I think people must suppose that I dispose of all the [[other:113]scholarship] money for the begging letters are endless. [[person:195]Anna Bramston] has photographed all the tableaux before the break up! Poor [[person:1610]George] at Kimberley is tormented as nephew to a lion, and was invited to go to a book dance as ‘[[cmybook:24]The Daisy Chain]’- pretty well for a great black police officer. So you may tell [[person:353]Walter] to prepare to be invited out as Jack a Lantern. [[person:1613]Helen] returns remembrances. I have had a charming letter from the old [[person:1637]Mignonette] about Maximilian and the Martinswand- and an Italian palace – quite good enough for an article. [[footnote:2]] The local demon [[person:1688]Mr ffinch] & co are a great success, he reads the lessons so beautifully that I am quite sorry to miss a fortnight of him. He is warden of a college for ‘decayed gentlewomen’ I should think you would have heard of it.

[[person:1630]Maurice] and [[person:1629]Maude] have the funniest little cottage at Fleet, in the middle of purple heath

your affectionate C M Yonge

Poor Lanty!
