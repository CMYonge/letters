My dear [[person:1124]Mr Palgrave]

You will think there is no end to the books I am doing, but the [[other:32]National Society] has set me on making [[cmybook:150]a historical Reader] for schools interspersed - after Reader fashion with poems. May I have an extract from your verses about Charles’s flight. They are so much easier than Wordsworth’s sonnet- and may I borrow one or two more from that [[otherbook:929]same book]?[[footnote:1]] The Montfort one, I think but I am not sure of any other.[[footnote:2]]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
