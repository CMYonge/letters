My dear [[person:787]Mrs Joyce],

I am sorry to say I must ask you to put off your kind visit till next week.

My [[person:1604]sister in law]’s brother, [[person:1464]Charles Walter], has been killed in Alderney by a fall from his dog cart.[[footnote:2]] He is to be buried on Tuesday, beside his [[person:1461]father ]at Winchester, and the relations will be in and out here to see the poor [[person:1462]invalid sister] so that I cannot venture to have anyone here on my own account.

Next week will be free, except on what ever day they may fix for electing the High school mistress Then I shall be very glad to see you

yours very sincerely C M Yonge
