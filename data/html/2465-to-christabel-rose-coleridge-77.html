My dear [[person:329]Christabel] Here is the pay for [[otherbook:818]Minne’s Knight], with many thanks.[[footnote:2]] I conclude you are at home. You will see proposals for a monstrous Goose affair in the next packet, but I shall get help in the selection of the essays.[[footnote:3]] I hope you will do the Snow and Sun next year

Who asks our question next? I should think [[otherbook:829]Hanbury Mills] must be a great success, every body seems to like it who has read it I am much afraid the Awdry’s departure will be the ruin of our [[other:240]lectures], there seems to be no one to take the same pains about them[[footnote:4]]

your affectionate C M Yonge
