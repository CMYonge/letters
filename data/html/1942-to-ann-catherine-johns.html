My dear [[person:887]Kittiwake] I am glad you are meditating a flight to us, but will you let it be on Monday the 14th this day week.[[footnote:1]] I want the skies to clear up a little, as I hope you will allow time enough for a walk to see our daffodil copse of which we are rather proud, and which will gain by the week’s delay.

Do not be deceived into turning into the wrong house for my [[person:1624]brother]’s comes before ours and looks more imposing, but make it clear that we are [[person:1606]Mrs] and not Mr Yonge. Our kind regards to [[person:772]Mrs Johns], and thanks to her for bringing you

Your faithful [[other:104]Mother Goose]
