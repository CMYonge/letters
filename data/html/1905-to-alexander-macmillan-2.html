[[person:939]Sir],

[[person:531]Mr Furnival] has forwarded to me your proposal of dealing with me for my tale of the [[cmybook:58]Clever Woman of the Family] as a book - I should be obliged if you would let me know your terms for publishing on commission; and like wise if in case of such an arrangement being practicable, whether you would take charge of my works that have been hitherto in [[person:1144]Mr Parker]’s hands - All have been published by commission, excepting the two first, the [[cmybook:15]Heir of Redclyffe] and [[cmybook:19]Heartsease] which were on the half profit system, and respecting which it would be necessary to make an arrangement with Mr Parker -

The last years half profits on the Heir of Redclyffe were £28 .. 15 - on Heartsease £9..13 -

I mention this to facilitate your estimate of the worth of the half profits in case you should wish to purchase them

Your obt servt

C. M. Yonge
