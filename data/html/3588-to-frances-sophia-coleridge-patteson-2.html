My dear [[person:1151]Fanny ]

[[person:1297]Mrs Selwyn] comes on Thursday, the [[person:1296]Bishop] on Saturday.[[footnote:1]] It will be a very thing if you do go down to enliven Mrs Selwyn on Friday for it has so turned out that this week is the only one when I can go down to see my poor dear old [[person:351]May], so I am going down there from Tuesday till Friday evening or Saturday morning I am not sure which. I am sorry to miss you but I dare say you will be about again and poor May wants me. [[person:327]Alice] will be there the last day I think.

If you do come down on Friday, and it is not a bad day with [[person:1462]Gertrude], she will be delighted to see you for a little while - the best time between 3 and 4 or 5

Your affte cousin C M Yonge
