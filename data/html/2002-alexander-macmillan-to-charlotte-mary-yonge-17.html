My dear Miss Yonge [[person:939]I] have given our printer orders to go on with the \'[[cmybook:58]Clever Woman]\'. I hope you will receive proofs at once & have a rapid supply. It will make some such work as \'[[cmybook:53]The Trial],\' as you wish.

I have corresponded with a friend of yours about \'[[other:69]Events of the Month].\'[[footnote:1]] I think the idea admirable the want is a crying one. But it should be [illegible] and well carried out. I hardly see my way to the right plan.

I have several times wished there was a [[otherbook:32]Don Quixote] in our Series. I see you have been telling the story briefly, and you recommend the one in Unwin’s Englishman’s Library. Is it very well done? I almost wish you would do it some day. But we should not lose sight of the more important book

ever dear Miss Yonge Yours faithfully A. Macmillan
