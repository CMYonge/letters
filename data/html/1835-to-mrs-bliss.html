I did not think when last I saw you that the accompanying book would have come so soon, so I said nothing about it, but if you would be so kind as to take charge of it, I should be very much obliged as it is a parcel that will not well travel by post.[[footnote:1]] The letters I think I shall have to send by post

yours sincerely
