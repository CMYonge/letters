Dear [[person:2094]Mr Wooldridge] I am afraid I am very ignorant about the term of the loan of the £1000. I cannot say I ever expect [[person:1604]Mrs Yonge] to pay it back to me. She does not even know that I have paid it off.

If I discontinue the payment for insuring her life, will the future payment of £1000 on her death lapse.

Or if I continue it, or my heirs (for I can hardly expect to survive her) will it be paid on her death to my heirs?

Also, to whom for the future shall I pay the fire insurance 1st for the London houses 2nd for those at Otterbourne?

Apologising for all the trouble I am giving Yours truly C M Yonge
