My dear [[person:28]Mary]

I don’t think I would venture. I think you would probably be left in the lurch in some way or other and the name is not known enough to inspire any confidence.[[footnote:2]]

I think you might find some one who would take the risk which would be much better for you. I am making my way home from Devonshire where I have been first with [[person:1626]Mary Yonge] and then with [[person:351]Mary Coleridge], and I hope to get back on Saturday.

I wish you had said how [[person:1588]Lottie] is

Your affectionate cousin C M Yonge
