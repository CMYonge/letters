Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik] I cannot at all tell whether this is the right length, as the only ‘Golden Treasuries’ I have seen are my own and I do not think they have introductions.[[footnote:1]]

I can easily add biographical details if you wish it to be longer - but I like to write to my own sense of the needs of the subject and to add or diminish afterwards as required.

Do you know whether [[person:1240]Mrs Ritchie] is abroad. I was moved to write to her by sympathy with her [[otherbook:688]preface] to Miss Mitford, and I have never had an answer

Yours truly C M Yonge
