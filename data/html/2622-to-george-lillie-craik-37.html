Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

I send 4 chapters of the [[cmybook:126]Story of the Moors] I will send some more as soon as I have touched it up. I think you may reckon on 30 chapters - 22 are written and bring me to Peter the cruel, but the actual siege of Granada will take up a good deal of room

About [[cmybook:353]the little French history], I went over and added to the sheets as far as I had them but I had not kept the duplicates of all. I wrote to ask for the next, but no notice was ever taken of my request and I concluded that the work was a failure, like the other.[[footnote:2]] I ought to have kept the duplicates, but one sometimes feels half smothered in old proofs.

I send the two corrections, I am not quite sure whether that for [[cmybook:102]Bp Patteson’s life] should go in Vol II p 388 - just after Captain Markham’s visit or as a sort of note - at the end of all.

I incline to the first, not liking to spoil the present conclusion

Yours truly C M Yonge

Of course I shall be glad to finish up the French primer
