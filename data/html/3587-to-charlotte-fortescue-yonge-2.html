My dear [[person:1588]Lottie]

Many thanks for the Water Soldier who came in excellent order.[[footnote:2]] Fancy [[person:1583]Arthur Yonge] being like [[person:1624]Julian]. He was in a very different style in his younger days being very dark and always carrying his chin rather in the air but I think his beard must have made a difference and I know there always is a tendency in us to be more like the Duke Yonges than anyone else. My [[person:1606]mother] and [[person:1591]Aunt Duke] were alike though no relations. Did you see Arthur’s very pretty [[person:1584]daughter] who is living with the Masons to go to the [[other:51]High School ]at Winchester[[footnote:3]] The [[person:1019]Moberlys] are quite uncertain what will become of them, as they have not yet been able to find a house. We hoped to have the [[person:182]Bowleses] here in August, but they cannot get their duty done, and have had to put it off. [[person:388]Joan] is still with them. [[person:130]Mrs Beck] is staying here till she goes to the Christening of her poor little grandson, who happily is a fine prosperous child[[footnote:4]]

your affectionate cousin C M Yonge
