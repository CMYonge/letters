My dear [[person:663]Ellie] I am delighted to hear of the Medallion![[footnote:1]]

Have you seen Sir Herbert Maxwell’s [[otherbook:738]book of the months]-? He disbelieved the rod and someone ought to write to him. He watched [[person:2189]Mullins] - and fancied he had discovered the places before. But [[person:2188]Lady Crawley], the mother of [[person:557]Mrs William Gibbs], who had the power only in that generation of the family laughed at it as imagination, and I believe Mrs Gibbs inherited it, but never spoke of it. [[person:1033]Mrs Monroe] had Mullins to find water at Colden Common and he did. All the servants tried under his tuition but only one maid succeeded.

I am doing our Birds with anecdotes if possible to add to the book.

[[person:1613]Helen] has a copy of [illegible] marked by her [[person:1624]father] and can complete the set. There really is much to say of them.

Your affectionate C M Yonge
