My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], [[cmybook:58]The Clever Woman of the Family] as far as she goes will be sent you in a day or two from a friend who had had her to read.

The [[other:167]illustrations] are decidedly not successful and I should not wish to perpetuate them. They are not half so good as what [[person:793]Miss Keary]’s young [[person:887]cousin] does.[[footnote:1]]

There is about a fourth more of the story to come.

I have nearly finished the [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds], but alas the Lotty story has eluded me twice again. However I have quite enough for the pages.

Yours very truly C. M. Yonge
