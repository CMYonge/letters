Dear [[person:571]Miss Gordon Cumming]

I had an intimation from the [[person:1344]publisher] that he is going to change printers, and so that I must finish up all there is in type before August. This is keeping you, as well as many other papers back, but I hope to be able to get them in then.[[footnote:1]]

Something unexpected always happens when one hopes one has comfortable space [[otherbook:969]Miss Sewell’s letters] went on two numbers more than I reckoned on.

I have just got [[otherbook:970]Granite Crags]

yours truly C M Yonge
