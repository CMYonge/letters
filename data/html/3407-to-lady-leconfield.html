'Dear [[person:1664]Lady Leconfield] As [[person:329]Christabel Coleridge] is here, I shall ask her to put in her autograph She is the remnant of my original [[other:104]Goslings] worked on as a [[other:116]Spider] and now edits the [[other:52]Packet], anxious to raise the tone, and bear it through the bad times for periodicals If you have read [[otherbook:205]Waynflete], the [[otherbook:206]Main Chance] and [[otherbook:207]The Tender Mercies], you will think her autograph worth having. She is the granddaughter of [[person:1667]S T Coleridge] yours sincerely C M Yonge
