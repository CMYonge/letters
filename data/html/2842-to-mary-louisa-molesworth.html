Dear [[person:1028]Mrs Molesworth]

I think [[otherbook:998]Felix ]can go into the December number as my [[cmybook:185]Ulysses] finishes in November, and there will be the space it leaves, before another begins[[footnote:1]] I have tried to keep down the serials this year to leave more room for selfcontained articles but it is very difficult.

I will do my best to notice your books if I see them, but I cannot always mention a book as a matter of course because I am very anxious to keep to notices in the [[other:52]Packet] of books for a definite purpose. Of all your books I liked best one of the [[otherbook:1016]Summer stories], but I have been witheld [sic] from giving it to children by the eeriness of the Ghost, and I must own myself to be not very fond of the dreamy semi fairy tale nor of [[person:2366]Walter Crane]’s drawings[[footnote:2]]

But I do like [[otherbook:1017]a charge fulfilled] - and also the live [[otherbook:1018]children lost in Paris] and the gypsy pair. But do you think children like phonetic spellings of their lispings? It seems to me to make their reading more difficult

Pray forgive me Yours sincerely C M Yonge
