My dear [[person:1184]John]

Thank you for your curious extract about names. I have one this morning from an Italian newspaper sent by [[person:296]Mrs Church], which beats everything. I translate it for your behalf- ‘The celebrated English Authoress of the [[cmybook:15]Era di Ratcliffe] is dead. Her name was Jong but in recognition of her talents, the Queen Victoria made her a Viscountess. She married the English ambassador at Constantinople but has continued until lately to write most beautiful romances’[[footnote:1]] I hope it will make [[person:1180]Augusta laugh], I am so sorry she is so entirely laid up.[[footnote:2]] I think we may be well satisfied with our new [[person:140]Archbishop] who is a real man of power and goodness - but Cornwall will be left lamenting.[[footnote:3]] I shall send [[person:1181]Beatrice]’s big card by the same post as this according to the [[other:75]Guardian]’s piteous appeal to spare the poor postmen on Christmas day. There is a smaller one inside with moss, which seems to me a wonderful specimen. Two or three packets of those mossy ones were sent me for notice in the [[other:52]Monthly Packet]

[[person:1624]Julian] is pretty well and as usual painting china

your affectionate cousin C M Yonge
