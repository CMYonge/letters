My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], I think I ought to refer to you a request I have had from Dr and Mrs Vaughan[[footnote:1]] for leave to put two of the stories in [[cmybook:51]Golden Deeds] i.e. \'The last fight in the Colyseum\', and the faithful slaves of Haïti into a [[otherbook:392]]book of readings] for National Schools acknowledging where they come from, but I conclude that you have no objection. When there is a reprint, I have another faithful slave to put in who maintained his mistress by hair dressing in New York.

Thank you for the [[cmybook:58]Clever Woman]’s arrival. She looks very well and attractive, but I am prepared for all the critics doubting her reformation

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
