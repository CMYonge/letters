Dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan], Would you be so kind as to send an order for me for Sir F. Palgraves [[otherbook:479]History of Normandy and England]. I cannot get my [[cmybook:73]Cameo’s] [sic] correct without it, and [[person:1015]Dr Moberly]’s copy on which I reckoned proves to be packed up for leaving Winchester so as to be unobtainable. I can do nothing till I get it, and though I could order it from the [[other:29]London Library], one is kept often very long waiting for that kind of book.

If I knew who was the publisher I would write direct, but I cannot remember.

Pray tell [[person:378]Mrs Craik] when you see her that [[person:423]Mme de Witt] writes to me that [[person:597]M Guizot] considers her \'[[otherbook:481]Two Marriages]\' \'comme un chef d’ouvre\' [sic]

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
