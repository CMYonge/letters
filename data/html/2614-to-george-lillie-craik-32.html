Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

I have got into a puzzle how to manage with the further [[cmybook:129]Scripture readings]. Clay has printed them so slowly that I have only gone on with them when the arrival of proofs has warned me that copy would be wanted. And now I find that the volume has come to its regular size - about 250 pp in the middle of the Acts - i e when St Paul is at Ephesus on his 3d journey, and the 1st Epistle to the Corinthians has just been worked in, in parts of course

What shall I do. I should think it would take about 100pp to finish up in due proportion to the rest, and I suppose we must make the volume take them in. In truth the 3d volume ought to have gone to the end of the Old Testament.

Will [[cmybook:126]‘Story and History of the Moors and Christians’] in Spain do for the new book -? Is there any objection to making large extracts from Lockhart’s [[otherbook:897]Spanish ballads], or would it be safer to /ask\\ consult [[person:2312]Mr Murray]’s consent.

I translate for myself when I have the original, but I have not in many cases.

Yours truly C M Yonge
