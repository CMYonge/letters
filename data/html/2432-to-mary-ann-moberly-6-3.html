'[[To [[person:1019]Mary Ann Moberly]]

There is a full detail of all that is known in a letter from [[person:1981]Mr. Brooke] in the new number of [[other:80]Mission Life] . . . both bring out more of the pain and grief than the first, which rests on one like a vision of the crystal sea and the palm.[[footnote:1]] But the sweet smile bears one on through it all. I go to Lichfield on Monday; [[person:1297]Mrs. Selwyn] says that [[person:1295]her Bishop] is overflowing with recollections of the early days of the mission. . . . Some islands have been quite depopulated by those wretches. I believe Satan always does stir up something dreadful when his kingdom is being taken from him, but when he can only make martyrs it is well. . .
