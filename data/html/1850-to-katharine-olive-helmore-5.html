My dear [[person:675]Cobweb],

I am glad to tell you that no one has passed you, though [[person:1168]Firefly] is your equal, in the number of votes, and I therefore should like to know which of [[other:104]Mother Goose]’s works you would like best to have- only do not expect it immediately as I should like to have it bound for you.

Thank you for letting me see the photograph I am glad you have got it as it is much better than the only one I have now to give away The broken negative was not quite so good as this.

[[person:81]Humble Bee], [[person:538]Irene] and Stormy Petrel[[footnote:1]] are the three best this time

your affectionate

Mother Goose
