Dear Madam,

As you kindly consent to look at the tale of child life in Italy that I mentioned to you, I now send you the three first chapters (it has twelve in all) of [[otherbook:917]\'The Angel of Viareggio\']. I hope the thin paper will not inconvenience you, but I shall not repeat the experiment & the rest of the MS. shall be sent to you on foolscap.

I enclose a post card & stamps & trusting the story may seem likely to suit your pages believe me I remain

Most truly yours [[person:1444]Linda Villari]

Miss Yonge
