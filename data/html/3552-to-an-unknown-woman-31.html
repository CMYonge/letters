My dear Madam That poem on the Sabbath Bells was given me by Lady [[person:679]Lucy Herbert], [[person:2076] Lord Powis]’s sister, at the time when she was collecting subscriptions for [[other:103]bells for Auckland] Cathedral New Zealand. She sent it to me undoubtingly to be printed with an appeal we were then making. She has since married and I cannot remember her present name - but perhaps you know her, and at any rate her sister, Lady [[person:680]Harriet Herbert] would, I am sure, be glad to tell you how the verses came into their family[[footnote:1]]

yours truly C M Yonge
