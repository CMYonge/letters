Miss Yonge is glad to be able to inform [[person:718]Mr Hoskins] that the 1st volume of the [[cmybook:119]Beginnings of Church history] is nearly printed and will soon be out. A large number of orders for it is the only way of cheapening it. Miss Yonge fears the Societies would not accept the book without omissions to which she could not consent.[[footnote:1]]
