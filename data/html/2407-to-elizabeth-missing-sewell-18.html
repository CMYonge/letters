My dear [[person:1301]Miss Sewell], Will Friday the 21st suit you for my coming for a conference? If you think there is any thing for me to bring let me know before Wednesday. I am going, I believe, to New College on the 31st, so that I can take anything for you.[[footnote:2]]

I don’t like to give up the French spelling of the names, I don’t think Count of is a parallel, since that is translated, but I like calling people as they were christened, and it is convenient as a rule in shewing each man’s nationality without specifying it.

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
