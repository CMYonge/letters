My dear Miss Yonge Your scheme as decided by [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] and yourself seems to me very admirable.[[footnote:1]] The one question is the compactness and vivid unity, which will be hard rather in an [illegible] such as you propose. But with artists like yourself [and] Miss Sewell there should be no difficulty[.] the thing ought to be very interesting indeed.

[[person:939]I ]have read all the [[cmybook:61]Prince and the Page] down to the present month. I think it delightful, and hope it will make a very nice Christmas book. But I confess I do not feel quite clear about the [[other:167]illustration].[[footnote:2]] At a first glance it does not quite please me. The Architecture in any case should be more subordinated, but my first impression of the figures is rather that they are weak. I will write more about it tomorrow.

ever most truly yours A. Macmillan
