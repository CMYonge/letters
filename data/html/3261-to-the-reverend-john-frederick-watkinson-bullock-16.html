Dear [[person:221]Mr Bullock], I waited to answer till I saw whether an offer of a story I had made was accepted in which case I should have had no time, but I find I have and can manage the two lives[.] I have Hook’s[[otherbook:695] Archbishops], and will make use of your materials gladly. I once had [[otherbook:696]Frances Havergal] but gave her away so I shall be glad of the loan

I think [[person:1275]Charles Simeon] ought to come in.[[footnote:1]] He did a great work and was far more of a Churchman than ‘Simeonites’

Yours truly C M Yonge
