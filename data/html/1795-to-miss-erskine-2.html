Dear [[person:489]Miss Erskine],

Many thanks for so kindly enquiring to satisfy the Salisbury correspondent. I will wait till this day week to answer him; in case you should have more particulars for me, I wonder whether he wants to make any use of the story, which should I am sure be widely known, I have no longer the address of Miss [[person:1822]Blanch Shelley] who sent it to me, and with whom it was a tradition from an aunt. I should think she knew no more, as she gave no names when I wrote to her for corroboration before inserting it, and she only knew half the story. I am glad to hear such good accounts of [[person:462]Mrs] and [[person:464]Miss Dyson], I hoped to have seen them again this summer, but it seems as if it would not be till they go to Testwood.[[footnote:1]] [[person:351]Mary Coleridge] seemed fairly well, and very animated and bright when I spent an evening with her when she was in London

Yours very truly

C M Yonge
