Dear [[person:379]Mr Craik]

The printer can judge better than I but I do not think there is enough of [[cmybook:185]the Quest of Ulysses] to make more than such a volume as [[cmybook:20]the Little Duke], so I think it had better belong to that Series.

Thank you for Lord Albemarles’ [[otherbook:1008]memoirs]. I was waiting to do so till I saw what [[other:137]Mudie] sent me, and his box is either delayed or lost, as I have just discovered.

I will not close this letter till I hear the result of the enquiry I have sent to make at the Parcels’ Office. If I do not get the books today, I think I must ask you to let me have the [[otherbook:1009]Queens Journals] the [[otherbook:1002]Greville Memoirs] [[otherbook:1010]Princess Alice], and [[otherbook:1011]Lady Blomfield], in all of which I know there are characteristic bits.[[footnote:1]] I have just down the year of revolutions, but I cannot finish off the beginning without more authorities.

The [[otherbook:1013]Annals] are very useful. Unluckily this copy has Sheet M twice over, and no sheet N but I do not think there is anything very important in the missing one[[footnote:2]]

Yours truly C M Yonge

Perhaps I ought to see Madame Bunsen’s [[otherbook:1012]memoirs]

No books come - so if you would kindly get them for me
