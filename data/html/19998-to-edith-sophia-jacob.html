'My dear [[person:749]Edith] Would this be the kind of thing?[[footnote:1]]  I suspect it is too long.  It is a beginning I once made and you may as well look at it.  I also send a paper on the study of the Bible, which an American Sunday School Society once made me write and which might serve you some day perhaps. You see this is a mere beginning, hardly getting beyond the Acts.  [[person:199]Canon Bright] wanted me to do a Church history, and I got so far and then quailed - wanting the lash of a serial to keep me up to it. We seem to be getting on well under the new dynasty, but it has been a strange thing that in one spring the school should lose Vicar, master and pupil teacher[[footnote:2]] your affectionate CMYonge I was going to cut out the article but on second thoughts I send the whole paper as there are curious things in it
