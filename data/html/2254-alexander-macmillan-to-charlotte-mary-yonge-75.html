Dear Miss Yonge, [[person:939]I] was from home when your note about Index & [[person:1606]Miss Sewell]’s suggestion of a chronological table came to hand.[[footnote:1]] But I have not been neglecting the suggestion. We are having a set of proofs made & shall put them into the hands of a competent person and submit the proofs to you.

With regard to announcing volume 3 [Next few lines illegible] the printers want something to do [illegible] and long and we have set them to work on the [[cmybook:69]Chaplet of Pearls]. You need not worry youself with this connection. Your story will end I suppose about October & we should publish about the same time.[[footnote:2]]

I was sorry to miss you when you were in town.

Yours very faithfully A. Macmillan
