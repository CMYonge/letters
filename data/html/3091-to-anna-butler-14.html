My dear [[person:237]Miss Butler] I enclose with the [[other:52]Packet]’s warm thanks the pay for [[otherbook:248]Likes & Dislikes]. I am so glad to think of the continuation for I think the notion of setting Emily to tame young ladies running to seed an excellent one.

[[person:1379]Miss Sturges Bourne] has just been conducting a sick cousin to Wiesbaden, and thinking with much diversion of Helen. She was near going to Marienbad itself which would have been amusing. I hope [[person:113]Lizzie Barnett] has been gaining all sorts of strength at St Leonards.

yours sincerely C M Yonge
