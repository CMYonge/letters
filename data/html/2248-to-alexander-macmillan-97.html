My dear [[person:939]Mr Macmillan] Thanks for the [[other:160]cheque] and for the proposal about the [[cmybook:71]Historical selections]. I am sending it on to [[person:1301]Miss Sewell] to see whether she consents, it is what I myself should prefer, thinking joint accounts would be troublesome. The [[cmybook:73]Cameo] looks exceedingly well and gives an uncommon appearance.[[footnote:1]] I should like a copy to be sent to the [[person:709]Dean of Chichester] and one to [[person:423]Madame de Witt]. To other friends I think it will not be necessary to send one.

I have had a MS lately to read by a [[person:2008]Miss Eyre] who wishes me to write to you about it. It is called Gabrielle and her Guardians[[footnote:2]] and strikes me as having a good deal of thought and promise, though there is a good deal of youth too. The hero is rather remarkably imagined, and the story catches the attention I found on reading it aloud

Yours sincerely C M Yonge
