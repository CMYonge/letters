Dear [[person:221]Mr Bullock], I will try to write Abp Sancroft[[footnote:1]], and [[person:929]Bp Mackenzie] but have you thought of asking [[person:82]Miss Awdry], Notton, Chippenham. She is the author of [[person:687]‘An Elder Sister’] and would do [[person:930]Anne Mackenzie]’s life excellently. She could also do well [[person:1952]Henrietta Robertson], a grand missionary worker.

And have you thought of [[person:1949]Mrs Macdougal] who sacrificed so much-? Miss Awdry could well do all these. She edited the [[other:227]Gospel Missionary] till lately when it was decided that the Editor must live in London.

I hope you will take [[person:2091]Miss Havergal]. You know there are two hymns of hers in [[otherbook:686]A and M] - and the way she took her poetical and musical powers as gifts to be used always struck me as most beautiful.

I see what you mean about [[person:1294]Miss Sellon], as the work and victory she effected, but after much enthusiasm about her, I own I was much disappointed (as were others) when she came to Hursley to see her [[other:46]Sisters] off to Honolulu. Being such an invalid herself, she did not know what was to be expected of her Sisters. However she fought the battle. I see [[other:228]Lincoln diocesan mag] has a life of [[person:244]Dean Butler]. Could you get leave to use that?

Yours truly C M Yonge
