My dear [[person:237]Miss Butler] I was going to return this long ago, but I wanted to hear from [[person:1065]Mr Mozley] whether he could conveniently print the remaining chapters to the end, and he has vouchsafed me no answer, so I mean to wait long enough to give him time to set his types free of the forthcoming number, and then send the whole with a request to have it done at once. I had been making a great dash at finishing [[otherbook:254]Long long ago] in the present number and for that reason had been obliged to put off the [[otherbook:248]Likes], cruel as it was to leave them all tumbled out in the road; but I thought you preferred being put off to being minced fine. [[footnote:1]]

You will see that I could not have shortened the Long long ago without leaving it in a state alarming to the weak minds of the Mamma’s[[footnote:2]]

yours sincerely C M Yonge
